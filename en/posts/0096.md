Rent a manual motorbike by the day when your plans are short, uncertain or built around one or two trips, and by the month when you will use it most days and your schedule is settled. A weekly package sits between the two. Nguyen Ha's published reference rates for manual and semi-automatic bikes are VND 150,000–200,000 per day, VND 700,000–800,000 per week and VND 1,000,000–1,200,000 per month, and the packages are priced on their own, not as a daily rate multiplied by days. The right choice depends less on the cheapest number than on how you will actually use the bike. This guide sets out the use cases for each, what the published terms say about extras such as delivery and late return, what changes about looking after a manual bike over a month and four example riders. Rates are reference ranges as of October 2026, and the final price is confirmed on the actual bike before you sign.

## The published reference rates

These are the reference figures for the manual group, which covers the Honda Wave, Yamaha Sirius and Honda Blade. The bike you actually receive is confirmed at booking.

| Period | Reference range |
|---|---|
| One day (24 hours) | VND 150,000–200,000 |
| One week | VND 700,000–800,000 |
| One month | VND 1,000,000–1,200,000 |

Three points from the shop's published terms shape every comparison.

**A day is 24 hours.** Shorter use is charged as a full day, so a three-hour errand costs the same as a full day on the road.

**Packages are priced separately.** A week or a month is not a daily rate multiplied up. Even so, a quick check shows why the longer packages exist: seven days at the daily rate adds up to more than the weekly range, and thirty days at the daily rate adds up to several times the monthly range.

**Early return does not refund unused rent.** If you take a monthly package and leave after three weeks, the remaining time is not credited back. That is the main risk of committing to a long period you may not use.

## When a daily rental is the better fit

A daily rental suits plans that are short or that might change.

**Sightseeing for a few days.** If you will ride on two or three days of a longer stay and walk or take taxis the rest, paying by the day keeps the cost tied to the days you ride. Our guide to [riding in the Old Quarter](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/riding-in-hanoi-old-quarter/) explains the traffic you will meet.

**A single day trip.** A day to Ninh Binh, Ba Vi or Tam Dao needs the bike for one full day and nothing more. Our guides to [Hanoi to Ninh Binh by motorbike](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/hanoi-to-ninh-binh-by-motorbike/) and [Hanoi to Ba Vi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/hanoi-to-ba-vi-by-motorbike/) give the distances and what to plan.

**A trial day.** If you have never ridden a gear bike in Hanoi, a day lets you find out whether you enjoy it before you commit to anything longer. Our guide to [whether a new rider can learn semi-automatic shifting in Hanoi traffic](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/can-a-new-rider-learn-semi-automatic-shifting-in-hanoi-traffic/) explains how to approach it.

**Uncertain dates.** If your onward travel could change, a short period lets you extend rather than commit. Ask the shop whether an extension is possible and at what price, and have it noted in writing.

**One-off needs.** A day for an event, a visit or an errand, where the bike is not part of your routine.

On a daily rental you also receive the bike with a handover check you can do thoroughly, and you return it soon, so the wear and upkeep questions that matter on a monthly rental hardly arise. Our [10-minute inspection checklist](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/rental-scooter-inspection-checklist/) is enough for most day rentals.

## When a monthly rental is the better fit

A monthly rental suits steady, repeated use.

**A commute.** If you ride to work or study most days, a monthly package is built for that. The bike sits at your door, you learn its quirks and you are not renegotiating every few days.

**A stay of several weeks with local errands.** Interns, students, remote workers and longer-stay visitors often use a bike for shopping, cafes and short trips across the city.

**Wanting delivery.** Delivery is offered only with weekly or monthly agreements, within 10 km of Hoan Kiem Lake, at VND 50,000 per trip or VND 100,000 for both ways. Beyond 10 km it is agreed in advance, and there is no airport service. A daily rental does not include this option.

**Wanting the same bike each day.** Over a month you learn how this particular bike feels, which helps a gear bike where timing and the throttle matter. Our guide to [how to avoid lurching when changing gears](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-avoid-lurching-when-changing-gears/) shows how smooth habits develop with practice.

**A settled schedule.** If you know you will be in Hanoi and will ride for the whole period, the monthly package means you are not paying the daily rate for days you will certainly use.

For a longer discussion of the contract questions, see our guide to [weekly and monthly rental in Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/weekly-monthly-motorbike-rental-hanoi/).

## The grey zone between a week and a month

Many stays fall between the cases above: ten days, three weeks, five weeks. There is no single answer, but the logic is straightforward.

- **Count only days you are certain to use.** The weekly and monthly packages are paid in full, and unused rent is not refunded after an early return.
- **Consider two weeks in a row.** Two weekly packages add up to more than the monthly range, but they leave you free to stop after week one. Ask the shop how a second week would be priced.
- **Ask about extending a monthly package.** If you might stay beyond the month, ask whether the new period would start at the existing return deadline and what rate would apply.
- **Do not guess.** Ask the shop for a quote on your exact dates and compare it with the alternatives.

## What changes about looking after a manual bike over a month

A manual bike is simple, but a month of daily use is not a day trip. A few questions matter more as the days add up.

**Chain care.** Honda's Wave RSX FI manual gives a chain lubrication and check interval of every 500 km, and sooner if you ride on poor roads, fast or with repeated hard acceleration. A commuter covering about 20 km a day reaches 500 km in roughly 25 days, so a month rental will cross that line. Ask who looks after the chain, and read our guides to [recognising chain noise](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/recognising-chain-noise-on-a-honda-wave-or-yamaha-sirius/) and [checking chain tension before a longer ride](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/checking-chain-tension-before-a-longer-ride/).

**Brakes.** Brake shoes wear with use, and Honda notes that free play increases as they wear. Re-check how the pedal and lever feel every week or so. Our guide to [checking the rear brake on a Honda Wave](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/checking-the-rear-brake-on-a-honda-wave/) explains what to look for.

**Tyres.** Pressure drifts down over weeks. Honda's advice is to check with a gauge at least once a month. Our guide to [reading a Honda Wave tyre size and pressure label](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-read-a-honda-wave-tyre-size-and-pressure-label/) shows how.

**Servicing.** Ask when the bike was last serviced and what to do if it needs attention during your rental. Do not authorise a major repair or attempt a makeshift fix without calling the shop first.

**Fuel.** Petrol bikes are handed over with about 0.5 to 2 litres and returned with about 0.5 to 1 litre, and an empty tank at return costs VND 20,000. Our guide to [estimating a Honda Wave trip fuel budget](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/honda-wave-fuel-economy-how-to-estimate-a-trip-budget/) helps you plan.

**Rain.** A raincoat is not included for short rentals but may be considered for monthly ones, so ask. Our guide to [riding in Hanoi rain](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/riding-in-hanoi-rain/) explains what a Hanoi downpour means for a bike rider.

## Costs beyond the rent

The rent is not the whole bill, and the extras apply differently by period.

| Item | Published term |
|---|---|
| Deposit | VND 2,000,000–5,000,000, depending on the bike and contract, separate from the rent |
| Delivery | Weekly or monthly only; VND 50,000 per trip or VND 100,000 both ways within 10 km of Hoan Kiem Lake |
| Late return | VND 30,000 per hour for 1 to 5 hours; one extra day from 6 hours |
| Empty tank at return | VND 20,000 |
| Helmet | One basic helmet per rider, free |

The deposit is a cash deposit, separate from the rent. Identity documents and the licence are checked and photographed for the contract, and originals are not kept. Our guide to [motorbike rental deposits in Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/motorbike-rental-deposit-hanoi/) explains the arrangement, and our guide to [returning your rental motorbike in Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/returning-your-rental-motorbike-hanoi/) explains how the return is checked.

## Four example riders

These are illustrations, not quotes.

**The two-day sightseer.** Arrives for a week, wants to ride on two days. A daily rental for those days fits, because the rest of the time the bike would sit idle.

**The weekend to Ninh Binh.** Wants to ride out on Saturday and return Sunday. A two-day rental fits, and the shop can confirm how the return time is counted. Our guide to [Hanoi to Ninh Binh and back in one day](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/hanoi-ninh-binh-one-day-motorbike-itinerary/) gives a route plan if you prefer to go and return in one day.

**The intern.** Has a placement across Long Bien for six weeks, rides most days and wants delivery. A monthly package, with a second period agreed in advance, fits.

**The remote worker with uncertain plans.** Might stay three weeks or might leave early. Two weekly packages, or a weekly and an extension, give the flexibility that a month would not.

## FAQ: daily or monthly manual bike rental

### Is a monthly rental always cheaper per day?

The published ranges suggest so, but only if you use the whole period. Unused rent is not refunded after an early return, so count only days you will certainly ride.

### What are the reference rates for a manual bike?

VND 150,000–200,000 per day, VND 700,000–800,000 per week and VND 1,000,000–1,200,000 per month, confirmed on the actual bike before you sign.

### Can I get delivery on a daily rental?

No. Delivery is for weekly and monthly agreements, within 10 km of Hoan Kiem Lake.

### What if I want to extend?

Ask the shop how an extension would be priced, and have it recorded in writing.

### Is a manual bike a good choice for a month?

It can be, if you are comfortable with gears. Ask about chain care, brakes and tyres, and read our guide to [when a semi-automatic bike is easier than an automatic scooter](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/when-a-semi-automatic-bike-is-easier-than-an-automatic-scooter/).

### Does the deposit change with the period?

The deposit is VND 2,000,000–5,000,000 depending on the bike and contract. Ask what applies to yours.
