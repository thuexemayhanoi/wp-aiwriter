Keep your passport safe during a scooter rental by never leaving it with the shop as a deposit, carrying a clear copy or phone photo instead of the original on the bike, storing the original somewhere secure at your hotel and keeping digital copies in more than one place. A passport is the one document you cannot replace in an afternoon, and a rental is a moment when it is easy to put at risk: shops sometimes ask to hold it, bikes have little secure storage and a long day out means a bag that can be left behind or taken.

Nguyen Ha takes a cash deposit, checks and photographs your identity documents and driving licence for the contract and hands the originals straight back, so you have no reason to part with yours. This guide explains why you should keep it, what a shop may reasonably do with it, how to carry and store it, what never to leave on the bike, what to do if a shop insists and what to do if it is lost or stolen. It is general information, not legal advice.

## Why a passport is different from other documents

A lost licence is a nuisance. A lost passport can stop your trip. Hotels in Vietnam register foreign guests and ask to see it at check-in. Banks, SIM-card shops and domestic flights ask for it. Replacing it means contacting your embassy or consulate, which takes time and usually involves a visit, photographs and fees.

A shop that holds your passport also holds a lever in any dispute, which is why leaving it is a poor idea even with a shop you trust. ## What Nguyen Ha does with your documents

Identity documents and the driving licence are checked and photographed for the contract, and the originals are not kept. This is not a no-deposit service, and it is not a passport-deposit service.

So at the counter:

- **Take them back at once,** and check you have the right documents before you leave.

- **Do not hand over the passport** to be held for the rental.

If you are unsure what happens to the photographs, ask how they are stored and who can see them. A good shop will explain. ## What to carry on the bike

Vietnam's Law on Road Traffic Order and Safety (Law No. 36/2024/QH15), in force since 1 January 2025, requires a rider to carry the vehicle's registration certificate, the driving licence and the compulsory insurance certificate. As we explain in our guide to [what documents to carry when riding in Vietnam](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/documents-to-carry-riding-in-vietnam/), on a rental the shop supplies the registration card and the insurance certificate, and you supply the licence and, for a foreign licence, the International Driving Permit.

The passport is a different question. The traffic law does not require it, but many riders carry a clear photocopy or a phone photo of the main page and the visa or entry stamp, and keep the original at the hotel. That is the arrangement we suggest, and you should ask your hotel, your embassy and the shop if you want to know whether they advise otherwise for your situation. Keep a copy in your wallet and a photo on your phone.

## Where to keep the original

- **In the hotel safe,** if your room has one, or at reception in a sealed envelope, if the hotel offers it and you trust it. Ask how it is stored.

- **On you, in a zipped inner pocket,** if you will be travelling that day, not in a bag you may put down.

- **Never on the bike.** A scooter has little secure storage. Honda's manual advises against valuables in the storage compartment, and bikes can be taken, forced open or left behind.

- **Never with the shop,** as security for the rental.

If you move between hotels during the rental, check you have the passport each time. ## Copies and digital backups

Make copies before you travel and again on arrival.

1. **Photograph the main page** and the page with your Vietnamese visa or entry stamp, if you have one.

2. **Save the photos** to a cloud account and send a copy to a family member or yourself by email.

3. **Keep a paper photocopy** separate from the original.

4. **Note your passport number, issue and expiry dates,** and your embassy's emergency number.

5. **Photograph your driving licence and IDP** the same way.

A copy is not a replacement, but it speeds up a police report, an embassy visit and an insurance claim.

## What not to leave on the bike

- **Your passport.**

- **Cash and cards.**

- **Phone and camera.**

- **Keys** to your hotel room or other property.

- **Any document** you would not want to lose.

Carry them on you, or leave them at the hotel. ## If a shop asks to hold your passport

Some shops in Hanoi still ask for a passport instead of money. Do not agree.

- **Say politely that you prefer to leave a cash deposit** and keep your passport, and ask what the cash deposit would be.

- **Offer to show it** so that it can be checked and photographed, and take it back.

- **If the shop insists,** choose another shop. A rental is not worth a lost passport.

- **Never leave an original document of any kind,** including your licence, as security.

A shop that works as Nguyen Ha does, with a cash deposit and photographs, shows that the system works without holding originals.

## If your passport is lost or stolen

1. **Retrace your steps,** and ask where you were. Check your bag, your hotel room and the places you stopped.

2. **Report it to the police.** The Ministry of Public Security's guidance on reporting crimes says a report may be made orally or in writing, in person at ward police or at a police station, and that the person who reports has the right to be told the result. Ask for a written record or reference number. Our guide to [what to do if your rental motorbike is stolen](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/what-to-do-if-your-rental-motorbike-is-stolen/) explains how to report.

3. **Contact your embassy or consulate.** They issue emergency or replacement documents and can advise on what Vietnam requires for you to leave. Bring your copies and the police record.

4. **Tell your hotel,** and your insurer if you have cover for lost documents.

5. **Tell the shop** if the loss may be linked to your rental, for example if it was in the bike's compartment. Our guide to [lost rental bike keys: reporting and replacement steps](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/lost-rental-bike-keys-reporting-and-replacement-steps/) covers the shop side of lost items.

6. **Keep a record** of every step, and every receipt.

## Risks to watch for

- **A passport left in a hotel room,** in plain view.

- **A bag hung on the handlebars,** which can be snatched.

- **A document pocket** that is open, or a zip left half done.

- **A request to "hold" your passport** for a booking, a tour or a bike.

- **Someone who offers to "register" you** for a fee and asks for the original.

- **Photographing your passport** and posting it online or sending it to unknown people.

## A quick checklist

1. **Pay a cash deposit; never leave your passport.**

2. **Let the shop photograph your documents, and take them back at once.**

3. **Keep the original at the hotel or on you, not on the bike.**

4. **Carry a copy or phone photo.**

5. **Back up copies in the cloud and with someone you trust.**

6. **Know your embassy's number.**

7. **If it is lost, report to the police and contact your embassy.**

## FAQ: keeping your passport safe

### Should I leave my passport as a deposit?

No. Nguyen Ha takes a cash deposit of VND 2,000,000–5,000,000 and does not keep original documents. A shop that holds your passport holds a lever in any dispute.

### Do I need to carry my passport while riding?

The traffic law requires the bike's registration, your driving licence and the insurance certificate. Many riders carry a copy or photo of the passport and keep the original at the hotel.

### Can I keep it in the bike's compartment?

No. Honda advises against valuables in the storage compartment, and bikes can be forced open or taken.

### What does the shop do with my documents?

They are checked and photographed for the contract and handed straight back. Ask how the photographs are stored.

### What if it is stolen?

Report it to the police, contact your embassy or consulate and keep the written record.

### What if the shop insists on holding it?

Choose another shop.
