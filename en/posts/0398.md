A monthly motorbike rental in Hanoi costs far less per day than renting week after week, but it locks you into a longer commitment with the same cash deposit, no refund for early return and more questions about servicing. At Nguyen Ha Motorbike Rental the published month range for a manual bike is VND 1,000,000 to 1,200,000, while a week is VND 700,000 to 800,000, so a month costs less than two weeks of weekly packages. If you will clearly need the bike for four weeks or more, the monthly package is the one to price first; if you are unsure, the weekly package is the cheaper way to find out.

The figures below are the shop's reference ranges, and the final price is confirmed on the actual bike. Use them to compare, then ask for the exact figure.

## How the ranges compare

The published weekly and monthly ranges by bike type, in VND, are:

| Bike type | Week | Month |
|---|---|---|
| Manual | 700,000 to 800,000 | 1,000,000 to 1,200,000 |
| Automatic | 600,000 to 1,000,000 | 1,200,000 to 2,000,000 |
| Electric | 600,000 to 800,000 | 1,500,000 to 1,800,000 |
| 50cc | 1,000,000 | 2,000,000 |

These are editorial snapshots read from the Vietnamese price page in October 2026, and the current ranges are on the [prices page](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/prices/). The pattern is consistent: the month range is roughly one and a quarter to three times the week range, depending on bike type and model, even though a month is about four weeks long. For a manual bike the top of the month range is less than two top-of-range weeks. For a 50cc scooter the month is exactly two weeks of rent. For electric scooters and wide-range automatics the multiple is higher, so compare the exact figures for your model.

## Four weeks against one month

The most useful comparison is not week versus month but four consecutive weeks versus one month. Four manual-bike weeks at the published range would cost well over double the top of the monthly range. The same logic holds for the other types: whenever you know you need about a month, the monthly package is the efficient one.

The exception is uncertainty. Suppose you have a month in Hanoi but might leave early for the mountains, or you are not sure the bike suits you. A weekly package gives you a natural exit each seven days. A monthly package, with no refund for early return, is a bet that you will use the whole term. The saving from a month is only a saving if you actually keep the bike.

A sensible approach for a doubtful case is to start with a week, learn how the bike behaves and how you use it, and then ask the shop whether you can convert to a monthly agreement and how the weekly rent already paid would be treated. Do not assume it can; ask before you sign. Our article on [what long-term renters should ask](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/weekly-monthly-motorbike-rental-hanoi/) lists the wider checklist.

## Terms that do not shrink with the price

The monthly price is lower per day, but several terms are the same in kind and larger in consequence.

### The deposit can outweigh the rent

The cash deposit is VND 2,000,000 to VND 5,000,000, depending on the bike and contract, and it is separate from the rent. For a manual bike the published month range is VND 1,000,000 to 1,200,000, so the deposit can be larger than the month's rent itself. Ask how it is held, what written condition record supports its return, and how quickly it is returned at the end. Our guide to [how a rental deposit works](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/motorbike-rental-deposit-hanoi/) walks through the return.

### Unused rent is not refunded

Early return does not refund unused rent. On a one-week package the loss from leaving early is small. On a monthly package, leaving after ten days can mean paying for twenty days you do not use. If your plans may change, a weekly package keeps the exposure small.

### Late return is counted by the hour, then by the day

From 1 to 5 hours late costs VND 30,000 per hour, and from 6 hours late it is one extra rental day. At the end of a month you may be moving between lodgings, packing and travelling, so build a buffer into your return day. Ask exactly what time the final day ends and have it written on the contract.

## Delivery and pickup

Delivery is offered for weekly and monthly agreements only, within 10 km of Hoan Kiem Lake, at VND 50,000 per trip or VND 100,000 for both ways, with distances beyond 10 km agreed in advance. There is no airport service. For a monthly renter, delivery matters at both ends, so ask whether the return can also be collected from your address and whether the both-ways price applies. ## Living with the bike for a month

A month is long enough for ordinary wear and care to matter. Short rentals rarely raise these points; a monthly rental always does.

### Servicing and wear

Ask who is responsible for routine care during the term: oil, tyre pressure, chain lubrication and brake wear, and what the shop expects you to report. The sensible rule is to report a change early, rather than ride on a fault. Our article on [what to do if a rental bike breaks down](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/rental-bike-breakdown-hanoi-what-to-do/) explains how to deal with a problem on the road. Ask whether a replacement bike is offered while a repair is done, and who pays when the cause is ordinary wear rather than damage.

### Parking and security

The shop does not provide an extra lock, and it advises locking the steering and parking in guarded places. Over a month, you will park the bike hundreds of times. Check that your lodging has a safe place, and consider whether a lock of your own is worth buying.

### Fuel

Petrol bikes are handed over with about 0.5 to 2 litres and returned with about 0.5 to 1 litre, and an empty tank at return costs VND 20,000. Over a month the fuel is your cost throughout, so see our guide to [filling up in Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/fuel-in-hanoi-petrol-stations-rental-scooter/) for how fuelling works in practice.

### Charging an electric scooter

An electric bike has a monthly range of VND 1,500,000 to 1,800,000 in the published list. For an electric scooter the battery level and charging are confirmed at handover, and for a month the question is where you will charge it every few days. If your lodging cannot charge it, the plan fails. Our guide to [charging an electric scooter in Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/charging-an-electric-scooter-in-hanoi/) covers hotels and swap stations, and the page on [renting an electric scooter](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/renting-an-electric-scooter-in-hanoi/) covers range.

### Rain gear and comfort

A raincoat is not included for short rentals and may be considered for monthly ones. Ask early rather than assuming. One basic helmet per rider is free, and over a month a helmet you chose and that fits properly is a sound investment.

## Cash flow and payment

Ask how the rent is paid. Is the whole month due at handover, or is it split? Is the deposit taken in cash on the first day? What proof of payment will you receive? Nothing here states a particular method, because the shop sets its own; the useful behaviour is to ask and write the answer down. Never pay into an account that you have not verified using the shop's own contact page.

## Licence validity across the month

Your licence must be valid and of the right class for the bike for the whole month, not only on the first day. The Law on Road Traffic Order and Safety, Law 36/2024/QH15, in force from 1 January 2025, lists Class A1 as covering two-wheeled motorcycles up to 125 cm3 or an electric motor up to 11 kW. Check that your licence and any international permit will not expire during the term. This is general information as of October 2026 and not advice on your own papers, so check the current text with the relevant authority.

## A checklist for a monthly term

Before you sign a monthly agreement, confirm these in writing:

- The exact monthly price for the specific bike, and what it includes.
- The start and end date and time, in 24-hour terms.
- The deposit amount, the condition record, and the return process.
- Who handles servicing and replacement if the bike fails.
- What happens if you return early or extend.
- Whether delivery and collection are available, and at what price.
- How fuel and, for electric, charging are handled at handover and return.
- Your licence validity across the whole period.

## FAQ about weekly and monthly terms

### Is a monthly rental always cheaper per day than weekly?

At the published ranges, yes, for every bike type listed. A month is priced at no more than about three weeks of weekly rent, so the daily cost falls. The saving only pays off if you use the whole term.

### Can I switch from weekly to monthly?

Ask before you start. Whether the weekly rent already paid counts towards the monthly price is a term for you and the shop to agree.

### Is the deposit larger for a monthly rental?

The deposit depends on the bike and the contract, and is between VND 2,000,000 and VND 5,000,000. Ask whether the term changes the figure for your case.

### Who pays for maintenance during a month?

Ask. Routine care and ordinary wear are usually agreed in the contract, and damage is treated differently. Get the answer in writing.
