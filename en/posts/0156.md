Choose a daily rental for short or uncertain plans, a weekly rental when you know you will ride for roughly a week and a monthly rental when the scooter will be part of your daily routine for three weeks or more. The right period depends less on the price per day than on how sure you are about your dates, because early return does not refund unused rent and a day is a fixed 24 hours. For an automatic scooter such as a Honda Vision, Air Blade, Click or Lead, a longer rental also brings extra jobs: checking tyres and brakes now and then, refuelling sensibly and knowing who to call if something wears out. This guide sets out how the three periods work at Nguyen Ha Motorbike Rental, what the price ranges do and do not tell you, what changes on a longer rental and a short checklist for deciding. Prices and availability are confirmed with the shop.

## The short answer by trip length

| Your situation | Usually fits | Why |
|---|---|---|
| One to a few days, dates may move | Daily | You pay only for the days you use, and you can extend if you stay longer |
| About a week, dates fixed | Weekly | A package is priced on its own and is usually better value than a run of day rates |
| Two weeks | Weekly, then extend | Two weekly periods let you review the bike halfway through |
| Three weeks or more, working or studying in Hanoi | Monthly | Lowest cost per day, and the bike becomes your commuter |
| A month, but you might leave early | Weekly or daily | Early return does not refund unused rent, so a long package is a risk |
| Unsure of everything | Start daily | Switch to a longer period once your plans settle, and ask the shop whether it can be arranged |

Treat this table as a starting point. The shop quotes the real price on the actual bike, and a package can be arranged differently from what is described here, so ask when you book.

## How the rental periods work here

The shop's own terms are the ground rules, so read them before you compare anything else:

- **A day is 24 hours.** Using the bike for a shorter time is still charged as a full day.
- **Weekly and monthly packages are priced on their own,** not as the daily rate multiplied by the number of days.
- **A late return costs VND 30,000 per hour for the first one to five hours,** and from six hours late it becomes one extra rental day.
- **Early return does not refund unused rent.** If you hand the bike back after four days of a seven-day week, the remaining days are not credited.
- **A cash deposit of VND 2,000,000 to 5,000,000** is taken, depending on the bike and the contract. It is separate from the rent and is not an additional rental charge.
- **Identity documents and the licence are checked and photographed** for the contract, and originals are not kept.

## Daily rental: when it makes sense

A daily rental suits plans that are short, flexible or not yet certain. You might be exploring the city for a weekend, testing whether you are comfortable on an automatic in Hanoi traffic or covering a trip where the weather decides the route.

Its strengths are simplicity and low commitment. You pay for the days you use, you can usually return the bike without worrying about an unused package, and the bike gets a fresh inspection at every handover.

Its weaknesses are cost and fuss. A run of daily rentals can add up to more than a weekly package, and each handover repeats the paperwork. If you find yourself extending day after day, it is worth asking the shop whether a weekly price can apply. Remember also that a day is 24 hours. Plan your return time so that you are not caught by the late-return rule.

## Weekly rental: the middle path

A weekly rental fits a trip with a settled shape: a week in Hanoi, a few days in the city plus a ride to Ninh Binh, or a visit where you want a bike but not an open-ended commitment. Because a weekly package is priced separately from the daily rate, it can work out better value than repeating day charges, as long as you will use most of it.

It also gives you time to get used to the bike. By the third day you will know whether the brakes feel right, whether the seat is comfortable and whether the storage suits your luggage. If something is wrong, you have time to ask for a swap rather than put up with it for a month.

The risk is the same as with any fixed period. Return early and the unused days are not refunded, so do not choose a week for a trip that might end after four days.

Delivery can come into the picture here. At this shop it is offered only for weekly and monthly agreements, within 10 km of Hoan Kiem Lake, at VND 50,000 per trip or VND 100,000 both ways. Beyond 10 km it is agreed in advance, and there is no airport service. Ask about it at booking, not on arrival.

## Monthly rental: living with the scooter

A monthly rental suits a longer stay: a work contract, a language course, remote work from a rented flat or a long season in the city. It usually has the lowest cost per day, and it turns the scooter into a commuter rather than a holiday item.

It also changes the relationship with the bike. Over a month you may ride a few hundred kilometres or far more, depending on your routine. The manufacturer's maintenance schedule exists for that reason. Honda's Vision manual lays out service checks in steps of 1,000 km, 6,000 km, 12,000 km and so on, covering items such as engine oil, the drive belt, brake pad or shoe wear and tyres. A rider doing a typical city commute will not reach the larger intervals in a month, but small things like tyre pressure and brake feel need attention within weeks.

For that reason, a monthly renter should agree in advance who handles routine upkeep and what happens if the bike needs a repair. Our [Hanoi motorbike rental contract explainer](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/hanoi-motorbike-rental-contract-explained/) covers how those clauses usually work. A raincoat is not included for short rentals but may be considered for monthly ones, so ask if you will ride through the wet months.

## What changes on a longer rental with an automatic

An automatic scooter has no gears or clutch to manage, which is a big reason people choose one. The care it needs is of a different kind.

### Tyres and brakes

Honda's manuals ask riders to check tyre pressure at least once a month and whenever a tyre looks soft, and to look for the tread wear indicator mark. The Vision manual gives 200 kPa at the front and 225 kPa at the rear, while the Lead manual gives 175 kPa and 225 kPa, so the numbers differ by model. Ask the shop for the right figure for your bike and where to inflate it.

Brakes need attention too. Honda describes wear marks on the front brake pads and on the rear brake, plus a rear lever free play of 10 to 20 mm on the Vision. You do not need to measure anything yourself, but if a lever starts to feel softer or noisier over the weeks, report it.

### Fuel

Petrol bikes are handed over with about 0.5 to 2 litres and should come back with about 0.5 to 1 litre. An empty tank at return costs VND 20,000. Honda lists the Vision's tank at 4.8 litres and the Lead's at 6.0 litres, with claimed consumption of about 1.82 and 2.1 litres per 100 km. These are manufacturer figures under test conditions, so real use in Hanoi traffic will differ. Our guide to [fuel in Hanoi and filling up a rental scooter](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/fuel-in-hanoi-petrol-stations-rental-scooter/) explains where to refuel.

### Storage and daily use

Over a month you will use the under-seat storage every day. Honda lists the Air Blade's at 23.2 litres and the Lead's at more than 37 litres. The Vision's is smaller, commonly reported around 18 litres, so check the space against your helmet and daily bag. For longer rentals, a comfortable seat and enough storage matter more than top speed.

### Breakdowns and swaps

Ask what happens if the bike fails: whether a replacement is possible and how fast. Our guide to [lost keys, flat tyres and breakdowns with a rental bike](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/rental-bike-breakdown-hanoi-what-to-do/) explains what to do on the road.

## Mistakes to avoid

- **Choosing a month "just in case."** If your plans might change, a monthly package is the wrong tool, because early return does not refund unused rent.
- **Stacking day rates for a long stay.** If you are on your fifth consecutive day, ask for a weekly price.
- **Comparing prices without comparing bikes.** A cheap rate on a worn bike is not a bargain.
- **Ignoring the 24-hour rule.** Collecting at 17:00 and returning at 11:00 two days later is two days and a bit, and the "bit" can matter.
- **Skipping the handover check.** A longer rental deserves a more careful inspection, so follow our [10-minute rental scooter inspection checklist](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/rental-scooter-inspection-checklist/).
- **Forgetting the paperwork.** Check our guide to [what documents to carry when riding in Vietnam](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/documents-to-carry-riding-in-vietnam/) before you plan a long stay.

## A short checklist for deciding

1. How many days will I really ride, counting optimistic and pessimistic dates?
2. Could my plans change, and would an unused period be wasted?
3. Have I asked for daily, weekly and monthly quotes on the same bike?
4. Does the bike's storage and size suit my luggage and my passenger, if any?
5. Do I need delivery within 10 km of Hoan Kiem Lake, which is offered for weekly and monthly agreements?
6. Do I know who handles upkeep and breakdowns during the rental?
7. Have I read the late-return and early-return terms?
8. Is the deposit amount clear before I sign?

If you can answer all eight, the right period is usually obvious. The [rental guide](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/rental-guide/) walks through the whole process from enquiry to return.

## FAQ: automatic scooter rental periods

### Is a weekly rental cheaper than seven daily rentals?

A weekly package is priced on its own, so it is not simply seven day rates. Ask the shop for both prices on the same bike and compare them.

### Can I switch from daily to weekly or monthly?

Ask the shop at the time. Do not assume an extension will be repriced automatically.

### What if I return the scooter early?

Early return does not refund unused rent, so choose the period carefully.

### What if I return it late?

For the first one to five hours it is VND 30,000 per hour. From six hours late it counts as one extra rental day.

### Does the deposit change with the rental period?

The cash deposit is VND 2,000,000 to 5,000,000, depending on the bike and the contract. Ask for the figure for your bike before you sign.
