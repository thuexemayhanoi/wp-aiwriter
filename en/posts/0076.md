A semi-automatic motorbike's dashboard is small and simple, but each light carries a message you should know before you ride. On a Honda Wave RSX FI, the instrument panel shows a speedometer, an odometer, a fuel gauge, left and right turn signal lights, a gear position display, a neutral indicator, a high-beam light and an engine fault lamp for the fuel-injection system. Other models, including the Wave Alpha, the Blade and the Yamaha Sirius, have different layouts, and some have fewer lights. This guide explains each indicator using Honda's Vietnamese owner's manual, shows what the panel should do when you switch on the key, explains which lights are urgent and which are routine and gives a handover routine so you know your bike's dashboard before you leave the shop. It is general information, not a diagnosis. Follow the manual and the demonstration for the exact bike you rent.

## Why a two-minute dashboard briefing is worth it

On a scooter or a small manual bike, you rarely look at the dashboard. Your eyes are on the road, the mirrors and the traffic. That is as it should be. But a few lights are important enough that you should know them without thinking: the neutral light, the turn signal lights, the high-beam light, the fuel warning and, on fuel-injected bikes, the engine fault lamp. Learning them at the shop takes two minutes, and it can save you from a stall, a dead battery or a long walk.

Ask the shop to start the bike and point to each light. If a light is missing or not working, that is something to record in the handover note.

## What Honda's Wave RSX FI manual lists

The manual's section on the instrument panel and indicator lights lists these items:

- **Speedometer.**
- **Odometer,** which shows the total distance the bike has travelled.
- **Fuel gauge.** The manual says that when the needle is on the red mark, about 1.2 litres of fuel remain in the tank.
- **Left turn signal indicator.**
- **Right turn signal indicator.**
- **Gear position indicator,** which shows positions 1 to 4.
- **Neutral indicator,** marked N, which lights when the gearbox is in neutral.
- **High-beam indicator.**
- **PGM-FI malfunction indicator lamp (MIL),** the engine fault lamp for the electronic fuel-injection system. The manual says it lights briefly when you turn the key on. It also covers what it means if the lamp comes on while the engine is running.

Not every Honda has every one of these. A simpler model may have a neutral light but no gear display, and a carburettor model has no fuel-injection fault lamp. Check what your bike has.

## The neutral light

The neutral indicator is the most-used light on a gear bike. Honda's start procedure calls for shifting to neutral until the N indicator lights before you press the start switch, and the training document's checklist for starting and ending a ride also has the rider return to neutral. If the light does not come on when you believe the bike is in neutral, do not assume. Squeeze the brake, try the starting procedure with care and ask the shop to check the switch. Our guide to [understanding neutral on a Honda Wave](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/understanding-neutral-on-a-honda-wave/) explains how to be sure.

If the neutral light stays on while you ride in gear, or stays off when you are in neutral, tell the shop. It is not an emergency, but a faulty indicator makes it harder to know what the gearbox is doing.

## The gear position display

On the RSX FI, a display shows which gear you are in, from 1 to 4. It helps when you have just changed and cannot remember where you are, and it helps new riders confirm that first gear is selected before pulling away. You should not stare at it while riding. Use it at a standstill and during slow, calm moments. Do not rely on it more than on the engine's sound and the feel of the bike. If the display shows a gear that does not match what the bike is doing, tell the shop.

## Turn signal lights

The left and right indicators flash when you use the turn signals and show that the signals are on. Check them at handover by operating each one and watching the dashboard light and the flashing bulbs. A signal that does not flash, flashes very fast or stays on is a bulb or relay problem. Fast flashing often means a bulb has failed. Our guide to [crossing Hanoi intersections](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/crossing-hanoi-intersections/) explains why signalling matters in dense traffic, and the law fines a rider who turns without a signal or does not keep the signal on during the turn VND 600,000–800,000 under Decree 168/2024/ND-CP as of October 2026.

Remember to switch the signal off after a turn. A signal left flashing misleads other riders.

## The high-beam light

The high-beam indicator lights when the main beam is on. Honda's safe-riding training document gives night-riding advice: use the high beam for better visibility, but switch to the low beam when a vehicle or pedestrian approaches or when riding in an urban area, and avoid looking straight into oncoming headlights. In Hanoi's lit streets you will almost always ride on low beam. Check that the switch changes the beam and that the indicator follows it.

## The fuel gauge and the red mark

The needle shows the fuel level. Honda says that at the red mark on the RSX FI, about 1.2 litres remain, out of a tank of 4.0 litres. That is roughly a quarter of the tank, and at Honda's quoted consumption of 1.56 litres per 100 km it is theoretically enough for many tens of kilometres, but real use gives less, and a small bike on a hill or with a passenger drinks more. Treat the red mark as a firm instruction to refuel at the next station, not as a reserve to use up. Our guide to [fuel in Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/fuel-in-hanoi-petrol-stations-rental-scooter/) explains how to fill the tank.

Gauges on small bikes can be optimistic or slow to move, and the needle can bounce on bumps. At Nguyen Ha, a petrol bike is handed over with roughly half a litre to two litres and is expected back with roughly half a litre to one litre, so check the gauge at pickup and note it on the handover record.

## The engine fault lamp (MIL)

On a fuel-injected model such as the Wave RSX FI, the PGM-FI malfunction indicator lamp lights briefly when you switch the key on, which is a bulb check, and should then go out. If it comes on while the engine is running, or stays on after the start, it is telling you the fuel-injection system has detected a problem. Honda's manual lists it among the items to read in the instrument panel section; we do not have the full text of the manual's instruction, so treat a lamp that stays on while riding as a fault, stop in a safe place and tell the shop. A bike with the lamp on may run poorly or use more fuel, and a mechanic needs to read it.

If the lamp does not light at all when you turn the key on, the bulb may be faulty, which also deserves a mention.

## Other lights you may see

Some bikes have additional indicators: a low-battery or charging lamp, an oil-pressure lamp, an immobiliser or smart-key light, or a side-stand warning. These are not on the RSX FI list we checked, and they differ by model. If your bike has one, ask what it means and what to do. The general rule is the same: a light that was off and comes on while riding is a message to stop and ask.

## A handover routine for the dashboard

At pickup, with the shop beside you:

1. **Turn the key on and watch the lights.** Note which ones light and go out.
2. **Start the engine.** The neutral light should be on if the bike is in neutral.
3. **Operate the turn signals** left and right and watch the dashboard lights.
4. **Switch to high beam** and back and watch the indicator.
5. **Check the fuel gauge** against what the shop says is in the tank.
6. **Note any missing or odd lights,** and photograph the dashboard with the key on and the engine running.

## What to do if a light comes on

- **Neutral light at the wrong time:** stop, check the gear and tell the shop.
- **Engine fault lamp on while riding:** stop in a safe place, switch off, restart once and, if it stays on, call the shop.
- **Fuel at the red mark:** refuel soon.
- **A turn signal that will not flash or flashes fast:** check the bulb and tell the shop. Do not ride without working signals in dense traffic.
- **Any new light you do not recognise:** photograph it and call the shop.

## FAQ: dashboard lights

### What does the N light mean?

It lights when the gearbox is in neutral. Honda's start procedure uses it before you press the start switch.

### What is the red mark on the fuel gauge?

On the Honda Wave RSX FI, Honda says about 1.2 litres remain at the red mark. Refuel soon.

### What is the engine fault lamp?

On a fuel-injected Honda, the PGM-FI malfunction indicator lamp lights briefly at key-on and should then go out. If it comes on while the engine is running, tell the shop.

### Should the lights come on when I turn the key?

Some indicator lamps light briefly as a bulb check. Ask the shop what your bike should do.

### Do all semi-automatic bikes have a gear display?

No. The Wave RSX FI shows positions 1 to 4, but other models may not.

### Should I ride with a light on?

Not if it is a fault light. Stop and call the shop.
