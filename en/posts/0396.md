A 24-hour rental day in Hanoi runs from the moment you take the bike to the same clock time the next day, not to midnight and not to the end of the shop's opening hours. At Nguyen Ha Motorbike Rental, shorter use is still charged as one full day, a return that is 1 to 5 hours late costs VND 30,000 per hour, a return 6 or more hours late becomes one extra rental day, and returning early does not refund unused rent. Understanding that arithmetic before you sign lets you choose your pickup time deliberately instead of finding out at the return counter.

The rules below are the shop's published terms. Another shop may count differently, so treat every point as a question to confirm for your own booking.

## The basic rule: a day is a block of 24 hours

Many visitors expect a "day" to mean a calendar date, the way a hotel night does. Motorbike rentals usually work the other way round. The rental is a block of time that starts when you take the bike and lasts 24 hours for each day you booked.

So if you collect the bike at 10:00 on Monday, a one-day rental is due back at 10:00 on Tuesday. A bike collected at 17:00 is due at 17:00 the next day. Nothing happens at midnight. The consequence is that your pickup time is also your deadline, so a late morning pickup gives you a full day of riding on Monday afternoon and Tuesday morning, while an evening pickup gives you the bike overnight and a day of sightseeing starting the next morning.

Nguyen Ha is open 09:00 to 21:00 daily, which sets the window in which you can collect and return the bike. A pickup at 20:00 would mean a return due at 20:00 the next day, still inside opening hours. If your plans put the return outside those hours, agree in advance what happens.

## Why short use still counts as a full day

At Nguyen Ha, shorter use is charged as a full day. Borrowing the bike for five hours does not bring a five-hour price. For the shop, preparing, handing over, inspecting and returning a bike takes the same effort whether the rental lasts five hours or twenty-four, and the bike cannot easily be rented again later the same day.

For you, the practical effect is simple. If you only want a bike for a short ride, you pay the day rate anyway, so you may as well make use of the whole 24 hours. Our [prices page](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/prices/) shows the current day ranges, which are VND 150,000 to 200,000 for the bike types the shop lists. Confirm the exact figure for your bike before you sign.

## Three ways the clock can surprise you

### 1. The clock starts when you take the bike, not when you book

Booking a bike for Monday does not start the clock on Monday morning. The count starts at handover. Ask the shop to write the handover time on the contract, and photograph that page. A recorded start time prevents any later disagreement about when the day began.

### 2. Long inspections and slow starts use your time

A careful 10-minute inspection is worth every minute, as our [rental scooter inspection checklist](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/rental-scooter-inspection-checklist/) explains. Whether that time counts is something to settle. Ask: "Is the day counted from the time on the contract?" If the answer is yes, a delay at the counter shortens your usable day, and you may prefer to arrive at a quiet time.

### 3. Your phone clock may be in the wrong time zone

Vietnam uses one time zone for the whole country, UTC+7, and does not change its clocks for summer. If your phone has not updated after a flight, your reminder for the return time may be hours off. Set an alarm using the local time and write the due time on a slip of paper or in a note.

## How late returns are charged

At Nguyen Ha the rule has two steps:

| Time after the due moment | What the shop applies |
|---|---|
| 1 to 5 hours late | VND 30,000 for each hour |
| 6 hours or more late | One extra rental day |

Read this as a ladder. For the first few hours the cost grows by a fixed amount per hour. Once you reach the sixth hour it no longer makes sense to count hours, so the shop treats it as another day at the daily rate. That means a short delay is cheap, but by the fifth late hour the hourly charges have grown to roughly the price of a day at the low end of the range, so a bike kept for most of an extra day is better treated as an extra day.

Questions worth asking about the ladder:

- Is a part hour counted as a full hour? For example, 20 minutes late.
- Is there a short grace period, and is it written down?
- Is the late period counted from the due time on the contract, or from the time you phone in?
- If you warn the shop in advance that you will be late, can the extension be agreed on the phone, and is the price the same?

Calling early is almost always better than arriving late without a word. The shop can tell you whether the bike is booked out for someone else, and you avoid the worst outcome, which is a bike that is needed elsewhere. The number is 0334 699 969 (+84 334 699 969), open 09:00 to 21:00 daily, and the [contact page](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/contact/) has the details.

## Early returns and unused rent

Returning the bike early does not refund unused rent at Nguyen Ha. If you booked three days and finish on the evening of the second, the third day is still paid.

This is the reverse of the late-return rule, and it matters for how you choose the length of your booking. If you are unsure whether you will use the bike for three days or two, ask whether an extension is possible and how it is priced. Paying for an extra day later may be better than paying for an unused one now, although the shop's answer depends on availability. ## Examples with clock times

These examples use the shop's terms and are illustrative only. Dates are invented to show the logic.

| Situation | How it is counted |
|---|---|
| Pickup Monday 10:00, one day booked, back Tuesday 09:30 | One day; the early half hour is not refunded |
| Pickup Monday 10:00, one day booked, back Tuesday 10:00 | One day, on time |
| Pickup Monday 10:00, one day booked, back Tuesday 12:00 | Two hours late, charged per hour at VND 30,000 |
| Pickup Monday 10:00, one day booked, back Tuesday 17:00 | Seven hours late, so one extra rental day |
| Pickup Monday 15:00, back Monday 19:00 | Four hours of use, still charged as one full day |
| Pickup Monday 10:00, two days booked, back Tuesday 18:00 | Two days booked; the second day is paid even though it was unused |

Treat the third row as a guide to the principle rather than a quote. Ask the shop how it would calculate your specific case, including whether part hours count.

## Weekly and monthly rentals count differently

Weekly and monthly packages at Nguyen Ha are priced on their own and are not simply the day rate multiplied. The question for a long rental is how the shop counts the package: whether a week is seven 24-hour blocks from handover, whether a month is a fixed number of days or a calendar month, and what a late return costs once the package ends. Ask for the end date and time to be written on the contract. Our guide to [what long-term renters should ask](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/weekly-monthly-motorbike-rental-hanoi/) covers the other terms.

Delivery is available only with weekly or monthly agreements, within 10 km of Hoan Kiem Lake, so if your bike is brought to you, ask which moment starts the clock: the time the bike leaves the shop, or the time it reaches you.

## Planning your day around the 24 hours

A few simple choices make the block work for you:

- **Pick up at the start of the day you want to ride.** A 09:00 or 10:00 pickup gives you the afternoon of day one and the morning of day two.
- **Plan your return for earlier than the due time.** A return an hour before the deadline absorbs traffic and a fuel stop. For the fuel rules at handover and return, see our guide to [fuel in Hanoi and filling a rental scooter](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/fuel-in-hanoi-petrol-stations-rental-scooter/).
- **Do not rush to beat the deadline.** Saving a few hours of late fee is never worth riding tired or fast. A licence that covers the bike is required whenever you ride, including during any extra hours; under Law 36/2024/QH15, in force from 1 January 2025, that means carrying a licence valid for the vehicle class. Read the official text and the shop's own terms rather than relying on this summary.
- **If you are running late, call.** An early phone call is the cheapest fix.
- **Photograph the return.** Take a photo of the bike and of the return time, for your own records.

## FAQ about the 24-hour rental day

### Does a rental day end at midnight?

No. At Nguyen Ha a day is 24 hours from handover. A pickup at 10:00 is due at 10:00 the next day.

### Do I pay for a full day if I only ride for a few hours?

Yes. Shorter use is charged as a full day, so use the whole 24 hours or choose a pickup time that suits your plans.

### What if I am only 20 minutes late?

The published rule is stated in whole hours, 1 to 5 hours late. Ask in advance whether a part hour is rounded up, whether there is a grace period, and how the clock is read. Phone the shop if you are going to be late.

### Is there a refund if I return the bike early?

No. Unused rent is not refunded at Nguyen Ha. If you are unsure of your plans, ask about an extension rather than booking extra days you may not need.

### Does the 24 hours include the time the bike spends being inspected?

Ask. Settle it before you sign, and have the start time written on the contract.
