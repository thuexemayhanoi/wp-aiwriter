Many semi-automatic motorbikes in Hanoi's rental fleets, including the Honda Wave RSX FI, carry both an electric starter and a kick starter, and the kick lever is the backup that works when the battery is weak. Using it is simple but has a few rules: the key must be on, the gearbox must be in neutral, the throttle must be fully released and the kick must be a quick, decisive stroke from the top of the lever's travel. This guide follows Honda's Vietnamese owner's manual for the Wave RSX FI, adds practical points for a visitor and explains what to do if the engine floods or will not start. It is general information. Always follow the manual and the demonstration for the exact bike you rent, because models differ.

## When you would use it

You are most likely to need the kick starter in four situations:

- **A weak or flat battery.** The electric starter turns slowly, clicks or does nothing, while the kick lever still works because it does not depend on the battery for cranking.
- **A bike that has been standing.** After a few idle days the battery may be low.
- **A cold morning start** when the engine is reluctant and the electric starter alone is slow.
- **A shop's older bike** whose electric starter has a fault.

Not every rental has a kick lever. Many modern automatic scooters have only an electric starter, and some versions of semi-automatic bikes have both. Ask the shop when you pick up the bike whether it has a kick starter and have staff show you how it folds out and back.

## What Honda's manual says

Honda's Wave RSX FI manual, in the section on starting the engine, sets out the following steps:

1. Turn the ignition key to the ON position.
2. Shift the gearbox to neutral, with the N indicator lit.
3. Release the throttle fully.
4. Press the kick lever down gently until you feel a light resistance, then let the lever return to the top of its travel.
5. Kick quickly and decisively, in a continuous stroke from the top of the travel downwards.

If the engine will not start, the manual says to open the throttle slightly, by about 3 millimetres not counting the free play, and try again.

The manual also lists cautions for the kick starter: do not kick while the bike is moving because it can damage the engine, do not kick too hard and, after use, fold the lever back in once it has returned. These are simple, but they matter. A kick lever that is left folded out can catch a boot or a trouser leg.

## The technique, step by step

**Stand beside the bike on the left,** with the bike upright on its stand on level ground. Make sure the stand is stable. Honda's training document warns against putting the main stand on a slope or uneven ground, so choose a flat spot.

**Check the basics first.** Key on, neutral light on, throttle released, enough fuel. If the fuel gauge needle is in the red zone, Honda says the tank holds about 1.2 litres, so a bike that will not start may simply be nearly out of petrol.

**Fold out the lever** and place the ball of your right foot on its pad. Wear closed shoes. Sandals and flip-flops are a bad idea for kicking, because a slip can bruise the foot or ankle.

**Find the resistance point.** Press down gently until you feel the engine's compression, then let the lever rise to the top. This puts the piston in a good position for the quick stroke.

**Kick firmly in one motion.** Honda's instruction is quick and decisive, from the top of the travel downwards, in a continuous movement, not a slow push. Put your body weight into it, but do not stamp wildly.

**Let the lever return,** release it and fold it away once the engine is running. Keep the throttle closed until the engine settles. Honda warns that letting the engine idle for a long time or revving it continuously can damage the engine and exhaust, so do not rev it while it warms up.

## If the engine does not start

Work through these steps in order and do not kick dozens of times in a row.

1. **Check the simple things.** Is the key on? Is the neutral light lit? Is there petrol? Is the throttle fully released?
2. **Open the throttle slightly,** about 3 millimetres as Honda describes, and kick again.
3. **Consider flooding.** If you have cranked a lot and the engine smells of petrol and will not catch, it may be flooded. Honda's manual says: turn the key off, open the throttle fully, kick several times, then repeat the start procedure. If the engine starts, open the throttle gently if the idle is unstable.
4. **Rest.** If the engine does not start after several tries, stop. Repeated cranking wastes effort and can make flooding worse.
5. **Call the shop.** Describe what you did and what you saw.

If the engine starts but runs unevenly, Honda's manual says that a clogged fuel filter can cause occasional unstable running, that you can usually continue riding, and that if the engine runs unevenly even with petrol in the tank, the bike should be taken to a Honda dealer for inspection as soon as possible. Tell the shop.

## Kick starter versus electric starter

The electric starter is easier and is the normal way to start the bike. Honda's procedure for it is similar: key on, neutral, throttle fully released, press the start switch. The manual also notes that if the engine does not start within five seconds you should turn the key off, wait ten seconds and then try again to let the battery voltage recover. Our guide to [what to check on a manual bike's electric starter](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/what-to-check-on-a-manual-bikes-electric-starter/) covers it in detail.

A good habit is to try the electric starter first and keep the kick starter as a backup. If the electric starter is slow or fails, try the kick starter instead of repeating the button, which drains the battery further. If you must kick-start every time, tell the shop, because the battery or charging system needs attention.

## Common kicking mistakes

A few errors account for most failed kick starts. Kicking with the key off does nothing, because the ignition has no power. Kicking with the bike in gear, rather than neutral, can make the bike lurch or refuse to start. Kicking with the throttle open floods the engine, which Honda's procedure avoids by telling you to release the throttle fully. Half-hearted pushes that stop partway down the stroke never turn the engine fast enough to fire. And kicking again and again without pausing tires your leg and gives you no new information. If three or four proper kicks do not work, stop and think about what is different: fuel, the key, the neutral light or a flooded engine.

## Safety points

- **Wear closed shoes** and keep trouser legs clear of the lever and chain.
- **Keep the bike upright and stable.** Use the stand on level ground.
- **Never kick while riding.** Honda warns it can damage the engine.
- **Do not kick too hard.** A violent stroke can hurt your leg and damage the mechanism.
- **Do not wear loose bags or scarves** that can catch the lever.
- **Make sure the bike is in neutral.** If it is in gear when you kick, it may lurch forward.

If the bike is in gear and the engine starts, hold the brakes and shift to neutral at once. Our guide to [understanding neutral on a Honda Wave](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/understanding-neutral-on-a-honda-wave/) explains how to be sure.

## Hanoi practicalities

In a busy street, kicking a bike is awkward and draws a crowd, so choose a calm place. Pull over to a safe spot, put the bike on its stand and do the procedure carefully. If the bike will not start at a junction, move it to the side first. Our guide to [why a manual motorbike might stall at a junction](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/why-a-manual-motorbike-might-stall-at-a-junction/) explains what to do in traffic.

Heat and humidity do not stop a kick start, but a bike that has been left in direct sun or in rain may start less easily. Do not spray liquids into the engine or the starter. If a bike has been in deep water, do not try to start it repeatedly; call the shop.

## FAQ: the kick starter

### Do all rental bikes have a kick starter?

No. Many scooters have only an electric starter. Ask the shop whether your bike has a kick lever.

### Which gear should the bike be in when I kick-start it?

Neutral. Honda's manual says to shift to neutral so that the N indicator is lit.

### How hard should I kick?

Quick and decisive, in a continuous stroke from the top of the travel, but not violently.

### What do I do if the engine floods?

Turn the key off, open the throttle fully, kick several times, then repeat the start procedure, as Honda's manual describes.

### Can I kick-start while riding?

No. Honda warns that kicking while the bike is moving can damage the engine.

### Why does the bike need the kick starter at all?

It works without the battery turning the engine, so it is a backup if the battery is weak.
