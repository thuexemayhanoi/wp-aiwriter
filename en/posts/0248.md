To check the tyres on a rented electric moped, look for the manufacturer's pressure figure on the bike, check both tyres with a gauge while they are cold, look for cuts, bulges, cracks, nails and worn tread, and ask staff to deal with anything doubtful before you leave. Tyres are the scooter's only contact with the road, and an electric moped carries a battery pack as well as the rider, so low pressure or a damaged tyre is something you want to catch before the first ride. The numbers in this guide come from VinFast owner's manuals for two models, the Evo 200 and the Motio, plus a Feliz S user guide. They are examples, not the figures for the bike you will ride, so always use the label and the shop's answer for your own scooter.

## Why tyres deserve a minute of your handover

VinFast's manual for the Evo 200 puts it plainly: the tyre is the only part that touches the road, and driving on a tyre that does not meet the standard creates a risk of losing control. Electric mopeds are quiet, so there is no engine note to tell you something has changed, and a soft tyre may feel odd rather than obviously flat. The checks below take about two minutes and can be done at the shop with the bike on its stand.

For the wider handover routine see our [10-minute rental scooter inspection](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/rental-scooter-inspection-checklist/); this article goes deeper on tyres only.

## Step 1: find the specification

Every bike should have a tyre size and pressure on a label, in the manual or on the tyre itself. For comparison, the VinFast manuals list:

| Model | Tyre size | Front pressure | Rear pressure |
|---|---|---|---|
| VinFast Evo 200 | 90/90-12, rating 44J | 200 kPa | 225 kPa |
| VinFast Motio | 3.00-10, rating 42J | 200 kPa | 200 kPa |

A kilopascal is a metric unit of pressure: 200 kPa is 2.0 bar, or roughly 29 psi, and 225 kPa is about 2.25 bar or roughly 33 psi. Most hand pumps and garage gauges show bar or psi, so ask which unit the shop's gauge uses before you compare readings.

Two things to understand:

- **Front and rear can differ.** The Evo 200 manual gives a higher rear pressure than front, which is why you should not simply copy one number to both wheels.
- **The number depends on the load.** The Evo 200 manual says pressure should be set according to speed, the weight of rider, passenger, cargo and accessories. If you plan to carry a passenger or luggage, tell the shop and ask what pressure it recommends.

Our article on [reading a Honda Wave tyre size and pressure label](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-read-a-honda-wave-tyre-size-and-pressure-label/) explains how the size code and load or speed ratings such as "44J" are written, which applies to scooters as well as to manual bikes.

## Step 2: check the pressure properly

Looking at a tyre is not enough: a tyre on a hard surface can look round and be many kilopascals low.

1. **Check cold.** The Evo 200 manual says pressure must be checked and adjusted when the tyre is cold, meaning at the temperature of the surroundings. A scooter that has just come back from a long ride will read higher.
2. **Use a gauge,** not a thumb press. Ask staff to measure each tyre in front of you.
3. **Compare with the label** for that exact scooter.
4. **Check the valve.** The cap should be on, the valve should not hiss, and a drop of water on the valve should not bubble.
5. **Add air only with staff.** Overinflating is as unhelpful as underinflating.

If a tyre is clearly soft, ask for it to be inflated and re-checked before you ride. A tyre that goes soft again within a day has a slow leak and should be repaired. ## Step 3: tread depth and wear indicators

The Evo 200 manual gives a minimum tread depth of 0.8 mm for both the front and rear tyres, and tells the rider to check the wear indicators on the tyre. Its advice is to replace tyres immediately once the tread wears to the indicator. The Feliz S user guide gives the same minimum of 0.8 mm. Those figures are for those models, but the practice applies widely.

At the shop:

- **Find the wear indicators.** Small marks or arrows on the sidewall point to raised bars in the tread grooves. If the tread is level with the bars, the tyre is at its limit.
- **Look across the whole tread,** not just the centre. Wear on one edge can point to a pressure or alignment problem.
- **Look for flat spots or a smooth, shiny centre** on a tyre that has done many kilometres of straight riding.
- **Check both tyres.** A rental scooter may have a good front and a tired rear.

You do not need a depth gauge for a handover. If the tread looks nearly gone, ask for another bike or a new tyre. The VinFast warranty terms list tyres and brake parts among items that wear naturally, so worn tyres are normal for a well-used rental. They are still not safe to ride.

## Step 4: cuts, bulges, cracks and foreign objects

The Evo 200 manual lists what to look for before riding: sharp objects stuck in the tyre, cuts, tears, cracks, visible reinforcing cords and bulges in the sidewall. For a renter that becomes a short routine:

- **Sidewalls:** run your eyes (and fingers, carefully) around both sidewalls. A bulge or a blister is a reason to refuse the bike.
- **Tread surface:** look for nails, screws, glass or stones lodged in the rubber. Do not pull out a nail. The air may rush out, and a tyre with a nail in it should go to a mechanic.
- **Cracks:** fine cracking in the rubber and a flaking sidewall are signs of age. The manual notes that tyres age even when unused or lightly used, and that cracks in the tread and sidewall, sometimes with deformation of the carcass, should be judged by a specialist.
- **Cuts:** a cut that reaches the cords is not cosmetic.
- **Cross-grooves:** the manual's advice on tread groove lines also says to take the scooter to a service workshop for a new tyre if lines appear across the tread, or if sharp debris is stuck in it.

When in doubt, photograph it and ask. Our guide to [choosing tyres for a manual bike rental in wet weather](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/choosing-tyres-for-a-manual-bike-rental-in-wet-weather/) explains why tyre condition matters even more on wet roads.

## Step 5: rims, spokes and wheel bearings

Tyres sit on rims, so check these at the same time:

- **Rims:** the Evo 200 manual says to check the rim for cracks or deformation before each ride, and that a cracked or bent rim should be replaced, not repaired. A dent in an alloy rim or a visible crack is a reason to refuse the bike.
- **Wheel play:** hold the scooter steady and rock the wheel by hand. The Feliz S guide says that if the hub is loose or the wheel does not turn smoothly, the bearings need checking.
- **Wheel spin:** lift the wheel, if the bike is on its stand, and spin it. It should turn smoothly and quietly, without wobble or rubbing.
- **Brake rubbing:** if the wheel does not spin freely, check the brake before assuming a tyre problem.

## Step 6: pressure in the ride, not just in the shop

A moped that feels right in the shop can drift during the day.

- **Notice steering and cornering:** a vague, heavy feel often means a soft tyre.
- **Notice vibration:** it can point to a tyre or wheel fault.
- **Check again after a long ride,** when the tyres are warm only for a rough idea. Re-check cold if you need a real figure.
- **Avoid kerbs and potholes** at speed; hitting one can bruise a tyre or bend a rim.
- **Keep to the loads the shop gives you.** The manual warns against overloading the scooter.

## Reject or ask for another bike if

- A tyre has a bulge, a cut to the cords or a nail stuck in it.
- The tread is at or near the wear indicators.
- The pressure is far from the label and the shop cannot correct it.
- The rim is cracked, bent or dented.
- The wheel wobbles or has play at the hub.
- The tyres are cracked all over or visibly old.
- Staff cannot tell you the right pressure for the load.

## FAQ: electric moped tyres

### What pressure should my rental moped's tyres have?

Use the label or manual for your exact bike. The Evo 200 manual lists 200 kPa front and 225 kPa rear, but that is one model.

### Can I check pressure after riding?

Warm tyres read higher. The Evo 200 manual says to check cold, so test before the first ride of the day if you can.

### Are electric moped tyres tubeless?

The Feliz S user guide says that model has tubeless tyres. Ask the shop about yours, because it changes how a puncture is repaired.

### Is 0.8 mm the limit for every tyre?

It is the minimum the VinFast Evo 200 manual and Feliz S guide give for those models. Other bikes can differ, so ask.

### What if I carry a passenger?

Tell the shop. The manual says pressure should be adjusted for the rider, passenger and cargo, and the scooter must not be overloaded.
