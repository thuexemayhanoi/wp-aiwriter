On a rental electric scooter the luggage limit is set by three things at once: the weight rating on the vehicle, the size limits in Vietnamese road rules and the small amount of storage the bike really has. As a rule of thumb, everything must stay inside the outline of the bike, within the weight rating once rider, passenger and bags are added together, and out of the way of the lights, your feet and the handlebars. One VinFast model's manual rates the whole load at 130 kg and gives it 22 litres under the seat, but other models differ, so ask the shop for the figures for the bike you receive. This guide explains the limits, where bags can and cannot go and how to keep them safe.

## First, which kind of electric bike you have

Luggage rules depend on the class of the machine, and electric two-wheelers fall into more than one. A pedal-assisted electric bicycle is treated in law as a non-motorised vehicle, while a pedal-less electric scooter is a moped or a motorcycle. Our guide to [electric bike classes and rules in Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/electric-bikes-in-hanoi-rules/) explains how to tell them apart.

Nguyen Ha Motorbike Rental lists electric bicycles, 50cc-equivalent electric scooters and some VinFast electric motorbikes as possible rentals, and the bike actually available is confirmed when you book. Tell the shop what you plan to carry at that point, because a daypack and a suitcase call for different bikes.

## What Vietnamese road rules say about loads

Law 36/2024/QH15 on Road Traffic Order and Safety, in force since 1 January 2025, covers goods carried on motorcycles and mopeds in Article 33. I read the text on the national legal database, vbpl.vn. Three points matter to a visitor.

- **Width.** Goods must not extend beyond the width of the luggage rack by more than 0.3 m on each side.
- **Length.** Goods must not extend beyond the back of the rack by more than 0.5 m. The rule refers to the rack as designed by the manufacturer.
- **Height.** Goods must not rise more than 2 m above the road surface.

The same article stops riders from carrying bulky items and from loading goods beyond the stated limits. Under Decree 168/2024/NĐ-CP, a rider who carries bulky goods on a motorbike can be fined VND 600,000 to 800,000, and a much heavier fine and loss of licence points apply if it causes an accident, as the broadcaster VOV explained in April 2025.

Most electric scooters have no luggage rack at all, so there is no rack to measure from. The practical conclusion is simple: carry nothing that sticks out past the body, and do not buy a large item at a market and balance it on the footboard.

### E-bicycles have different limits

For pedal-assisted electric bicycles, Article 31 of the same law applies. Goods on these vehicles must be safe, must not obstruct traffic or block the rider's view, and must not exceed one third of the vehicle's length, 1 m ahead or behind, or 0.4 m beyond each side of the wheels. An electric bicycle also may not carry bulky loads.

## The weight rating is the number most people miss

Every vehicle has a maximum load, and it includes the people. VinFast's Evo 200 owner's manual, one model of electric scooter, states a permitted load of 130 kg including rider, passenger and everything attached, and a total vehicle mass of 230 kg. It also says the structure is designed for one rider and one passenger and that goods must not exceed the vehicle's load rating.

This is the Evo 200, not necessarily the bike you will rent, so treat it as an example of how the rating works. It is also a reminder that the figure is shared. Add your own weight, a passenger's weight, a daypack and a bag of shopping, and a bike with a 130 kg rating can be used up quickly.

The manual gives a second reason to respect the limit: it says that using the vehicle above its permitted load can swell the battery and shorten its life. That is a rental-bike cost as well as a safety issue.

### A simple way to check

1. Ask the shop for the permitted load of the exact bike.
2. Add the weight of everyone on the bike, then bags, then helmets that will be carried.
3. If the total is close to the limit, choose a lighter load, a second bike or a different type of bike.
4. Remember that heavy loads behind the rider make a light bike feel less steady, so keep heavy items low and close to the seat.

## Where a bag can and cannot go

| Place | Usually fine? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Under-seat compartment | Yes, within its size | Shared with the charger on some models |
| Backpack on the rider | Yes, if light | Heavy packs tire the shoulders and shift your balance |
| Hook near the handlebars | Light items only | A hook is built for a small bag, not a heavy load |
| Footboard | No | Blocks your feet, can jam the controls |
| Handlebars | No | Changes steering and can cover the lights |
| Between rider and passenger | No | Takes seat space and pushes people about |
| Hanging from the helmet hook | Not while riding | VinFast's manual says not to ride with a helmet hanging on the bike |

The Evo 200 manual also warns that raincoats and bulky goods must not cover the vehicle's signal lights. This holds for any electric scooter: if a bag hides an indicator or the brake light, it is in the wrong place.

## What shares the under-seat space

An electric scooter's under-seat compartment is not always empty. On the Evo 200, the manual says the compartment holds the charging port and the battery switch. It adds that you should not put hot, wet or sharp objects in it, that the charger should be wrapped in shock-absorbing material if it is stored there and that the charger must not be left in the compartment while it is charging because it can overheat.

VinFast states the compartment volume of that model as 22 litres, and its Feliz S page gives 25 litres. Those are the manufacturer's figures for those models. A shop's electric scooter may have another, and the space may be reduced by a battery or the charger. Ask the shop where the charger lives and whether the compartment is dry.

## Storing luggage safely when you park

The VinFast manual advises against keeping valuables in the under-seat compartment, so treat it as shelter, not a safe. A few habits protect your things:

- **Take valuables with you.** Passport, phone, wallet, camera and laptop travel with you rather than staying in the seat.
- **Pack the compartment to protect the vehicle.** Nothing wet, hot or sharp, and nothing that could scrape the charger or its lead.
- **Lock the steering and use guarded parking.** Nguyen Ha does not provide an extra lock, so the usual advice is to lock the steering and park in guarded places. Our guide to [parking a motorbike in Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/parking-a-motorbike-in-hanoi/) and the one on [where to park near Hoan Kiem Lake](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/where-to-park-a-scooter-near-hoan-kiem-lake/) explain how it works.
- **Do not leave bags on the seat or hooked on display.** A visible bag invites trouble.
- **Keep the keys separate** from the bag, so one loss does not become two.

## Carrying a passenger and luggage together

Two people and bags use the rating faster than one person with bags. Our guide to [carrying a passenger and luggage on a Honda Lead or Vision](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/scooter-for-two-riders-and-luggage-hanoi/) compares petrol scooters, and the same logic applies to electric ones, with the added issue that the electric bike's load rating is often lower and battery range falls with weight. If you need to carry two people and luggage, a petrol scooter may be the simpler choice.

## Rain, heat and luggage

Hanoi's weather changes how you pack. In a downpour, anything outside the compartment gets wet, and the manual warns that rain gear and bulky items must not hide the lights, so keep a poncho folded under the seat rather than draped over the back. In summer heat, keep drinks, electronics and anything that melts out of direct sun. Our guide to [riding in Hanoi rain](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/riding-in-hanoi-rain/) covers the wet season.

## A quick loading check before you leave the shop

1. Find the load rating and compartment size from the shop.
2. Weigh up the total, including everyone on the bike.
3. Put heavy items low and central, light items on top.
4. Keep every light, mirror and indicator uncovered.
5. Check that the seat closes fully without pressing on the charger or its cable.
6. Stand with the bike and shake it gently to check nothing shifts.
7. Ride a short distance, then stop and re-check.

## FAQ: luggage on a rental electric bike

### How much luggage can an electric scooter carry?

It depends on the model's load rating, which includes riders. One VinFast model is rated at 130 kg in total. Ask the shop for the figure for your bike.

### Can I carry a suitcase on an electric scooter?

A large suitcase that sticks out of the body, sits on the footboard or blocks your legs is not a safe load and may count as a bulky one under the road rules. Smaller bags inside the outline of the bike are the sensible choice.

### Can I hang a bag from the handlebars?

Only a light bag on a purpose-built hook. Bags on the handlebars can affect steering and cover the lights.

### Is the under-seat compartment safe for valuables?

The manufacturer advises against leaving valuables there. Keep passports, money and electronics with you.

### Does carrying a passenger reduce how much luggage I can take?

Yes, because the load rating includes the passenger.

### Are the limits different for an electric bicycle?

Yes. Article 31 of the road law has its own limits for goods on electric bicycles, and e-bicycles cannot carry bulky loads.
