A small manual motorbike can carry two people legally and safely only if the pair fits within the bike's load limit, can sit without being crammed and can handle the trip's length and roads. The popular rental models, the Honda Wave, Honda Blade and Yamaha Sirius, are all compact 110 cc underbones with a similar frame, so the question is less which badge to choose than whether this size of bike suits you both. This guide gives the dimensions and load figures the manufacturers publish, shows how to test the fit at the shop, explains what changes for the passenger's comfort and the rider's control, and says when a bigger scooter is a better answer. For riding technique with a passenger, see our guide to [riding a manual motorbike with a passenger in Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/riding-a-manual-motorbike-with-a-passenger-in-hanoi/). This article is about choosing the bike.

## The legal baseline

Article 33 of the Law on Road Traffic Order and Safety (Law No. 36/2024/QH15), in force since 1 January 2025, allows a rider of a two-wheeled motorbike to carry only one person, with narrow exceptions such as a child under 12. Both must wear a helmet that meets the national standard, with the strap fastened. Under Decree 168/2024/ND-CP, carrying two passengers outside the exceptions is fined VND 400,000–600,000 as of October 2026, and carrying three or more is fined VND 600,000–800,000. So the question for two adults is always about one rider and one passenger.

The three common models are close in size. These figures come from Honda Vietnam's and Yamaha Motor Vietnam's pages, checked in October 2026:

| Wheelbase | 1,224 mm | 1,227 mm | 1,217 mm | 1,235 mm |

| Weight | 96 kg | 98 kg | 98–99 kg | 98 kg |
| Fuel tank | 3.7 litres | 4.0 litres | 3.7 litres | 4.0 litres |

The differences are a few centimetres, not a class apart. The Sirius is the longest, with the longest wheelbase. A slightly longer wheelbase usually means a little more room between rider and passenger and a steadier ride, but with a few millimetres' difference you will not feel it. The published figures say nothing about seat length, padding or footrest position, which matter more for a passenger. The only way to judge those is to sit on the bike.

## The load limit comes first

Honda's owner's manuals for the Wave give a maximum load that covers the rider, passenger and all luggage: 115 kg for some versions and 155 kg for others, and they state that the bike carries the rider and one passenger. Two adults of ordinary build can pass 115 kg before a single bag is added, so ask the shop which figure applies to the bike you are offered. Our guide to [how much luggage you can carry on a Honda Wave](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-much-luggage-can-you-safely-carry-on-a-honda-wave/) shows how to do the weight sum. We did not retrieve load limits for the Blade or Sirius, so ask for them too.

A load near the limit is not unsafe by itself, but it uses up the bike's margin. Acceleration, braking distance and hill climbing all suffer. If the sum comes out over the limit, choose a different bike or a different plan.

## Tyre pressure for two

A passenger changes the rear tyre pressure. Honda's Wave RSX FI manual gives these cold pressures: front 200 kPa for both rider alone and rider with passenger; rear 225 kPa for the rider alone and 280 kPa for rider and passenger. These are for that model, so ask the shop for the figures for your bike and ask them to check the rear tyre before you leave. A rear tyre at solo pressure under two adults squirms and heats up, and it makes the bike feel vague. Our guide to [choosing tyres for a manual bike rental in wet weather](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/choosing-tyres-for-a-manual-bike-rental-in-wet-weather/) explains what else to check.

## The two-up fit test at the shop

Before you accept the bike, do this together, with the shop's staff beside you:

1. **Rider sits first** in the riding position, hands on the grips, feet on the footrests, back straight. Check you can reach the gear lever and the rear brake pedal comfortably.

2. **Passenger sits behind** with feet on the passenger footrests. The seat should give each of you a distinct place. If the rider is pushed forward onto the tank or the passenger is perched on the very back edge, the bike is too small for the pair.

3. **Check the grab rail or rack.** The passenger should have something solid to hold. If there is none, they must hold the rider's waist, which tires both of you on a long ride.

4. **Check the passenger footrests.** Both should be present, firm and at a height that does not force the knees up high.

5. **Rock the bike gently** on its stand. It should feel stable under the two of you.

6. **Look at the rear suspension.** With two people aboard it should sag a little but not bottom out. Ask staff how the shocks are.

7. **Check the mirrors and lights** with both aboard. A passenger can block a mirror, as our guide to [adjusting mirrors on a Honda Wave rental](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-adjust-mirrors-on-a-honda-wave-rental/) explains.

8. **Take a short test ride** with the passenger on, in a quiet place, and notice acceleration, braking and steering.

If either of you is uncomfortable now, it will be worse after an hour. Ask for another bike.

## What changes for the rider

With a passenger, the rider has to manage a heavier, less responsive bike. Starts are slower, braking distances longer and hills harder. Honda's manuals warn that overloading affects handling, braking and stability. The rider should also expect:

- **Less feel in the steering,** because weight over the rear wheel lightens the front.
- **More effort to hold the bike upright at a stop,** especially on a slope or camber.
- **A need for earlier, smoother braking.** Honda's braking sequence is to release the throttle, use both brakes together with the front stronger and keep the steering straight.
- **More careful parking,** since the bike is harder to manoeuvre with a passenger just getting off.

## What changes for the passenger

A passenger on a small bike has little to hold, no backrest and a short seat. Over an hour or two, comfort depends on:

- **A place to hold,** such as a grab rail, strap or the rider's waist.
- **Footrest height,** since high footrests push the knees up.
- **Seat padding and shape.** A flat, thin seat is tiring on a long ride.
- **Wind and heat,** because the passenger has no windshield or faring.
- **Their own balance,** since a passenger who leans or moves suddenly upsets the bike.

Agree signals before you go, and plan stops. A passenger who is uncomfortable tends to shift and lean, which affects control. ## Trip length and road type

The longer or rougher the trip, the more the size matters. For a short city ride of a few kilometres, a small manual bike is fine for two. For a day trip of 100 km or more, or a hilly road, a small bike with two adults is working hard. Our guide to [Hanoi to Ninh Binh by motorbike](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/hanoi-to-ninh-binh-by-motorbike/) notes that a scooter with a bigger engine is more comfortable at highway speeds, especially two-up, and our guide to [riding a semi-automatic bike on steep mountain roads](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/manual-bikes-and-steep-mountain-roads-when-to-choose-another-vehicle/) explains when to choose another vehicle.

## When to choose a bigger or different bike

Consider an automatic scooter with a larger engine and a flat floor if:

- the load sum is near or over the limit;
- either of you is tall or heavy;
- you plan a long day or a hilly route;
- the passenger is inexperienced or nervous;
- you have a lot of luggage.

## FAQ: renting a manual bike for two

### Can a Honda Wave carry two adults?

Legally yes, one rider and one passenger. Check the bike's load limit, which Honda's manuals give as 115 kg or 155 kg depending on version.

### Which small manual bike is biggest?

The Yamaha Sirius FI is the longest of the common models, but the differences are a few centimetres. Sit on the bike to judge.

### What tyre pressure do I need with a passenger?

For the Honda Wave RSX FI, Honda gives 280 kPa at the rear for rider and passenger, against 225 kPa for the rider alone. Ask the shop for your bike's figures.

### Is a small manual bike comfortable for two?

For short trips, yes. For long or hilly trips it can be tiring and underpowered. Do the two-up fit test.

### What is the fine for carrying too many people?

As of October 2026, carrying two passengers outside the exceptions is fined VND 400,000–600,000 under Decree 168/2024/ND-CP.

### When should we choose a different bike?

When the load is near the limit, the trip is long or hilly, or either of you is uncomfortable on the test.
