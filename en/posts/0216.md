A deposit on a 50cc rental is a refundable cash security held by the shop against things that go wrong during the rental: damage beyond normal wear, missing parts or keys, and charges that are left unpaid. It is not rent, and it is not a fee. At Nguyen Ha the cash deposit is set by the bike and the contract, between VND 2,000,000 and VND 5,000,000, it is separate from the rental price, and this is not a no-deposit service. What exactly the deposit may be used for is written in the contract, so the practical skill is knowing which questions to ask before you sign. This guide sets out what such a deposit usually covers, what it should not cover, how Vietnamese civil law describes a deposit, and how to document the bike so that you get your money back in full.

## What the deposit is, and what it is not

The deposit is a sum of cash you hand over when you collect the bike. It is not part of the price per day, week or month. The prices for 50cc bikes are on the [prices page](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/prices/), and the deposit is on top of them. The shop sets the deposit according to the bike and the contract, which is why the figure falls anywhere between VND 2,000,000 and VND 5,000,000 and is confirmed on the actual bike before you sign.

The shop checks and photographs your identity documents and licence for the contract, but it does not keep the originals. That detail matters, because some travellers worry that a deposit means leaving a passport behind. At this shop it does not.

## Why the amount can differ between bikes

A Cub 50cc and a 50cc scooter are not the same. A Cub has a foot gear pedal, a kick lever on many models and a rack. A scooter has more plastic bodywork and an under-seat box. The cost of replacing a cracked panel or a bent lever differs between them, and so does the age and value of the individual bike. The shop's own rule is that the amount depends on the bike and the contract. A sensible question is: "Why this amount for this bike?" You are not being rude. You are checking that the figure fits what is on the contract.

## What a deposit usually covers

### Damage beyond normal wear

This is the main reason for a deposit. If the bike comes back with a cracked leg shield, a bent brake lever, a snapped mirror or a torn seat that was not there at pickup, the shop may use the deposit towards the repair. The key words are "was not there at pickup". That is why you photograph the bike before you ride, as our guide to the [10-minute rental scooter inspection](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/rental-scooter-inspection-checklist/) explains.

### Missing items

Keys, a mirror, the helmet that came with the bike, a seat lock or a rack fitting that disappears during the rental are replaced at the renter's cost under most contracts. Ask whether there is a spare key and what happens if you lose the main one.

### Unpaid charges

The shop's published rules include a late return charge of VND 30,000 per hour for 1 to 5 hours, a full extra day from 6 hours, and a charge of VND 20,000 if a petrol bike is returned with an empty tank. Whether such charges are paid at the counter or taken from the deposit is a question for the shop and the contract. Ask it before you sign, and ask for the answer in plain words.

### Other costs named in the contract

Contracts sometimes mention costs that arise during the rental, such as a penalty notice sent to the bike's owner. Not every contract does, and the wording differs, so read that clause rather than assume. Our guide to [what the clauses in a Hanoi rental contract mean](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/hanoi-motorbike-rental-contract-explained/) walks through the typical headings.

## What a deposit should not cover

A deposit should be tied to the renter's responsibility. Reasonable expectations, which you should check against the contract, are these:

- **Normal wear.** Faint scuffs at touch points, light marks on the footboard and ageing of rubber parts on an older bike are a cost of running a rental fleet. Our guide to [normal wear on a rented bike](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/what-counts-as-normal-wear-on-a-rented-honda-wave/) explains the line between wear and damage.
- **Damage that was already there.** If your pickup photos show a crack, it is not your damage.
- **Ordinary mechanical failure.** A battery that dies of age or a worn cable is not the same as damage from a drop. Responsibility for repairs is something to clarify in the contract.
- **Your own medical costs or other people's property.** A deposit does not substitute for travel insurance. Our guide to [travel insurance and motorbike rentals](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/travel-insurance-motorbike-rental-vietnam/) explains what a policy usually leaves out.

## How Vietnamese civil law describes a deposit

Vietnam's Civil Code 2015 deals with deposits in Article 328. In that article, a deposit is a sum of money or valuables that one party hands to another for a set period to secure the making or the performance of a contract. According to the Ministry of Justice's legal education page, thuvienphapluat.vn and a summary by the law firm DB Legal, if the contract is made and performed, the deposit is returned to the person who paid it or counted against what they owe. If the depositor refuses to make or perform the contract, the deposit goes to the other party.

For you, the point is not to quote the article at the shop. It is that a deposit is meant to be returned or offset when the contract is properly performed, and that the contract wording decides the details. As of October 2026 this is general information, not legal advice, and a rental shop's deposit may be framed differently from the deposits discussed in the Code, so read your own contract.

## What to do at pickup so the deposit comes back

1. **Photograph the whole bike** from the front, back and both sides, with close-ups of existing marks.
2. **Photograph the levers, mirrors, seat, rack and tyres.** Small parts are where disputes begin.
3. **Film a short walk-round** with the date visible, if you can.
4. **Ask for the handover to be written down** if the shop's form has a damage diagram.
5. **Confirm the deposit amount** and the way it is returned, in writing if you can, and keep the receipt.
6. **Ask about charges:** what is paid at the counter and what may be taken from the deposit.

## What to do at return so it comes back in full

Return the bike on time with a little fuel in the tank, the key, the helmet and nothing missing. Walk round the bike with a staff member and compare it with your photographs. If something has changed, say so immediately. If a deduction is proposed, ask what exactly is being repaired, how the figure was reached and whether a receipt is available. Agree on the timing for the rest of the deposit. The shop is open 09:00 to 21:00 daily, and you can ring 0334 699 969 (+84 334 699 969) if there is a question after you leave.

## Questions worth asking about the deposit

- How much is the deposit for this exact bike, and why?
- In what form is it returned, and how long does that take?
- What costs may be taken from it?
- Are late return and empty-tank charges taken from it or paid separately?
- What happens if I lose the key or helmet?
- What if I return the bike early or extend the rental?
- Who decides whether damage is normal wear, and what happens if we disagree?

## FAQ: 50cc rental deposits

### Is the deposit the same as the rent?

No. The deposit is a separate cash security. The rent is what you pay for the days of use.

### Does the shop keep my passport as the deposit?

No. At Nguyen Ha, identity documents and licence are checked and photographed for the contract, and originals are not kept.

### How much is the deposit for a 50cc bike?

It is between VND 2,000,000 and VND 5,000,000, depending on the bike and the contract, and the final figure is confirmed on the actual bike before you sign.

### Can the shop take money for scratches I did not cause?

It should not. Compare the bike with your pickup photographs and ask the shop to show where the damage is on the handover record.

### Will I get the deposit back if I return the bike early?

Early return does not refund unused rent, but the deposit is a separate matter. Ask how it is handled in your contract.

### Is a deposit the same as insurance?

No. It is a security held by the shop. It does not pay for your medical costs or other people's property.
