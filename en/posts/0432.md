If the rental shop looks closed when you arrive, do not wander off or accept help from a stranger. First check the time against the shop's hours, which are 09:00 to 21:00 every day for Nguyen Ha, and check that you are at the right address, then call and message the shop with your map pin and wait somewhere safe while you do. If you cannot reach anyone, find a place to stay nearby and collect the bike the next morning rather than taking a bike from someone who offers to help. Most closed-door problems have simple causes: you are outside opening hours, you are at the wrong alley or the staff are out for a short time and have not seen your message.

This guide sorts the possible situations with a table by arrival time, gives a step-by-step routine, explains how to handle a late arrival after the shop has closed, warns about people who redirect new arrivals, covers what it means for your booking and your deposit and shows how to avoid the problem. It builds on our guide to [how to check the rental shop's opening hours and pickup location](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-check-the-rental-shops-opening-hours-and-pickup-location/).

## First, what Nguyen Ha publishes

These are the published facts as of October 2026.

- **Hours:** 09:00 to 21:00 every day of the week.

- **Address:** Alley 5, Nguyen Van Cu Street, Bo De Ward, Long Bien District, Hanoi, near Gia Lam bus station.

- **Contact:** phone and Zalo 0334 699 969, WhatsApp +84 334 699 969.

- **Pickup:** daily and short rentals are collected from and returned to the shop in Long Bien. There is no separate pickup counter at the bus station or elsewhere in Gia Lam.

- **Before you travel:** the shop asks renters to get in touch first so that the bike, the price for your dates and the deposit can be confirmed.

The published terms do not describe an out-of-hours pickup service. So if you arrive outside 09:00 to 21:00, do not expect staff to be there unless you have arranged it in advance.

## A table by arrival time

| When you arrive | Likely situation | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Before 09:00 | The shop has not opened | Wait or have breakfast nearby, and call at 09:00 |
| 09:00 to 21:00, door shut | Staff out briefly, wrong alley, or a message missed | Call and message; check the address; wait safely |
| 09:00 to 21:00, shop open but no one answers | Staff busy or helping others | Wait a few minutes, call again |
| After 21:00 | The shop has closed for the day | Find a place to stay, and collect the bike in the morning |
| A public holiday or special day | Hours may differ | Call; check the shop's own channels |

If you are not sure whether your phone shows Vietnam time, remember that Vietnam is seven hours ahead of UTC all year and does not change its clocks. Check it against a trusted source.

## Step-by-step routine

1. **Stop and stay safe.** Move out of the traffic lane. Do not leave your luggage unattended.

2. **Check the time.** Is it inside 09:00 to 21:00 in Hanoi?

3. **Check the address.** Is this Alley 5 on Nguyen Van Cu Street in Bo De Ward, Long Bien? Several alleys on a street can look alike, and a search for "Nguyen Van Cu" alone can lead to another street or city. Look at the number on the wall or gate, and compare your map pin.

4. **Call the shop** on 0334 699 969 (+84 334 699 969), and say you are at the address.

5. **Send your pin and a photo of where you are,** by WhatsApp or Zalo, with the nearest landmark.

6. **Wait somewhere safe and lit,** such as a cafe or a busy shopfront, if staff say they are coming.

7. **Try again** after ten or fifteen minutes, and check your messages.

8. **If you cannot reach anyone,** go to the fallback plan below.

## What not to do

- **Do not hand money to anyone** you cannot connect to the shop you booked.

- **Do not accept a bike from a stranger** who says the shop is closed or has moved and offers "the same price" elsewhere.

- **Do not follow a person** who offers to lead you to the shop or to another shop.

- **Do not leave documents** with anyone but the shop's staff, and do not give your passport as a deposit anywhere. Our guide to [how to keep your passport safe during a scooter rental](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-keep-your-passport-safe-during-a-scooter-rental/) explains why.

- **Do not assume** the business has closed for good because the door is shut.

## Watch out for people who redirect you

Transport hubs attract people who offer help to new arrivals, and most are simply being friendly. But a visitor who has just arrived with luggage is easy to redirect to another business, so confirm before you follow anyone who offers to "take you to the shop" or says that it is closed. Nguyen Ha's own pages say not to confuse the Gia Lam bus station with a Nguyen Ha branch, because there is no separate counter in Gia Lam. If someone tells you the shop has moved, confirm it by calling the number on the shop's own website, not a number the person gives you. Our guide to [how to book a Hanoi motorbike rental on WhatsApp](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-book-a-hanoi-motorbike-rental-on-whatsapp/) explains how to spot a fake contact.

## If you cannot reach the shop at all

Sometimes the phone is not answered, perhaps because staff are on the road or the line is busy. The sensible fallback is to arrange somewhere to stay nearby and return in the morning.

1. **Send a clear message** now, so there is a record: "I am at Alley 5 Nguyen Van Cu at [time]. The shop is closed. I will come back tomorrow at [time] unless you tell me otherwise."

2. **Find a hotel or guesthouse nearby,** or head back into central Hanoi by taxi or ride-hailing app. From the Old Quarter, the shop is across the Red River by the Chuong Duong bridge, about ten to twenty minutes by taxi or motorbike depending on traffic, according to the shop's contact page.

3. **Keep your belongings with you.**

4. **Return during opening hours,** and call before you set out.

You are not in a worse position by waiting a night. A rental day is 24 hours from the moment you take the bike, as our guide to [how a 24-hour rental day is counted in Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-a-24-hour-rental-day-is-counted-in-hanoi/) explains, so a delay in collecting does not use up rental time you have not yet started.

## If you arrive after 21:00

This happens to people who travel on late buses or flights. The shop has closed for the day, and its published terms do not offer an out-of-hours handover.

- **Do not wait at the door.** Nobody is coming unless you arranged it.

- **Plan the night:** a nearby hotel, or return to your own accommodation.

- **Message the shop** that you will collect in the morning, and ask for a time.

- **Next time, arrange in advance.** If you know you will arrive late, ask the shop before you travel whether any out-of-hours arrangement is possible, and get the answer in writing. Do not assume one exists.

Our guide to [planning an early morning departure with shop opening hours](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/planning-an-early-morning-departure-with-shop-opening-hours/) explains how to work with the hours when you want an early start.

## If you have paid or booked in advance

What the closed door means for your booking depends on what you have agreed.

- **If you only messaged and have paid nothing,** you can simply collect the next day. At Nguyen Ha the deposit and rent are normally paid when the contract is signed at the shop.

- **If you paid something in advance,** keep the receipt or the message, and ask the shop in writing how it will be applied to the new pickup time.

- **If the shop confirmed a time and was not there,** send a message with your arrival time, your location and a photograph of the shop front, and ask what it proposes. Keep the reply.

- **If you want to cancel,** ask about refunds in writing. Nguyen Ha's published terms do not describe a booking-fee policy, so ask rather than assume.

The Civil Code (Law No. 91/2015/QH13), in force since 1 January 2017, says a lessor must hand over the property at the agreed time and place, and if it is late, the lessee may extend the time or cancel and claim damages (Article 476). We read the lease articles in the text published by the Tuyen Quang Department of Justice. As of October 2026 that is the general rule, and your contract and the facts decide how it applies. This is general information, not legal advice.

## How to avoid the problem

- **Confirm the date, the time and the address in writing** before you travel.

- **Test the map pin** at home, and save it offline. Our guide to [how to check the rental shop's opening hours and pickup location](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-check-the-rental-shops-opening-hours-and-pickup-location/) explains how.

- **Arrive well before 21:00,** with time to spare for traffic.

- **Message when you are on your way,** and again when you are five minutes from the shop.

- **Keep a charged phone,** with data or a SIM, and the shop's number saved.

- **Ask about holidays** and any change of hours.

## FAQ: the shop is closed when I arrive

### What are the shop's opening hours?

09:00 to 21:00 every day, as published by Nguyen Ha as of October 2026.

### What if I arrive after 21:00?

The shop is closed and its published terms do not offer an out-of-hours handover. Find somewhere to stay, and collect the bike in the morning, or arrange something in advance.

### Does my rental day start when I arrive?

No. A rental day runs for 24 hours from the moment you take the bike, so a closed door does not use up rental time.

### Someone says the shop has moved. Should I follow them?

No. Confirm by calling the shop's own number, not one the person gives you.

### Is there a pickup counter at the Gia Lam bus station?

No. Nguyen Ha's pages say there is no separate pickup counter in Gia Lam, and that the shop is in Long Bien, near the bus station.

### What if I paid in advance?

Keep the receipt or message, and ask the shop in writing how the payment will be applied or refunded.
