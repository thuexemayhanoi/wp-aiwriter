There is no single stable list of one-way streets around Hoan Kiem that a rider can memorise. Directions in the Old Quarter and around the lake are set by the city and have been adjusted by trials, by the 2026 low-emission pilot and by temporary event closures, so the street sign in front of you outranks any map, blog or navigation app. What does stay constant is the rulebook. Under Law 36/2024/QH15, in force since 1 January 2025, a rider may not make a U-turn or reverse on a one-way road, and may not stop or park on its left side. This guide explains those rules, shows which named streets the city uses to define the central zones (useful as orientation landmarks), and gives a calm method for planning an approach so that you never need to turn back against the flow. It is general information as of October 2026, not a street-by-street direction chart.

## What the law says about one-way roads

The national road traffic law, Law 36/2024/QH15, sets out several rules that bite on one-way streets. The text on the government's policy portal contains the following provisions, which we summarise here in plain English.

| Rule | Where it sits | What it means on a scooter |
|---|---|---|
| Road signs, markings and signals must be obeyed, in a set order of priority | Article 11 | An officer's commands rank first, then traffic lights, then signs, then road markings |
| No U-turn on a one-way road, unless an officer or a temporary sign allows it | Article 15, clause 4 | Do not turn round in the street if you have missed your turn |
| No reversing on a one-way road | Article 16, clause 2 | Do not back up to correct a mistake |
| No stopping or parking on the left side of a one-way road | Article 18, clause 4 | Park only on the right-hand side of the direction you travel |
| On city streets, stop or park beside the kerb on the right, with the nearest wheel no more than 0.25 metres from it | Article 18, clause 6 | Pull in tight to the kerb, never out in the lane |
| No stopping or parking within five metres of a junction | Article 18, clause 4 | Corners are poor places to pull in |
| Failing to obey an on-duty officer's commands is prohibited | Article 9, clause 4 | If an officer redirects you, comply |

Riding the wrong way down a one-way street is a violation with penalties set in separate regulations. We do not quote amounts here because they depend on the decree in force on the day. ## Why directions around the lake keep changing

Several verified examples show why a memorised list goes out of date.

- **A one-way trial on two Old Quarter streets in 2025.** Hanoi announced a pilot from 11 June 2025 to the end of November 2025 that organised traffic on Hang Khoai and Cao Thang, in Hoan Kiem, as one-way streets. A trial of this kind can be extended, altered or dropped.
- **Large-vehicle limits from March 2025.** A separate pilot restricted coaches and cars with more than 16 seats on a ring of streets, including Dinh Tien Hoang, Hang Dao, Hang Ngang, Hang Duong, Dong Xuan and Hang Giay, during morning and evening peak periods. It did not target scooters, but it shows that the city adjusts the street plan in this area on a rolling basis.
- **The low-emission pilot from 1 July 2026.** Hanoi's Department of Construction adjusted signage and traffic diversion for the pilot, and arranged four passenger transfer points. We describe these below.
- **Event closures.** For a security-forces anniversary in July 2026 the city announced that all vehicles would be banned on Dinh Tien Hoang between Tran Nguyen Han and Dong Kinh Nghia Thuc Square from 20:00 to 23:00 on 9 July, with other restrictions on larger vehicles across 29 streets.
- **Flexibility by officers.** When the lake weekend walking space started in 2016, the traffic police chief said that officers could make some streets that are normally one-way run in both directions to relieve congestion. That is a reminder that police on the spot can change the flow in a way no map shows.

The lesson is not that the area is hopeless. It is that the sign, the officer and the barrier you can see are the authority.

## Landmarks that define the central zones

We could not verify an official, current direction for each street in the area, so rather than guess we use the street names that the city itself published for the low-emission pilot. They give you a mental frame for the grid.

| Area | Streets named in the pilot plan | Why it helps a rider |
|---|---|---|
| Zone 1, the core | Trang Tien, Hang Khay, Le Thai To, Hang Dao, Hang Ngang, Hang Buom, Ma May, Hang Bac, Hang Mam, Nguyen Huu Huan, Ly Thai To | Inside this perimeter all motorbikes, motorcycles and cars are barred from 19:00 to midnight on Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays |
| Zone 2, the buffer | Trang Thi, Phung Hung, Hang Dau, Tran Nhat Duat, Tran Quang Khai, Trang Tien, Hang Khay | Wider streets round the Old Quarter and lake; in the pilot phase the limits fall on large coaches and cars in peak hours |
| Passenger transfer points | Ba Trieu (Hang Khay to Hai Ba Trung), Tran Nhat Duat (Cao Thang to O Quan Chuong), Tran Quang Khai (Hang Mam to Hang Thung), Tran Khanh Du (in front of the History Museum) | Places to drop off or pick up a passenger, not long-stay parking |

The pilot began on 1 July 2026 and is planned to run to the end of 2026, according to the government news portal. Use the table as a map of where the rules change, not as a list of directions.

## Reading signs and markings in the dense grid

Because the signs rule, learn to read them before you reach the Old Quarter. As general guidance: a red circle with a white horizontal bar means no entry for vehicles from that direction, and a blue sign with an arrow marks the permitted direction of travel. Where signs and road markings disagree, the law's order of priority puts signs above markings, and an officer's hand signal above both.

A few habits help:

- **Look at the first vehicle at the mouth of the street.** If you cannot see a sign, watch the traffic. If every vehicle in a street is travelling away from you, the street is probably one-way against you. This is a cue, not proof, because local riders sometimes ride the wrong way.
- **Check both ends of a block.** Signs often stand at the start of a street, so a sign you passed may have applied to the next block.
- **Do not copy local riders.** The fact that other motorbikes are doing something is not evidence that it is allowed. Our guide to [crossing Hanoi intersections](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/crossing-hanoi-intersections/) explains how junction flow works and how to merge.
- **Use lanes correctly.** The [lane rules guide](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/lane-rules-motorbikes-vietnam/) covers where scooters belong on multi-lane roads.

## Planning an approach so you never need to turn back

The safest way to handle one-way streets is to avoid needing a correction in the first place.

1. **Pick a parking place first, then the street.** Look for an attended lot on the outer ring of the area. Our guide to [where to park a scooter near Hoan Kiem Lake](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/where-to-park-a-scooter-near-hoan-kiem-lake/) explains how guarded lots work.
2. **Approach along the outer streets and turn in late.** Wider streets such as those at the edges of Zone 2 carry more regular traffic and clearer signs.
3. **If you miss a turn, keep going.** Take the next legal turn and loop round the block. Circling is slower than a U-turn but it is the only option that is lawful on a one-way road.
4. **Arrive so that the kerb is on your right.** This lets you stop in the legal position without crossing the flow.
5. **Leave on foot for the last stretch.** For a lake visit it is usually quicker to walk from the outer ring than to thread the Old Quarter on a bike. Our [Old Quarter riding guide](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/riding-in-hanoi-old-quarter/) covers when to walk instead.

## Weekend evenings: when the pattern stops mattering

From 1 July 2026 the core zone is closed to motorbikes, motorcycles and cars from 19:00 to midnight every Friday, Saturday and Sunday, except for military and public-security vehicles, according to the government news portal and VOV. During those hours the one-way pattern is irrelevant because you should not be inside the perimeter at all. If you are on a street in the core at 18:50 on a Friday, plan to leave before 19:00 or park and walk.

Outside those hours the normal street pattern applies, subject to signs and officers. For the specific question of where to leave the bike near the lake, use our parking guide above rather than looking for a spot inside the closed streets.

## Navigation apps and one-way streets

Navigation apps are useful for orientation but they lag behind trials, temporary closures and signs. They can also route a scooter along a street that has just become one-way, or through a closed section. If the route and the sign disagree, believe the sign, pull over where it is legal, and choose another route.

## If you realise you are going the wrong way

Stay calm and do not make a sudden move. Law 36/2024/QH15 forbids reversing and U-turns on one-way roads, so the usual instincts are the wrong ones. Slow down, keep to the edge, watch oncoming traffic and look for the nearest safe gap. If an officer is present, follow the instruction. ## FAQ: one-way streets around Hoan Kiem

### Is there an official list of one-way streets I can download?

We could not verify a single current list. City decisions, trials and events change the arrangements, so rely on the signs on the day.

### May I do a U-turn on a one-way street?

No. Law 36/2024/QH15, Article 15, bars U-turns on one-way roads unless an officer or a temporary sign allows it.

### May I reverse to correct a wrong turn?

No. Article 16 bars reversing on one-way roads.

### On which side may I park on a one-way street?

Not on the left side. Stop or park close to the kerb on the right-hand side of your direction of travel.

### Do the weekend evening rules change the one-way pattern?

They override it inside the core zone: from 19:00 to midnight on Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays, motorbikes and cars are barred from the area.

### Which streets mark the core zone?

The city's plan names Trang Tien, Hang Khay, Le Thai To, Hang Dao, Hang Ngang, Hang Buom, Ma May, Hang Bac, Hang Mam, Nguyen Huu Huan and Ly Thai To.
