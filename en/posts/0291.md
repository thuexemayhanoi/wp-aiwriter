Hoang Mai is southern Hanoi's big residential district, and visitors reach it for three reasons: a day at Yen So Park, a meal in one of its food villages, or a longer stay in an apartment area such as Linh Dam. It is not a sightseeing district with a compact centre, so a motorbike helps because places are spread along long avenues. The main trap is the road network. Giai Phong and Tam Trinh carry heavy traffic, Ring Road 3 and the Phap Van to Cau Gie expressway meet near Yen So Park, and the expressway is closed to motorbikes. This guide explains where Hoang Mai sits after the 2025 ward changes, what to plan around, how to approach from the east bank, which roads to avoid, and what longer-stay riders should ask. It is general information as of October 2026.

## Where Hoang Mai is, and its new ward names

Hoang Mai lies south-east of the centre, between the Red River and the Thanh Xuan side of the city. One local guide describes it as divided into three roughly equal parts by Giai Phong and Tam Trinh, which run north to south. The district was created in 2003 and had 14 wards. Hanoi's reorganisation under Resolution 1656/NQ-UBTVQH15 dropped the district level from 1 July 2025. The Hanoi culture department lists seven new wards in this area: Linh Nam, Hoang Mai, Vinh Hung, Tuong Mai, Dinh Cong, Hoang Liet and Yen So.

You will still find "Hoang Mai District" on maps, hotel pages and older guides. Use street names and the name of the park, market or restaurant, and expect addresses with either form.

## Why visitors come

Hoang Mai is not on most first-trip itineraries, which is part of its appeal. Three kinds of reasons come up in travel and expat guides:

- **Yen So Park.** Often called a green lung of the city, it sits beside the Phap Van to Cau Gie expressway interchange in Yen So ward. Vietnam Airlines' travel page puts its area at 323 hectares and describes a large lake, forest, a cultural village, a boat house and a maze garden. One guide lists free entry and opening hours of about 05:00 to 19:00; another says there is a small charge for parking a motorbike. Both are worth confirming at the gate.
- **Food villages.** A local guide names Thanh Tri for its banh cuon, rice rolls, and the Phap Van area in Hoang Liet for snail noodle soup. Both are traditional specialities. One listing for a Thanh Tri banh cuon shop gives hours of about 06:30 to 17:00, but check each place.
- **Apartment and expat areas.** Linh Dam, Dai Kim and Dinh Cong are large planned residential areas, and a guide to the district says it has the fastest urbanisation in Hanoi and many upscale apartments.

There is also Thinh Liet Cathedral, known by other names including Giap Bat, which the same guide lists as a place to visit. Treat any single restaurant or shop name as something to verify, because small businesses move.

## The expressway and Ring Road 3 problem

The most important road fact in Hoang Mai is that the Phap Van to Cau Gie expressway is closed to motorbikes. Law 36/2024/QH15, in force since 1 January 2025, says in Article 25 that motorbikes and mopeds may not use expressways, apart from road-management vehicles and personnel. Our ring roads guide adds that motorbikes have been directed onto the old National Highway 1 via Ngoc Hoi instead.

The risk for a visitor is a navigation app that sends you towards Yen So Park via the expressway ramps, because the park sits beside the interchange. Before setting off:

1. Check the final approach on a map and note the street names you will use.
2. Watch for the green expressway signs and the round motorbike prohibition sign.
3. If you miss a turn, carry on to the next legal exit rather than stopping or reversing.

Our guide to [checking whether a navigation app suggests an expressway](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-check-if-a-navigation-app-suggests-an-expressway/) goes through this step by step. Ring Road 3 has an elevated deck that is car-only, with a ground road below that is open to bikes, as explained in the ring roads guide.

## Traffic patterns to expect

Lao Dong's January 2026 report on the post-holiday return to the capital describes Ring Road 3 as a long-standing hotspot, with the Phap Van to Cau Gie highway, old National Highway 1, National Highway 5 and other axes carrying heavy flows. It says that Hanoi's traffic police identified 51 jam-prone points and routes, and that fixes before the New Year included narrowing the barrier at the Kim Dong and Giai Phong intersection, which is in this part of the city. A December 2025 Lao Dong headline linked from the same page says Hanoi would pilot limits on trucks over 10 tonnes and container trucks heading to Ring Road 3 at rush hour from 1 January 2026.

What that means for a rider:

- **Return days after public holidays are the worst times.** Lao Dong's report describes heavy flows on the southern axes when people return to the capital.
- **Trucks and buses share Giai Phong,** so keep a wider gap than you would on a side street.
- **Plan to be out before the evening peak,** which on weekdays runs roughly from late afternoon into the early evening.

For general night and rain advice, see our guides to [riding at night](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/riding-at-night-in-hanoi/) and [riding in Hanoi rain](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/riding-in-hanoi-rain/).

## A one-day plan

1. Cross the river mid-morning, after the commuter peak.
2. Ride to Thanh Tri or Hoang Liet for a late breakfast or early lunch.
3. Continue to Yen So Park by its street entrance, park in the signed area and walk the lake path.
4. Head back before the late-afternoon build-up.

Keep the plan loose: a long lunch or a slow walk around the lake is a better use of the afternoon than a rushed extra stop. Do not try to fit in other districts on the same day. If you want a second area, our [Hanoi areas guide](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/areas/) lists the options.

## FAQ: Hoang Mai by motorbike

### Is Hoang Mai a good place to visit on a motorbike?

Yes for a day trip to Yen So Park or a food village, with the caveat that its roads are busy and the expressway is off limits.

### Can I ride on the Phap Van to Cau Gie expressway?

No. Law 36/2024/QH15 bars motorbikes from expressways. Use the old National Highway 1 or other ordinary roads.

### Is Hoang Mai still a district?

No. The district level ended on 1 July 2025. The area now has wards such as Hoang Mai, Linh Nam, Vinh Hung, Tuong Mai, Dinh Cong, Hoang Liet and Yen So.

### What is there to do at Yen So Park?

Walk or cycle by the lake, visit its gardens and cultural areas, or picnic. Confirm hours and parking charges at the gate.

### Can the shop deliver a bike to Hoang Mai?

Only for weekly or monthly rentals, and within 10 km of Hoan Kiem Lake unless agreed in advance. Ask the shop about your address.
