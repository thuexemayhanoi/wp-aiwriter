With a passenger and luggage, plan on noticeably less than the headline range, and treat the manufacturer figure as a ceiling rather than a promise. The published numbers for VinFast scooters are measured at a steady 30 km/h with one 65 kg rider, so a second adult, a backpack and a stop-and-go city ride all pull the real figure down. No manufacturer figure for two-up riding turned up in the sources used here, so this guide shows how to reason about it: what the test assumes, how extra weight changes the energy needed, where load limits sit, and how to turn a headline number into a cautious budget for a day with two people. It is general guidance, and the shop confirms the actual bike you are given.

## What the headline range really measures

When VinFast lists a range for a scooter, the fine print matters. The Feliz Lite, for example, is described as reaching 134 km with its standard 2.4 kWh battery "under standard conditions at 30 km/h with a 65 kg person". That is a gentle, constant speed with a light rider and no passenger.

Hanoi riding looks different. You accelerate away from lights, brake for crossings, weave around parked bikes and sometimes sit in a queue. Every start from rest costs more energy than cruising, and every bit of braking wastes some of it (a few models recover a little through regenerative braking, but not all of it).

Add a second person and the gap between the test and your day widens. The test condition is not wrong; it simply describes the best case.

## How extra weight changes the energy you need

Two things use energy on a scooter: pushing the vehicle along the road, and getting it moving after each stop. Both rise with total mass. Rolling friction of the tyres grows in proportion to weight, and the energy to accelerate a heavier load is higher every single time.

Wind resistance does not depend on weight, but at city speeds it is the smaller part of the total. So in town, extra mass matters more than it would on an open highway.

A rough illustration using published figures. The Feliz S is listed at 110 kg including its battery. Add the 65 kg test rider and you have about 175 kg to move. Replace that with two adults of 65 kg each plus a 5 kg bag and the total is about 245 kg, roughly 40 per cent more mass. Energy used in town will not rise by exactly that much, because some losses do not change with weight, but it will rise by a meaningful fraction. That is the author's arithmetic on published weights, not a measured result.

Independent guidance points the same way. One e-bike range calculator says each additional 10 kg adds roughly 5 to 8 per cent to energy use on flat ground, and more on hills, and notes that cargo hurts more than the rider's own weight on a pedal-assist bike. That figure is for e-bikes, so use it only as a sign of direction.

## Check the load limit before you check the range

Range is a comfort question. The load limit is a safety question, and it comes first.

Dealer specification pages for the VinFast Evo200, Evo200 Lite and Feliz Lite list a maximum load of 130 kg. Older VinFast e-scooters described on VinFast's own support page list 130 kg or 150 kg depending on the model. Those are different models from whatever the rental fleet holds on a given day, so they illustrate the typical size of the limit and are not a promise about your bike.

Now do the sum. Two adults of 65 kg each already reach 130 kg before any luggage. Heavier riders and bags push the total over the line. A vehicle loaded beyond its stated limit can accelerate poorly, brake with less margin and feel unstable on rough surfaces, and the battery works harder too.

If your combined weight plus luggage is near or above the listed limit, the honest answer is to choose a different vehicle, or two vehicles, not to squeeze out more range. Ask the shop what the limit is for the specific bike you are offered. ## Luggage: where it goes matters as much as how much

Small electric scooters were designed around one commuter and a bag. The published boot sizes are modest: 22 litres on the Evo200, 25 litres on the Feliz S, 17 to 22 litres on the NEO and Klara Neo models. Those figures come from a news summary of manufacturer specifications.

Practical rules for carrying bags:

- Put heavy items low and close to the centre, such as under the seat if there is room, not on the handlebars.
- Keep bags off the front hooks. A heavy bag on a hook changes steering feel and wears the bag against the front wheel.
- Do not let a passenger hold a large pack on their lap if it pushes them forward and crowds the rider.
- Keep everything clear of the footboard, the rear wheel and the lights.
- Remember that a full backpack on the rider's back raises the load and shifts balance.

For day trips, the smart move is to travel light and keep luggage at the hotel. Our [rental guide](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/rental-guide/) explains what the shop expects at handover.

## A cautious way to budget a two-person day

Start from the headline figure and apply two reductions: one for realistic city riding, one for the extra load. Then keep a reserve.

A conservative planning rule is to assume you will get about half of the headline number with two people and bags in stop-and-go traffic, and to treat only the first 80 per cent of that as usable. Why half? Independent testers of electric kick scooters report getting roughly 50 to 70 per cent of the claimed range in tests that include stops and some hills, and a passenger pushes you toward the low end. Kick scooters are a different class of vehicle, so this is a planning habit, not a measurement of a moped.

An example, using a figure from a different model than a rental bike would necessarily be. Take a scooter with a 134 km headline range, the Feliz Lite single-battery figure.

| Step | Result |
|---|---|
| Headline range | 134 km |
| Two-up, city, bags: plan on about half | about 67 km |
| Keep a 20 per cent reserve | about 54 km usable |

If your day is a loop around the Old Quarter, West Lake and a market, 54 km is plenty. If you wanted a trip out of the city, the arithmetic says no. ## Ways to claw back range with two on board

You cannot change the physics, but you can reduce waste.

1. **Use the economy mode.** VinFast lists ECO and SPORT modes on the Evo, Feliz and Neo models. ECO prioritises distance. Sport trades distance for punch, and a loaded scooter does not need punch.
2. **Accelerate gently.** The energy spent on a quick launch is large when the mass is large.
3. **Hold a steady speed.** Constant cruising is where range figures are best.
4. **Brake early and smoothly.** Late hard braking wastes the energy you spent speeding up.
5. **Keep tyres at the right pressure.** Soft tyres add rolling resistance, and a loaded vehicle squashes them further. Ask the shop for the correct pressure for two riders.
6. **Plan the route.** Avoid long climbs and the busiest junctions at rush hour.
7. **Start full.** The battery level is confirmed at handover, so confirm it reads what you expect before you leave.

## Comfort and handling with two people

A loaded electric scooter feels different. The rider sits further forward, the rear suspension compresses, and the front can feel lighter. Brakes also need longer to stop the same bike.

Test it before you commit. In a quiet street, let your passenger climb on, ride a short loop and brake from walking pace. If the scooter feels twitchy or the brakes seem weak, say so and ask for a different bike. Everyone on the bike must wear a helmet. ## FAQ: range with a passenger and luggage

### How much range do I lose with a passenger?

It depends on the model, the speeds and the weights, and no single published figure exists for two-up rides. Expect a clear reduction, and budget for about half of the headline figure to be safe.

### Is the manufacturer's range figure reliable for two people?

It is reliable for what it measures: one 65 kg rider at a steady 30 km/h. Two people in city traffic are outside that test.

### What is the maximum load of an electric scooter?

Listed loads for the VinFast models mentioned here are 130 kg or 150 kg, but the figure differs by model, so ask the shop about the bike you are given.

### Does carrying luggage hurt range more than a passenger?

Both add mass. A passenger usually adds far more than a bag, but a heavy pack on the handlebars hurts handling more.

### Should I charge to 100 per cent for a two-up day?

VinFast's own guidance on older e-scooters says battery life is better when kept between 20 and 80 per cent in everyday use, but for a day that needs the range, start with the level the shop confirms and plan a charge before you run low.
