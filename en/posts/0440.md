A rental motorbike can be exchanged for another most clearly when it has a defect that affects safety or use, when it is not the bike you agreed to rent or when it cannot be repaired so that the purpose of the rental is not achieved. Swaps for comfort, fit or a change of plan are possible only if the shop agrees and has a suitable bike free, and they may change the price. Nguyen Ha's published pages do not describe an exchange policy, so there is no fixed rule to quote, and the answer depends on the reason, the contract and what is available.

Vietnam's Civil Code gives a lessee certain rights when leased property is defective, including asking for repair, a reduction in rent or an exchange. This guide explains the situations in which an exchange makes sense, what the Code says, how to ask, how price, deposit and the rental clock are handled, how to inspect and record the replacement and when an exchange is not the right answer. It is general information, not legal advice.

## What is and is not published

The shop's pages say several relevant things, and are silent on others.

- **Published:** the exact price is confirmed on the actual bike, its condition and your dates before you sign. A model mentioned online may not be available for your dates. The shop asks you to confirm the exact vehicle, engine capacity, accessories and condition before paying. The cash deposit is reconciled against the recorded condition. A rental day is 24 hours. Early return does not automatically refund unused rent.

- **Not published:** an exchange policy, how a swap affects the rental period, how the price changes if the class changes and how the deposit carries over.

So the first rule is to ask. Do not assume that a swap is free or that it is impossible.

## Reasons for an exchange

| Reason | Is a swap reasonable? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| A safety defect, such as faulty brakes or steering | Yes, ask at once | Stop riding; see our guide to requesting a replacement |
| A mechanical fault that cannot be fixed quickly | Yes | The shop may repair or swap |
| The bike is not the one agreed | Yes | The Civil Code requires delivery of the agreed property |
| A bike that does not fit, such as too tall, too heavy or uncomfortable | Possibly | Depends on stock; may change price |
| A bike that is not suitable for your plan, such as a trip with a passenger | Possibly | Depends on stock and price |
| A change of mind about the model | Possibly, if the shop agrees | A courtesy, not a right |
| A cosmetic issue only | Usually no | Record it and carry on |

## What the Civil Code says

Vietnam's Civil Code (Law No. 91/2015/QH13), in force since 1 January 2017, treats a motorbike rental as a lease of property. We read the lease articles in the text published by the Tuyen Quang Department of Justice. As of October 2026, they say:

- **Article 476:** the lessor must hand over the property in the agreed quantity, quality, type and condition, at the agreed time and place, and give the necessary information on its use. If the property is not of the agreed quality, the lessee may ask the lessor to repair it, reduce the rent or cancel the contract and claim compensation.

- **Article 477:** the lessor must keep the property in the agreed condition and fit for its purpose throughout the lease and must repair damage and defects, except minor damage that custom requires the lessee to repair. If the property's value in use falls without the lessee's fault, the lessee may ask the lessor to repair it, reduce the rent, or exchange it for other property or terminate the contract and claim compensation, if the defect was unknown to the lessee or cannot be repaired so that the purpose of the lease is not achieved.

So the Code expressly mentions exchange for other property in the case of a defect that you did not know about or that cannot be repaired. It does not give a right to swap for convenience. For that, the contract and the shop's goodwill decide. This is general information, not legal advice.

## How to ask for an exchange

1. **Say what the problem is,** plainly, with a photograph or video if it is a fault.

2. **Say what you would like:** a repair, or another bike of the same class, or a different class.

3. **Ask whether a suitable bike is available,** and when.

4. **Ask about price:** will it change, and by how much?

5. **Ask about the clock:** does the rental period change?

6. **Ask about the deposit.**

7. **Ask for the answer in writing.**

A message you can adapt: "Hello, I rented the Honda Wave, plate [number], on [date]. [Describe the problem.] I would like to ask whether you can repair it or swap it for another bike of the same class. If a swap is possible, could you tell me when, whether the price or the deposit change and whether the rental period stays the same? Thank you."

Nguyen Ha can be reached on 0334 699 969 (+84 334 699 969), open 09:00 to 21:00 daily. Our guide to [how to report a mechanical problem to the rental shop](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-report-a-mechanical-problem-to-the-rental-shop/) explains how to give clear facts.

## Price: same class, different class

If the replacement is the same class, a swap for a defect should not cost you more, but ask the shop to confirm. If it is a different class, the price may differ, and a swap you ask for may be charged at the new bike's rate.

For reference, Nguyen Ha's published ranges as of October 2026 show how groups differ: manual bikes VND 150,000–200,000 per day, automatic scooters VND 150,000–200,000 per day with higher weekly and monthly ranges, 50cc bikes VND 200,000 per day and electric bikes quoted by model. Packages are priced separately. If you move between classes, ask for the new price for the remaining period, and ask for it in writing. Our guide to [how to confirm the exact model you will receive](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-confirm-the-exact-model-you-will-receive/) explains how to check, and our guide to [comparing two rental quotes for the same motorbike class](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/comparing-two-rental-quotes-for-the-same-motorbike-class/) shows how to compare.

## The rental clock and the deposit

Two things can be unclear after a swap, so ask.

**The clock.** A rental day is 24 hours from the moment you take the bike. If you swap the bike, ask whether the period continues from the original handover or restarts. If the swap is due to a fault, you should not lose time because of it. Ask the shop to state the new end time in writing, so that you are not caught by the late-return charge, VND 30,000 per hour for 1 to 5 hours and a full extra day from 6 hours late. Our guide to [how a 24-hour rental day is counted in Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-a-24-hour-rental-day-is-counted-in-hanoi/) explains the arithmetic.

**The deposit.** Ask whether the deposit carries over to the replacement or changes, and which handover record applies. The shop's pages say the deposit is reconciled against the recorded condition. If the replacement is a different bike, a new record should be made. Our guide to [how to check deposit return conditions before signing](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-check-deposit-return-conditions-before-signing/) explains what to check.

## Inspect and record the replacement

Do not accept the new bike without checking it, even if you are in a hurry.

- **Check the brakes, tyres, lights, mirrors and controls.** Our [10-minute inspection checklist](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/rental-scooter-inspection-checklist/) gives the routine.

- **Photograph existing marks,** and ask for them to be written down. Our guide to [how to document existing scratches on a rental bike](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-document-existing-scratches-on-a-rental-bike/) explains the method.

- **Record the fuel level and odometer.** Our guide to [how to record the fuel level at handover](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-record-the-fuel-level-at-handover/) explains how.

- **Check the helmet and any accessories,** and the papers that come with the bike.

- **Ask the plate number,** and note that the contract now refers to the new bike.

- **Hand back the first bike's key and items,** and note it.

Our guide to [questions to ask about a high-mileage manual rental bike](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/questions-to-ask-about-a-high-mileage-manual-rental-bike/) explains what to ask about condition.

## Put the swap on the contract

A swap should leave a trace.

- The old bike and its plate, and the new bike and its plate.

- The date and time of the swap.

- The new condition record.

- The price and period after the swap.

- The deposit position.

- Any other change agreed.

If the form has no space, write it on your copy and have staff sign or initial it, then photograph it. Our guide to [reading a motorbike rental agreement in English](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/reading-a-motorbike-rental-agreement-in-english/) explains how to add notes.

## When an exchange is not the right answer

- **If the bike is fine,** and you only want a change of look, ask politely, but do not insist.

- **If you want a bigger bike for a long trip,** a swap may mean a new contract and price. Our guide to [whether to rent a manual bike for touring outside Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/should-you-rent-a-manual-bike-for-touring-outside-hanoi/) explains how to choose.

- **If you have damaged the bike,** a swap does not remove your responsibility for the damage.

- **If no suitable bike is available,** the realistic options are a repair, a different class, or ending the rental. Ask what each would mean for rent and deposit, and have the answer in writing.

- **If the shop declines,** ask why, and decide whether to continue.

## If you are away from the shop

If the bike must be swapped while you are on the road, the shop may send a replacement or ask you to bring the bike in. Ask for the details before you agree, and keep every message. Prices and terms are on the [prices page](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/prices/).

## A quick checklist

1. **Ask the shop, and say why you want a swap.**

2. **For a defect, stop riding and ask at once.**

3. **Ask about price, the rental clock and the deposit.**

4. **Get the answer in writing.**

5. **Inspect the replacement properly.**

6. **Record condition, fuel and odometer.**

7. **Put the swap on the contract.**

## FAQ: exchanging a rental motorbike

### Can I swap my rental bike for another?

Sometimes. For a defect or a bike that is not as agreed, the Civil Code allows a lessee to ask for repair, a lower rent or an exchange in some cases. For other reasons it depends on the shop and its stock.

### Does Nguyen Ha publish an exchange policy?

No. The published pages do not describe one, so ask and get the answer in writing.

### Will a swap cost more?

A swap for a defect within the same class should not, but ask. A different class may change the price.

### Does my rental period change after a swap?

Ask. The published terms say a rental day is 24 hours, so get the new end time in writing.

### What happens to the deposit?

Ask whether it carries over, and which handover record applies to the new bike.

### What should I check on the new bike?

Brakes, tyres, lights, mirrors, controls, fuel, odometer and existing marks, and note them on the contract.
