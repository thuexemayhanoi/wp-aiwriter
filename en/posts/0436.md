Confirm payment methods before pickup by asking the shop, in writing, how you can pay the rent and the deposit, in what currency, at what moment, with what fees and what receipt you will get. Nguyen Ha's published pages set out the deposit, which is cash and separate from the rent, but they do not list the payment methods accepted for the rent, so this is something to ask rather than assume. The cost of not asking is practical: you arrive with the wrong kind of money, you pay a surcharge you did not expect, or you send money to an account that is not the shop's.

This guide explains what is published and what is not, what to ask, how cash, cards and transfers differ for a visitor, how to plan the cash you need, how to avoid payment scams, what to do about receipts and what to do if the shop cannot take your preferred method. It is general information, not financial advice.

## What is published, and what is not

As of October 2026, Nguyen Ha's pages state these things about money.

- **The deposit is cash.** It is commonly VND 2,000,000–5,000,000 depending on the vehicle and agreement, it is paid separately from the rental charge and it is reconciled on return according to the recorded condition and obligations.

- **Reference rates** for each vehicle group, with the exact price confirmed on the actual bike, its condition and your dates before you sign.

- **Charges** such as late return, an empty tank and delivery, in dong.

- **Documents:** the shop does not keep an original passport or identity card instead of the cash deposit.

What the pages do not state is which payment methods the shop accepts for the rent, whether a card or a bank transfer is possible, whether any other currency is accepted or whether any fee applies. The shop's contact page asks renters to get in touch before travelling so that the bike, the price and the deposit can be confirmed, and the payment method is a natural thing to add to that conversation. ## Rent and deposit are two payments

Keep them apart in your mind and in your questions.

| | Rent | Deposit |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | The price of using the bike for the period | Security that is meant to be returned |
| Published form | Not stated | Cash |
| When it is paid | Ask | Paid separately from the rent, normally when the contract is signed |
| What happens at the end | It is spent | It is returned once the bike is checked against the handover record and any late or fuel charge is settled |

If one method works for the rent but only cash works for the deposit, you need both ready. Our guide to [understanding the difference between rent and deposit](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/understanding-the-difference-between-rent-and-deposit/) explains how to read the two on a contract.

## What to ask the shop

Send these questions before you travel and ask for answers in writing.

1. **How can I pay the rent:** cash, bank card, bank transfer, an e-wallet, another method?

2. **How must I pay the deposit,** and in what form? The published terms say cash.

3. **In what currency?** Prices and charges are published in Vietnamese dong. Ask whether any other currency is accepted, and if so, at what rate.

4. **When do I pay?** At signing, on arrival, or in advance? If in advance, is it a booking payment, and what does it cover?

5. **Is there any fee** for paying by card, by transfer or in another currency?

6. **What receipt will I get,** and for which payments? Our guide to [what should be written on a rental payment receipt](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/what-should-be-written-on-a-rental-payment-receipt/) explains what it should show.

7. **How is the deposit returned:** in cash, by transfer, and when?

8. **What if I need to cancel or change the dates,** and is any advance payment refundable?

9. **Who can I pay,** and to which account, if a transfer is accepted? Is the account in the business's name?

Our guides to [questions to ask before reserving a rental scooter](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/questions-to-ask-before-reserving-a-rental-scooter/) and [what to send the rental shop before pickup](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/what-to-send-the-rental-shop-before-pickup/) explain how to put these in a message.

## Cash, cards and transfers for a visitor

Each method has strengths and limits for someone from abroad.

**Cash in dong.** The simplest for a deposit, because it can be counted, recorded in the contract and handed back in the same form. You need to have it on the day. Plan how you will get it: a cash machine, a currency exchange or a hotel. Check your own bank's withdrawal limits and foreign-transaction fees before you rely on a machine, because these vary and we do not state them here.

**Bank card.** Convenient if the shop accepts it, but ask about any surcharge and any hold. A card payment for the rent does not replace a cash deposit if the deposit is cash only.

**Bank transfer.** Useful from abroad, but it carries the highest risk of being directed to a wrong or false account. Only use an account the shop has confirmed through its verified channels, and check the name on the account.

**Other currency.** Prices in Vietnam are normally paid in dong. If a shop says it accepts another currency, ask for the rate in writing, because an unfavourable rate is a hidden cost.

## Plan the cash you need

Work out what you will need on the day.

- **The deposit,** VND 2,000,000–5,000,000 depending on the bike and agreement. Ask the exact amount for your bike.

- **The rent,** if it is paid in cash.

- **A margin** for fuel, parking and small costs. Our guide to [how to pay for fuel, parking and tolls as a visitor in Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/paying-fuel-parking-tolls-hanoi-visitor/) explains what to expect.

- **Notes in sensible denominations,** so that you can pay exactly. Large notes can be hard to break.

Bring it in more than one place, keep it out of sight and do not carry the whole amount on the bike. Our guide to [parking a motorbike in Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/parking-a-motorbike-in-hanoi/) explains why valuables should travel with you.

## When you pay

Be clear about the moment of payment, because it decides what you can still change.

- **At the shop, when you sign.** This is the usual pattern for a rental where the bike is confirmed on the spot. You see the bike, agree the price and pay.

- **In advance.** Some bookings ask for a payment before you arrive. If so, ask what it is for, whether it is refundable, what happens if the bike is unavailable and who receives it. Do not pay in advance on a promise alone.

- **After the rental.** Some charges, such as late return or fuel, are settled at return. Ask how.

Do not pay before the price, the bike and the deposit are confirmed in writing. Our guide to [why a low advertised rental price may not be the final price](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/why-a-low-advertised-rental-price-may-not-be-the-final-price/) explains why.

## Avoiding payment scams

Payment is where fake listings and false contacts do harm.

- **Use verified channels.** Nguyen Ha's published contact details are 0334 699 969 for phone and Zalo and +84 334 699 969 for WhatsApp. Check any number against the shop's own contact page before you send money or documents.

- **Do not send money to a personal account** that a contact gives you, unless the shop has confirmed it through its verified channels and the name matches the business.

- **Beware of urgent requests,** such as "pay now to hold the bike" or "the price ends today".

- **Beware of a request for a large advance** for a bike you have not seen.

- **Do not share card details, a PIN or a one-time code** with anyone.

- **Do not pay a "booking fee"** to a stranger who contacted you first.

- **Keep screenshots** of every payment and message.

## Receipts and records

Every payment should have a record. For cash, a receipt or a line on the contract; for a card, the slip; for a transfer, the confirmation. Check that the record shows the date, the amount, what it is for and the bike. Keep photographs of the receipts and of the signed contract, and keep them until the deposit has been returned. Our guide to [how to keep receipts for repairs on a rental motorbike](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-keep-receipts-for-repairs-on-a-rental-motorbike/) explains how to store records.

## What the law says about rent

Vietnam's Civil Code (Law No. 91/2015/QH13), in force since 1 January 2017, says the lessee must pay the rent in full and on time as agreed, and where there is no agreement on the time, according to local custom, or, if that cannot be determined, when returning the property (Article 481). It also says rent is set by agreement (Article 473). We read the lease articles in the text published by the Tuyen Quang Department of Justice. As of October 2026 that is the general rule, and your contract sets the actual time and method of payment, so get them in writing. This is general information, not legal advice.

## If the shop cannot take your preferred method

- **Ask what it can take,** and whether there is a way to make that work, such as a cash machine nearby.

- **Do not hand over a passport** as a substitute for the deposit.

- **Do not agree to an unusual method** under pressure.

- **Consider another shop** if the options do not suit you. Our guide to [comparing two rental quotes for the same motorbike class](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/comparing-two-rental-quotes-for-the-same-motorbike-class/) explains how to compare.

## A quick checklist

1. **Ask which methods the shop accepts for rent and deposit.**

2. **Expect the deposit to be cash, as published.**

3. **Ask about currency, fees and timing.**

4. **Plan the cash you need, in sensible notes.**

5. **Use only the shop's verified channels and accounts.**

## FAQ: payment methods before pickup

### Is the deposit paid in cash?

Yes. Nguyen Ha's published terms say a cash deposit is paid separately from the rental charge.

### Can I pay the rent by card or transfer?

The shop's pages do not say. Ask in writing before you travel.

### What currency should I use?

Prices are published in Vietnamese dong. Ask whether any other currency is accepted, and at what rate.

### Should I pay in advance?

Not unless the price, the bike and the refund terms are confirmed in writing and the payment goes to a verified account.

### What receipt should I get?

A written record showing the date, amount, purpose and bike. Our guide on what should be written on a rental payment receipt explains it.

### How do I avoid payment scams?

Use only the shop's verified contacts, do not send money to a personal account you cannot verify and do not share card details or codes.
