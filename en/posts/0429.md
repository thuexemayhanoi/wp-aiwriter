The person who commits the traffic offence is the person who is fined, so if you ride a rented motorbike and break a rule, the fine is yours in principle, and rental contracts normally say so. But the bike is registered to its owner, and when a violation is picked up by a camera or another technical device, the authorities write to the owner, not to you. Under Vietnam's penalty decree the owner must cooperate to identify the driver, and an owner who does not cooperate can be fined for the violation.

That is why a shop will contact you about a fine that arrives after you have left, and why the contract clause on fines matters. This guide explains who is responsible under the decree, how camera notices work, which parking offences are fined and by how much, what rental contracts usually say, what to ask before you sign and what to do if a notice arrives. It is general information, not legal advice.

## The basic rule: the violator is fined

Vietnam's penalty decree for road traffic, Decree 168/2024/ND-CP, in force since 1 January 2025, sets fines for the person who commits each offence. A rider who parks on a bridge, runs a red light or rides without a helmet is the person the rules apply to. A rental shop does not become responsible for your offence simply because it owns the bike, and a rental contract normally confirms that the renter pays fines incurred during the rental.

Two articles of the decree matter for the owner's side, which we read in the text on the Government portal.

## When the owner has duties: Article 47

Article 47 deals with violations found through technical devices such as cameras. As of October 2026, it provides that:

- the authority sends a notice asking the vehicle owner and any related person to come to the office of the person with fining authority to settle the case;

- the vehicle owner must cooperate with the authorities to identify the person who drove the vehicle and committed the violation;

- an owner who is an individual and does not cooperate, or cannot show or explain that they were not the driver, is fined for the violation that was detected;

- an owner that is an organisation and does not cooperate or explain so that the driver can be identified is fined as the violating organisation.

For a rental shop, this means the notice arrives with the shop, and the shop must be able to say who had the bike on that date and time. The rental contract, with the renter's name and the dates, is the shop's proof. If the shop cannot identify the renter, it risks being treated as the violator. That is why shops keep records, and why they will pass notices to you.

## Why shops check your licence: Article 32

Article 32 of the same decree sets penalties for vehicle owners. Among them, an owner of a motorbike or moped who hands the vehicle to, or lets, a person who does not meet the conditions in Article 56(1) of the Law on Road Traffic Order and Safety drive it is fined VND 8,000,000–10,000,000 if an individual, and VND 16,000,000–20,000,000 if an organisation. Article 56 of Law No. 36/2024/QH15 requires a driver to hold a valid licence that matches the vehicle.

This is the legal reason a rental shop asks to see and photograph your licence before it gives you a bike, and why it may decline to rent if the licence does not fit the class. ## The main parking and stopping fines

Parking offences are some of the most common fines for visitors. The table sets out the main motorbike provisions in Article 7 of Decree 168/2024/ND-CP, as we read them on the Government portal. The amounts are ranges, and the officer decides within them.

| Behaviour | Fine range |
|---|---|
| Not obeying a sign, marking or instruction | Fine of VND 200,000–400,000 |
| Stopping or parking on the carriageway outside a built-up area where a verge exists | Fine of VND 400,000–600,000 |
| Stopping or parking on the carriageway so as to obstruct traffic, or parking vehicles on the carriageway or pavement unlawfully | Fine of VND 400,000–600,000 |
| Stopping or parking at a pick-up point, a junction or a pedestrian crossing, or under a "no stopping" or "no parking" sign | Fine of VND 400,000–600,000 |
| Stopping or parking on a bridge | Fine of VND 600,000–800,000 |
| Stopping or parking in a road tunnel not at a designated place | Fine of VND 800,000–1,000,000 |

## What rental contracts usually say

Contracts vary, but a fines clause often does these things.

- **Makes the renter responsible** for fines and penalties incurred during the rental period.

- **Requires the renter to pay promptly,** or allows the shop to pay and recover the amount from the renter or the deposit.

- **Covers fines that arrive later,** after the bike has been returned.

- **Allows the shop to give the renter's details** to the authorities.

- **Sometimes adds an administrative fee** for dealing with a notice.

Nguyen Ha's published terms say the cash deposit is held against damage, loss and unpaid charges, but the published terms do not list a fee for handling a fine. Read your own contract, and ask what applies. Our explanation of [what the clauses in a Hanoi rental contract mean](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/hanoi-motorbike-rental-contract-explained/) covers the usual wording, and our guide to [returning your rental motorbike in Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/returning-your-rental-motorbike-hanoi/) notes that fines can surface after the fact.

The Civil Code (Law No. 91/2015/QH13), in force since 1 January 2017, says a lessee must use the leased property as agreed and preserve it. It does not deal with traffic fines specifically, and the contract fills the gap.

## Questions to ask before you sign

- Who is responsible for fines during the rental, and for fines that arrive after it?

- If a fine arrives after I leave, how will I be told, and how long will I have to respond?

- Can the shop deduct a fine from the deposit, and when?

- Is there any handling fee?

- How is the amount confirmed: will I see the notice?

- Will the shop provide my details to the authorities?

- What happens if I disagree with a fine?

Ask for the answers in writing.

## If a fine or notice arrives

1. **Do not ignore it.** The notice has a deadline, and the owner has a duty to cooperate under Article 47.

2. **Check the details:** the date, the time, the place, the plate number and the offence. Compare them with your rental dates and where you were.

3. **Check it is genuine.** The shop should be able to show the notice, and an official notice comes from the authorities.

4. **Reply to the shop quickly,** and tell it whether you were the rider.

5. **If you were the rider,** agree how the fine will be paid and ask for a receipt.

6. **If you were not the rider, or the details are wrong,** say so, and give your evidence, such as your rental dates and photographs. The authority decides, and the shop must cooperate with it.

7. **Keep every message and receipt.**

## How to avoid parking fines

- **Read the signs** before you stop, especially "no stopping" and "no parking" signs.

- **Do not stop on a bridge,** at a junction, at a pedestrian crossing or on a footpath.

- **Use guarded car parks,** which exist for this reason.

- **Do not park on the carriageway** where it obstructs traffic.

- **Photograph where you park,** with signs visible, if you are unsure.

1. **The rider is fined; the contract usually makes it you.**

## FAQ: parking and traffic fines on a rental

### Who pays a fine on a rented motorbike?

The person who committed the offence is fined, and rental contracts normally make the renter responsible for fines during the rental.

### Why does the shop contact me about a fine?

Camera notices go to the registered owner, who must cooperate to identify the driver under Article 47 of Decree 168/2024/ND-CP.

### Can the shop be fined too?

An owner that does not cooperate to identify the driver can be fined for the violation, and an owner who lets an unqualified person drive can be fined under Article 32.

### What is the fine for parking on a bridge?

As of October 2026, VND 600,000–800,000 under Article 7 of Decree 168/2024/ND-CP.

### What if a fine arrives after I return the bike?

Respond promptly, check the details against your rental dates and agree with the shop how it will be handled.

### Should I pay a fine on the roadside?

Pay only through the official procedure and ask for an official record. Do not hand cash to anyone who cannot give you one.
