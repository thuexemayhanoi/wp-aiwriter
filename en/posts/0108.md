The Honda Vision's under-seat box is built for one helmet and the small things of a day out: a rolled raincoat, a charging cable, a water bottle, a light bag. Honda's Vietnamese page says only that the box is large enough for a helmet and many personal items. It gives no volume, and third-party sources quote different figures, from about 16 to about 18 litres, so treat any litre number as approximate. What Honda does state firmly is the weight limit of 10 kg for the box, 1.5 kg for the front pocket and the hook together, and 3 kg for the rear grab rail. Honda also warns that some helmets will not fit, and that valuables and heat-sensitive items do not belong in the box. This guide explains what those limits mean for a visitor, what typically goes where, how to test the box at the shop and what to leave out.

## What Honda actually says

Honda Vietnam's Vision page describes a "spacious" under-seat box that can hold a helmet and a good number of personal items, and a covered front compartment with a USB Type-C socket. It does not publish a volume figure for the box. Two Vietnamese news and dealer pages quote Honda-derived capacity numbers that disagree: one says 16 litres, another 18 litres. The difference may reflect different versions or different measuring methods, so do not plan around either figure. A litre figure describes volume, not shape, and a helmet or a hard-sided bag is an awkward shape.

The Vision's owner's manual, December 2025 edition, gives the limits that matter more:

| Storage point | Honda's maximum |
|---|---|
| Under-seat box | 10 kg |
| Front compartment and hook together | 1.5 kg |
| Rear grab rail | 3 kg |
| All luggage on the bike | 15 kg |
| Rider, passenger, luggage and accessories combined | 170 kg |

The manual also tells riders not to exceed these limits, to tie luggage tightly, balanced and close to the centre of the bike, and to keep it away from the lights and exhaust. It adds that overloading affects steering, braking and stability.

If you rent an older Vision than the 2025 model, the figures may differ, so ask the shop which version you are given.

## The under-seat box: what goes in

Reasonable contents, within the 10 kg limit and the space available:

- **A helmet, if it fits.** Honda says a helmet can be stored in the box with its front facing towards the front of the bike, and that some helmets, because of their size or design, cannot fit. A half-head helmet is the likeliest to go in; a full-face helmet may not.
- **A raincoat,** rolled in a bag.
- **A light jacket or scarf.**
- **A phone charger,** but see the note below about phones themselves.
- **A small umbrella or a water bottle,** laid flat.
- **A small paper bag or lunch box** that will not be crushed.

The box also holds a document compartment on the underside of the seat, which Honda describes for the owner's papers and manual. A rental bike may have its own papers there already, so ask staff what is in it before you add items.

## The front pocket and the hook: small and light

The front compartment on the Vision sits below the handlebar and has a lid. The manual limits it and the carrier hook together to 1.5 kg, which is a little more than a phone, a wallet and a small bottle of water. It also has the USB Type-C port, rated at 15 W (5 V, 3 A) in the manual, for charging a phone while you ride. Honda says you use the port at your own risk and that it is for charging only.

The hook sits below the handlebar, and Honda warns against hanging bulky items on it because they can slip off or interfere with your legs. Think of it as a hook for a single light bag, not for shopping. If you are buying things on a market trip, our piece on [carrying a passenger and luggage on a Lead or Vision](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/scooter-for-two-riders-and-luggage-hanoi/) explains the wider load limits.

## The helmet: a specific caution

Do not assume a rental helmet goes under the seat. Honda says that some helmets cannot fit in the box. The basic helmet supplied with a rental is one per rider and is free. If you are two on the bike, only one is likely to be able to go in the box, and the other must be worn or carried.

Honda also states that the helmet hook under the seat is only for use when the bike is parked. Never ride with a helmet hanging from it. ## A ninety-second test at the shop

Before you leave, with staff present:

1. **Ask for the seat to be opened,** and see how it opens, by key or by the seat switch.
2. **Empty the box** and look at it. Is it clean, dry and free of loose items?
3. **Try your own helmet** or the one the shop gives you, front facing the bike's front, as Honda shows. If it does not close easily, it does not fit.
4. **Put in the bag you plan to carry** and close the seat. Lift the rear of the seat gently to check it is latched, as Honda recommends.
5. **Open the front compartment** and check its lid latches.
6. **Check the hook** is firm and not bent.
7. **Photograph the inside** so that its condition is on record.

If the helmet does not fit, you can still carry it: wear it, or hold it while you are walking. Our [10-minute rental scooter inspection](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/rental-scooter-inspection-checklist/) has the other checks to do at the same time.

## Three example loads and where each goes

These are planning examples, not tested capacities. Always confirm with the actual bike.

**A sightseeing day.** Raincoat rolled in a bag and a light jacket go in the box. The phone stays in your pocket; if the bike has the front USB port and staff agree, a cable can run to it, but ask how it behaves with the key system first. Passport and cash stay on your body. A small camera bag is worn across the chest, not hung from the hook.

**A market or food stop.** Fruit or a bag of snacks goes in the box only if the weight is modest and nothing leaks. A single light bag can hang on the hook. Honda's 1.5 kg limit for the hook and front compartment together is low, so a heavy bag does not belong on the hook; carry less or ask the shop about a bigger scooter.

**A rainy afternoon.** The raincoat comes out of the box at a safe stop. Wet items go back into a plastic bag, and the plastic bag goes in the box away from documents. A helmet that does not fit stays on your head, or with you if you stop for food.

## What not to put in the box

Honda's manual says not to put flammable items, or anything damaged by heat, in the box, and not to leave valuables or fragile objects there. A box under a seat in direct sun can get warm, so think about what heat could spoil.

Specific things to avoid:

- **Passports, cash and cards.** Keep them on your body.
- **Phones and power banks.** Heat can damage batteries, and on Smart Key versions Honda's manual says not to put mobile phones or other radio-emitting devices in any storage box because they can disturb the Smart Key system. Keep the phone on you.
- **Aerosols, lighters and fuel containers.**
- **Wet clothing** without a bag. Honda notes that water in the box can damage documents and other contents.
- **Anything fragile,** such as a camera or glass bottle.
- **Spare keys or a smart key fob,** which Honda says not to store in the box. On Smart Key versions the manual warns that anyone with the fob can start the bike if the ignition is on.

## The box and the fuel

On the Vision, the fuel filler lies under the seat, so refuelling involves lifting the seat. Honda's manual describes opening the seat, turning the filler cap anticlockwise and filling no higher than the bottom of the filler neck. Take out anything loose first, so it does not fall into the filler, and never leave your key in the box when you close the lid. ## FAQ: Honda Vision storage

### How many litres is the Vision's under-seat box?

Honda's Vietnamese page gives no litre figure. Third-party sources quote about 16 to 18 litres, so treat it as approximate.

### How much weight can the box hold?

Honda's manual gives a maximum of 10 kg for the under-seat box.

### Will a full-face helmet fit?

It may not. Honda says some helmets cannot fit in the box. Test it at the shop.

### What is the limit for the front pocket?

The front compartment and the hook together may carry 1.5 kg, according to the manual.

### Can I charge my phone on the Vision?

Some versions have a USB Type-C port in the front compartment, which Honda describes as a charging port only. Check that your bike has it.

### Is the box safe for valuables?

No. Honda says not to leave valuables or fragile items in it. Keep passports, cash and cards on you.
