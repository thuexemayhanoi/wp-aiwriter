Compare a northbound and a southbound day trip on seven points rather than on how famous the destination is: how far the road really is, what kind of road it is, whether it climbs, how much daylight you will have, what time the attraction stops taking visitors, where you can refuel and how tired you will be on the way back. In October 2026 the sun sets in Hanoi at about 17:44 at the start of the month and about 17:21 at the end, so the daylight budget alone often decides the answer. This guide uses Tam Dao to the north and Ninh Binh to the south as worked examples, then gives you a scoring sheet you can reuse for any pair of directions. It does not claim exact journey times, because they depend on traffic, the bike and how often you stop.

## Why compare directions at all

Most visitors choose a day trip by looking at photographs. That works for a car with a driver and a flexible schedule; on a small rental bike the road matters as much as the view. A flat road on a busy trunk route and a quiet climb to a hill town are different kinds of day, even when the map distances are similar. Comparing the two directions before you book also shows you what you need to ask the shop: the bike, the return time and whether the plan is acceptable under your rental agreement.

Start from the east bank. Nguyen Ha Motorbike Rental is at Alley 5 Nguyen Van Cu Street in Bo De Ward, Long Bien District, and opens from 09:00 to 21:00. Whichever way you go, you begin by crossing the Red River to the city side, so a "northbound" or "southbound" trip includes one bridge crossing in each direction. Our guide to [Hanoi's ring roads and highways](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/hanoi-ring-roads-highways-motorbikes-allowed/) explains which main roads accept motorbikes.

## The seven points that decide it

| Point | What to ask | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Road distance | How far on the roads a motorbike can use, not the fastest car route | Expressways are closed to motorbikes |
| Road type | Trunk road, village road or mountain road | Sets your speed and your concentration |
| Climb | Does the route rise into hills? | Affects the gear, the engine and the brakes |
| Daylight | When is sunset and when do you need to be off the road? | October days are short |
| Last entry | When is the last boat, ticket or visit? | Late arrival can waste the trip |
| Fuel | Where are the stations and how big is the tank? | See our guide to planning around fuel range |
| Return | What time must the bike be back? | Late returns are charged |

Score each point from 1 (easy) to 3 (demanding) for each direction, then add them up. A lower score means a gentler day. The numbers are your own judgement, not a rating from any authority, and they change with the weather and your experience.

## Northbound example: Tam Dao

Tam Dao is a hill town in the Tam Dao range, and published distances from Hanoi vary: a VietnamPlus report gives about 60 kilometres, VnExpress says 70 in one place and 90 in another, and Vinh Phuc's provincial page gives 90. The differences come from different starting points and routes, so measure your own route on a map. Tourism sources describe the town at about 900 metres above sea level, with a cool climate that can change within a day.

### What the north asks of a rider

VnExpress warns that the ride up has steep slopes and multiple bends, and advises a moderate speed. A tour operator's guide says the mountain section of National Highway 2B deserves serious attention and that Tam Dao is best suited to experienced mountain riders; it also notes that Vinh Phuc has merged into Phu Tho province, so older maps and listings may still say Vinh Phuc. A hill road is a different task from a flat one: you will use lower gears, brake earlier and need tyres and brakes in good condition. Our guides to [shifting gears on hills](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/shifting-gears-on-hills-without-straining-a-rental-motorbike/) and [steep mountain roads and manual bikes](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/manual-bikes-and-steep-mountain-roads-when-to-choose-another-vehicle/) explain what to expect.

### Weather and light in the north

October and November are described by one operator as a good balance of clearer views and lower rainfall, but hill weather changes quickly, and fog is a common reason to slow down or turn back. Because you climb late in the day, the descent is the part most likely to happen in poor light. Plan to be heading down well before sunset.

## Southbound example: Ninh Binh

The Ninh Binh tourism department places the province 93 kilometres south of Hanoi, with National Highway 1A, National Highway 10 and the north-south railway running through it. UNESCO describes Trang An as a mixed cultural and natural property, and the province's own overview names Tam Coc, Trang An and Hoa Lu among the sights. The land is flat, so the day is about distance, traffic and timing, not climbing.

### What the south asks of a rider

The practical difficulty on this side is the road. The expressway that runs parallel to National Highway 1A is closed to motorbikes under Article 25 of the Road Traffic Order and Safety Law (36/2024/QH15, effective from 1 January 2025), so a rental scooter must stay on ordinary roads, which carry trucks and coaches. Our guide to [Hanoi to Ninh Binh by motorbike](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/hanoi-to-ninh-binh-by-motorbike/) describes the route, and [Hanoi to Ninh Binh and back in one day](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/hanoi-ninh-binh-one-day-motorbike-itinerary/) gives a realistic itinerary. The risk here is fatigue from a long, monotonous day, not steep bends.

### Opening times in the south

Boat operations stop in the afternoon. One travel site lists Trang An hours of 06:00 to 16:45 in summer and 07:00 to 16:30 in winter, and advises buying tickets before 15:30; it also says hours can change with weather and conditions. Treat that as an example of why last entry matters and confirm the current hours on the day. If the last boat leaves a little before 16:30 and sunset is a little after 17:20, a late start leaves you little room.

## Putting the two side by side

| Point | Northbound (Tam Dao) | Southbound (Ninh Binh) |
|---|---|---|
| Road distance | Published figures range from about 60 to 90 km; measure your route | About 93 km to the province, according to Ninh Binh tourism |
| Road type | National highway, then a mountain road | National highway and local roads |
| Climb | Steep slopes and many bends on the final section | Flat |
| Daylight | Descent is the risky part | Return ride in the dark is the risk |
| Last entry | Town is open all day; light is the limit | Boat tours end mid-afternoon; check the day |
| Fuel | Fill before the climb | Stations along the trunk road |
| Fatigue | Concentration on bends | Long monotonous ride |

These comparisons are general. Neither direction is better; they test different skills. If you are new to riding in Vietnam, the flat road is usually more forgiving, and our guide for [new riders learning semi-automatic shifting](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/can-a-new-rider-learn-semi-automatic-shifting-in-hanoi-traffic/) may help you decide whether either trip is realistic yet.

## Work out the daylight budget

The easiest way to compare two trips is to count backwards from sunset. In October 2026 sunset falls between about 17:44 and 17:21 according to Timeanddate. Subtract a safety margin, the time you want to spend at the destination and the time to ride out and back, including stops. If the result means you must leave before the shop opens or ride the last part in the dark, choose the shorter trip, or choose a different transport.

Night riding on unlit rural roads is a separate skill. Our guide to [riding at night in Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/riding-at-night-in-hanoi/) explains the lights and visibility issues. For a day trip, the simplest rule is to be off the unfamiliar roads before dusk. If you start at the 09:00 opening, ask the shop whether an earlier arrangement is possible, and do not assume one.

## When to choose neither

A day trip is not always the right size for the destination. Tam Dao and Ninh Binh both reward a night, and some tourist information describes Tam Dao as better with one night. If your day looks tight on paper, an overnight trip or a bus is often a better plan than a rushed ride. If you have only one day and one tank, treat a bus or an organised tour as a perfectly sensible alternative rather than a failure of the plan.

## Before you commit

- Measure both routes on a map app set to motorbike and check the result avoids expressways.
- Check sunset for your date and work backwards.
- Check the last entry time of the main attraction.
- Ask the shop whether the bike, the destination and the return time are acceptable and get the answer in writing.
- Choose the direction whose hardest part you are most comfortable with.
- Plan one fuel stop in each direction. Our guide to [when to refuel during a day trip](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/when-to-refuel-a-manual-motorbike-during-a-day-trip/) shows how.

## FAQ: comparing northbound and southbound day trips

### Which direction is easier for a beginner?

Generally the flat road south, because it avoids steep bends, but it has heavier trucks and a longer, more monotonous day. Neither is easy for someone who has not ridden in Vietnamese traffic.

### Is Tam Dao or Ninh Binh closer?

Published distances for Tam Dao range from about 60 to 90 kilometres depending on the source and route; Ninh Binh tourism gives 93 kilometres to the province. Compare the roads you can actually use, not the straight-line distance.

### Can I use the expressway to save time?

No. Ordinary motorbikes are not allowed on expressways under the 2024 road traffic law, effective from 1 January 2025. Use the national highway and ordinary roads.

### What time should I turn back?

Work backwards from sunset, which in October 2026 falls between about 17:44 and 17:21, and leave a safety margin. If the plan needs you to ride in the dark, shorten it.

### Do I need the shop's permission?

Ask. Whether a rental bike may make a particular trip is for the shop to decide, and the answer should be agreed before you leave.
