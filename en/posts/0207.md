A 50cc rental is a good fit for a short visit to Long Bien if you want a slow, simple bike for a few nearby stops and you can use it for most of a day. The rental day is 24 hours and shorter use is charged as a full day, so a two-hour trip costs the same as a long one. The moped is capped at 40 km/h in Vietnamese traffic rules, which suits the bridge, the riverside lanes and the market streets of the east bank. Nguyen Ha is based in Long Bien itself, near Gia Lam bus station, so you can collect the bike close to where you plan to ride. This guide explains how the rental works, who it suits, a loop that fits into half a day and the places and times where plans can go wrong.

## How a short rental is charged

Short does not mean cheap here, and it helps to understand why before you book. According to the shop's terms, one rental day is 24 hours, and use for less than that is still charged as a full day. Weekly and monthly packages are priced separately and are not simply the day rate multiplied.

For a 50cc bike or scooter the listed rate is VND 200,000 per day, and the [prices page](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/prices/) shows the weekly and monthly figures. Other points that affect a short visit:

- **Late return:** VND 30,000 per hour for the first one to five hours, and from six hours late it becomes one extra rental day.
- **Early return:** unused time is not refunded.
- **Helmet:** one basic helmet per rider is included.
- **Fuel:** petrol bikes are handed over with about 0.5 to 2 litres and come back with about 0.5 to 1 litre; an empty tank at return costs VND 20,000.
- **Deposit and paperwork:** a cash deposit is held separately from the rent, and identity documents are checked and photographed for the contract. Originals are not kept.

The practical conclusion is simple. If you only have two hours free, a taxi or a ride-hailing bike may be cheaper. If you have a morning or an afternoon and several stops in mind, the day rate is more reasonable, and you can fit more into it.

## Who a 50cc bike suits for this trip

A moped is the one kind of motorbike that Vietnamese law lets you ride without a motorcycle licence. Under the Law on Road Traffic Order and Safety (Law 36/2024/QH15), in force since 1 January 2025, a moped has a design speed of no more than 50 km/h and, if it has a petrol engine, a capacity of no more than 50 cc. Article 59 sets the minimum age at 16. Tell the shop when you book that you want a bike you can ride legally without a motorcycle licence, and keep in mind that other rules of the road still apply.

A 50cc bike also suits nervous beginners who want the lightest and slowest machine while they get used to traffic. The east bank has quieter streets than the Old Quarter, which makes it a fair place to practise. Our guide to [renting a 50cc scooter in Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/renting-a-50cc-scooter-in-hanoi/) goes through who the bike suits and who should choose a larger one.

## Where the shop sits in relation to the ride

The shop is at Alley 5 Nguyen Van Cu Street, Bo De Ward, Long Bien District, near Gia Lam bus station, and it is open from 09:00 to 21:00 daily. Starting there means you are already on the east side of the Red River, so the bridge is a ride towards the centre and not a crossing you have to plan from your hotel. The [Long Bien area page](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/long-bien/) lists what the district offers visitors. Delivery is available only for weekly and monthly rentals, so a short rental starts at the shop.

## A half-day loop that fits a moped

This is an outline, not a fixed route, and you should adapt it to your energy and the weather. Think of it in four parts.

### Part 1: Long Bien Bridge

The bridge is the main reason most visitors come. It was built between 1899 and 1902, and Vietnam's tourism authority describes a single-track railway down the middle, with two side paths for motorbikes and bicycles and a narrow one for pedestrians. Cars are banned. The tourism authority also notes that traffic on the bridge runs on the left, unlike other bridges, so follow the arrows and the flow of other riders. Our [first-ride guide to Long Bien and the Red River](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/long-bien-red-river-first-ride/) describes the approaches in more detail.

Access has changed this year. Hanoi closed the road section of the bridge to motorbikes for about two months of repairs from 28 March 2026, and local reports say it reopened to motorbikes, bicycles and pedestrians from 28 May 2026. Closures like this can happen again, so ask the shop on the day whether the bridge is open and follow any posted signs. During the repair period traffic was sent to Chuong Duong Bridge.

### Part 2: the riverside and dyke roads

On the east bank the dyke roads run beside the river, with fewer junctions than the city streets. At moped speed this is a pleasant stretch, but stay to the right and let faster bikes pass. Pull in at wide places to take photographs. Do not stop on the bridge itself, where the lane is narrow.

### Part 3: Long Bien Market

The market is a working wholesale place with deliveries, so it is better to visit with some care. Our guide to [visiting Long Bien Market without blocking deliveries](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-visit-long-bien-market-without-blocking-deliveries/) explains where to leave the bike and when it is busiest.

### Part 4: an optional longer leg

If you have more time, the pottery village of Bat Trang lies on the Red River more than 10 km from the city centre, according to cycling-tour operators. It is reachable by moped but a long outing at 40 km/h, so treat it as a full-day plan. Our [east bank and Bat Trang guide](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/gia-lam-east-bank-bat-trang-by-scooter/) covers it.

## Speed, lanes and the weekend evening rule

A moped may not exceed 40 km/h on the road under the speed regulation in force since 1 January 2025 (Circular 38/2024/TT-BGTVT), as summarised by thuvienphapluat.vn. On the bridge and on city streets this is rarely an issue, since traffic is slow, but on the dyke roads you will be the slowest vehicle. Keep right, signal when you move out and do not ride on pavements.

There is one new rule that affects the end of the trip. Hanoi launched a pilot low-emission zone on 1 July 2026. In its first zone, which includes the Hoan Kiem Lake pedestrian area, the Old Quarter and the Hoan Kiem night market, motorbikes, motorcycles and cars are banned from 7 p.m. to midnight on Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays. If you plan to ride towards the centre on a weekend evening, read our guide to [riding in the Old Quarter](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/riding-in-hanoi-old-quarter/) and plan your route around the closure, or leave the bike and walk.

## Parking at your stops

Look for a guarded place with an attendant and a ticket, and take your helmet with you. The shop does not provide an extra lock, so the choice of place does the work. Our guide to [parking a motorbike in Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/parking-a-motorbike-in-hanoi/) explains the options, and the [Hoan Kiem Lake parking guide](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/where-to-park-a-scooter-near-hoan-kiem-lake/) helps if you carry on into the centre.

## Before you ride away from the shop

1. Walk round the bike with staff and photograph any scratches.
2. Check the brakes, lights, indicators, horn and mirrors.
3. Confirm the fuel level and how the bike is started.
4. Ask whether Long Bien Bridge is open today.
5. Ask the shop's latest advice on closed streets for your day.
6. Note the opening hours (09:00 to 21:00) and your return time.
7. Save the shop's number, 0334 699 969, in your phone.

## FAQ: a short 50cc rental in Long Bien

### Can I rent a 50cc bike for just a few hours?

The shop charges by the 24-hour day, and shorter use is charged as a full day. Ask the shop to confirm how it applies to your booking.

### Can a moped cross Long Bien Bridge?

Motorbikes and mopeds have used the bridge's side lanes, and it reopened to them on 28 May 2026 after repairs. Check on the day, since closures can recur.

### Do I need a motorcycle licence for a 50cc bike?

Under Law 36/2024/QH15 a moped needs no motorcycle licence, and the minimum age is 16. Helmets and the other rules still apply, and your own travel cover may have its own conditions.

### How fast can a 50cc moped go in Hanoi?

It may not exceed 40 km/h on the road under the regulation in force since 1 January 2025.

### Is the Old Quarter open to motorbikes at weekends?

Not in the evenings in the pilot zone: motorbikes are banned from 7 p.m. to midnight on Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays. Check current notices before you go.
