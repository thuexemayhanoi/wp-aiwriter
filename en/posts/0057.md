Carrying a passenger on a semi-automatic motorbike is legal in Vietnam, but it changes how the bike starts, shifts and stops. A Honda Wave or Yamaha Sirius is a light machine with a small engine, and a second adult adds a large share of its own weight to the load. This guide explains what the 2025 law allows, how the extra weight alters the gear technique, what to tell your passenger before you move and when it is wiser to choose a bigger scooter or a taxi. It is written for riders who already hold the right licence and can ride the bike solo. It is general information, not legal advice or a substitute for training.

## What the law allows

Article 33 of the Law on Road Traffic Order and Safety (Law No. 36/2024/QH15), in force since 1 January 2025, says that the rider of a two-wheeled motorbike may carry only one person. A rider may carry at most two people only in four cases: taking a sick person to emergency care, escorting a person who has broken the law, carrying a child under 12, or carrying an elderly, frail or disabled person. Both the rider and the passenger must wear a helmet that meets the national standard, with the chin strap fastened correctly.

The same article lists what neither the rider nor the passenger may do. The rider may not carry bulky loads, carry anyone standing on the seat or carrier or sitting on the handlebars, or ride abreast. A passenger may not carry bulky objects, use an umbrella, hold on to or push other vehicles, or stand on the seat. For the child rule in detail, see our guide to [child passengers on a motorbike in Vietnam](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/child-passengers-motorbike-vietnam/).

## What breaking the rules costs

The penalty decree, Decree 168/2024/ND-CP, which we read on the Government portal, sets out these fines for riders in Article 7 as of October 2026:

- **Carrying two passengers outside the listed cases:** a fine of VND 400,000–600,000.
- **Carrying three or more passengers:** a fine of VND 600,000–800,000 and two licence points.
- **A passenger without a helmet or with the strap undone:** a fine of VND 400,000–600,000, except for a patient taken for emergency care, a person being escorted after an offence and a child under six.
- **Carrying bulky objects, or a person standing on the seat or sitting on the handlebars:** a fine of VND 600,000–800,000.

Decree 238/2026/ND-CP, which amends the decree from 15 August 2026, does not change Article 7. Licence points are deducted from Vietnamese licences, so a foreign licence does not accumulate them, but the fine and the written record remain. Our guide to [helmet rules in Vietnam](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/helmet-rules-in-vietnam/) covers the helmet standard in detail.

## The weight budget

A passenger is the single biggest change to the bike. Honda's owner's manuals for the Wave list a maximum load that covers the rider, one passenger and all luggage and accessories. In the manuals we checked, the figure is 115 kg for some model versions and 155 kg for others, and the manuals also state that the bike carries the rider and one passenger. Two adults of ordinary build can pass 115 kg on their own, before a single bag is added.

That is not a reason to panic, because those figures are manufacturer limits and bikes are routinely ridden near them. It is a reason to ask the shop which limit applies to the bike you are offered and to do the sum honestly. Our guide to [how much luggage you can carry on a Honda Wave](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-much-luggage-can-you-safely-carry-on-a-honda-wave/) shows how. If the two of you plus bags are over the limit, pick a bigger bike. Our comparison of [scooters for two riders and luggage](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/scooter-for-two-riders-and-luggage-hanoi/) explains the alternatives.

## How the extra weight changes the gears

With a passenger, the bike has more mass to move and the engine has the same power. In practice that has four effects on the gear technique described in our guide to [starting, shifting and stopping a Honda Wave smoothly](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/honda-wave-gears-starting-shifting-and-stopping-smoothly/):

- **Starts are slower.** Roll on the throttle a little more firmly and stay in first gear a little longer, but keep the throttle smooth. A snatched start makes the passenger's head jerk back.
- **Upshifts come later.** The engine has to work harder to reach each gear's useful speed, so give each gear more time before changing up, and do not change up in the middle of an incline.
- **Hills need a lower gear sooner.** Change down before the engine starts to labour, not after, and avoid steep ramps with a heavy load until you know how the bike behaves.
- **Stops need more distance.** Start slowing earlier, use both brakes together and keep the steering straight, as Honda's safe-riding training document teaches.

Honda's document also warns about the two classic braking errors, using only the rear brake, which locks the wheel, or only the front brake, which can lift the rear. With a passenger over the rear wheel, a gentle but firm two-brake stop is even more important.

## Before you move: briefing your passenger

A passenger who does not know what to do is the largest variable on the bike. Before the engine is running, tell them:

1. **Mount after you, and only when you say.** Hold the bike steady and let them step on from the left, with their weight low.
2. **Feet on the footrests, always.** Away from the chain, the exhaust and the rear wheel.
3. **Hold the grab rail or your waist,** not your shoulders or the handlebars.
4. **Lean with you.** Look over your shoulder in the direction of the turn and keep still. Do not shift weight suddenly or lean the other way.
5. **Keep bags and clothing clear.** Scarves, bag straps and loose trousers must not touch the wheel or the chain.
6. **Tell you early** about any problem, with a tap on the shoulder, before the bike is moving fast.

Agree a signal, for example two taps on the shoulder to say "stop". In Hanoi's noise, spoken words disappear.

## Moving off with a passenger

Check the mirrors, signal and wait for a gap that is longer than you would need alone. Select first gear with a foot on the brake, roll on the throttle smoothly and keep your head up. If the bike wobbles at the start, put a foot down rather than trying to correct with the handlebars.

Do not start on a slope with a passenger until you are confident, and avoid wet surfaces, painted lines and metal covers until you have practised. If the passenger is nervous, go slowly in the first block. Their confidence matters because a stiff, tense passenger affects the bike's balance.

## Riding two-up in Hanoi traffic

Keep a larger gap in front. Honda's training material recommends a safe distance to the vehicle ahead, and with a passenger you need more of it. Plan the stop earlier at junctions, brake progressively and avoid filtering through narrow gaps where the rear wheel and the passenger's knees add width. Our guide to [crossing Hanoi intersections](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/crossing-hanoi-intersections/) explains how traffic flows through junctions.

Corners need extra margin. Honda's advice is to reduce speed before the bend, avoid braking in the middle of it and keep to the right side of the road. With a passenger, enter bends slower than you would alone, hold a steady throttle and let the passenger lean naturally.

## Speed, distance and fatigue

A long ride with a passenger tires both of you. Take breaks every hour or so, check that the passenger's feet are still on the footrests and agree on a place to stop. Do not use the trip as a chance to try a new route or a steep climb. Keep to the speed limits; our guide to [speed limits for motorbikes in Vietnam](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/speed-limits-motorbikes-vietnam/) explains them. For a full day trip, a bike with more power and a more comfortable seat suits two people better than a small Wave.

## What to ask the shop

Tell the shop at booking that you will carry a passenger, and ask the following:

- Which bike version is this, and what is its maximum load?
- Does the bike have a passenger footrest and grab rail in good condition?
- How are the rear tyre and rear suspension?
- Is the rental price or deposit affected by a passenger?

## When to choose another option

A manual or semi-automatic bike with two adults is a stretch in some situations: steep climbs, long highway stretches, wet roads, heavy luggage, or a nervous passenger. In those cases an automatic scooter with a flat floor and a bigger seat is easier, a taxi or ride-hailing car is safer, and a guided tour with a driver removes the problem entirely. Choosing another option is not a failure. It is a sensible response to a load the bike was not built to carry comfortably.

## FAQ: carrying a passenger on a manual motorbike

### How many passengers can I carry on a Honda Wave in Vietnam?

One. Article 33 of the 2024 law allows two passengers only in specific cases, such as a child under 12, an elderly or disabled person or a patient being taken to emergency care.

### What is the fine for carrying too many passengers?

As of October 2026, carrying two passengers outside the listed cases is fined VND 400,000–600,000, and carrying three or more is fined VND 600,000–800,000 under Decree 168/2024/ND-CP.

### Does my passenger need a helmet?

Yes. The helmet rule applies to everyone on the bike. A passenger without a helmet or with the strap undone is fined VND 400,000–600,000, with narrow exceptions.

### Will a passenger make the bike harder to ride?

Yes. Starts are slower, braking distances are longer and hills need a lower gear sooner. Practise with your passenger on a quiet road first.

### Can a passenger carry a bag?

A passenger may carry a normal bag, but not bulky objects. Keep the load low and tied down and stay within the bike's maximum load.

### Should a beginner carry a passenger on a manual bike?

No. Learn the bike solo first. Our guide on [learning semi-automatic shifting in Hanoi traffic](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/can-a-new-rider-learn-semi-automatic-shifting-in-hanoi-traffic/) explains how.
