A 50cc motorbike suits university neighbourhoods in Hanoi when your trips are short and local: from a dormitory or rented room to lectures, the library, a market or a cafe a few streets away. It is light, easy to park and does not need a driving licence for riders aged 16 or over, which is why it is common among students. It is a poor choice when your daily route includes fast main roads, because a moped is limited to 40 km/h under the current speed rules. At Nguyen Ha a 50cc costs VND 200,000 per day, VND 1,000,000 per week and VND 2,000,000 per month, with a cash deposit between VND 2,000,000 and VND 5,000,000. This guide explains where Hanoi's main university areas are, who a 50cc suits, what to ask for a monthly rental, and how to ride around campuses.

## Why a 50cc suits student life

- **Short hops.** Many student trips are a few kilometres: dormitory to lecture hall, flat to library, a market run. A small, light bike handles that without effort.

- **Easy to manoeuvre and park.** Narrow lanes beside campuses and crowded bike parks favour a light bike.

- **Slow by design.** A moped is held to 40 km/h on roads other than expressways, according to a summary of Circular 38/2024/TT-BGTVT on thuvienphapluat.vn, which suits streets where traffic is slow anyway.

- **No licence class required for the moped class.** Under Law 36/2024/QH15, in force since 1 January 2025, a person aged 16 or over may ride a moped (a design speed of no more than 50 km/h and no more than 50 cm3 for a petrol engine), and the licence requirement for drivers does not apply to moped operators. As of October 2026 this is the position stated in the sources listed below, and a visitor should still confirm with the shop that the bike they are given is in the moped class.

- **Light on fuel.** A small engine sips petrol, though the amount depends on the bike.

A 50cc is not the cheapest long rental. ## Who it suits, and who should think again

**It suits:**

- Exchange and language students who hold no motorcycle licence, or whose home licence does not cover motorcycles.
- Interns and short-course students who need transport for a few weeks.
- Nervous riders who want the lightest bike while they get used to the traffic.
- Anyone living and studying within a few streets of each other.

**Think again if:**

- Your daily route uses fast roads, ring roads or long stretches at 50 km/h or more.
- You regularly carry a passenger and a bag, since a 50cc is slow with two people.
- You are under 18. A person aged 16 or over may ride a moped, but the shop's conditions for younger renters differ, so ask before you plan around it.

## Setting up a monthly rental near campus

Most students do better with a weekly or monthly rental than with day-by-day hire. Weekly and monthly packages are priced on their own, not as the day rate multiplied by the number of days. Points to settle first:

1. **Which bike.** The shop's 50cc range is Cub 50cc bikes and 50cc scooters, and the actual bike is confirmed when you book. Check the [50cc bikes page](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/50cc-bikes/).

2. **Delivery.** Delivery is offered only for weekly or monthly agreements, within 10 km of Hoan Kiem Lake: VND 50,000 per trip or VND 100,000 both ways, and beyond 10 km by agreement. Ask whether your accommodation is within range.

3. **Deposit and documents.** The deposit is VND 2,000,000 to VND 5,000,000, separate from rent. Identity documents are checked and photographed, and originals are not kept.

4. **Helmet.** One basic helmet per rider is included free. Our article on [choosing a helmet that fits](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/choosing-a-helmet-that-fits-for-a-manual-motorbike-journey/) explains why fit matters.

5. **Late returns and extension.** A petrol bike returned 1 to 5 hours late costs VND 30,000 per hour, and from 6 hours it is an extra rental day, so tell the shop in advance if your term ends late.

6. **Raincoat and lock.** A raincoat is not included for short rentals and may be considered for monthly rentals. An extra lock is not provided, so lock the steering and park in guarded places.

## Riding around campuses

### Main roads beside the universities

Giai Phong and Dai Co Viet are busy main roads with heavy traffic. A moped limited to 40 km/h should keep right, stay in its lane and let faster vehicles pass. Do not weave to keep up. Our guide to [crossing Hanoi intersections](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/crossing-hanoi-intersections/) explains how traffic flows and how to merge calmly.

### Narrow lanes and gates

The streets beside campuses often fill with people on foot, bicycles and parked bikes. Ride at walking pace near gates, expect pedestrians to step out between parked bikes, and avoid the pavement. Some campuses may limit vehicles at their gates or inside, so ask the university or a classmate before you plan to ride in.

### Parking

Parking is the part of student life with the most friction. Use guarded lots where you can, keep your ticket, lock the steering, and do not block lanes or entrances. Our guide to [parking a motorbike in Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/parking-a-motorbike-in-hanoi/) explains guarded lots, tickets and towing. The shop does not provide an extra lock, so a secure place matters even more.

### Rain, night and fatigue

Late lectures and study sessions mean riding after dark and sometimes in rain. Check lights before you leave, use a rain poncho of your own, and slow down on wet paint lines. Our guides to [riding at night in Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/riding-at-night-in-hanoi/) and [riding in Hanoi rain](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/riding-in-hanoi-rain/) cover both. Never ride after drinking, since Vietnam's rule on alcohol for riders is strict.

## A weekly habit that saves trouble

Once a week, spend two minutes on the bike: look at the tyres, test both brakes, check the lights and indicators, see that the mirrors are firm, and wipe the chain case. Our [ten-minute inspection guide](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/rental-scooter-inspection-checklist/) gives the full list. Photograph any new mark and tell the shop. For fuel, remember that petrol bikes are handed over with about 0.5 to 2 litres and come back with about 0.5 to 1 litre, and an empty tank at return costs VND 20,000.

## FAQ: 50cc rental near universities

### Can a student ride a 50cc without a motorcycle licence?

Under Law 36/2024/QH15, a person aged 16 or over may ride a moped, which is a vehicle with a design speed of no more than 50 km/h and, for a petrol engine, no more than 50 cm3. The shop confirms that your bike is in that class.

### Is a 50cc fast enough for Giai Phong or Dai Co Viet?

It is slow on those roads. A moped is limited to 40 km/h, so keep right and let faster vehicles pass, or choose another route.

### Does the shop deliver to my dormitory?

Delivery is only for weekly or monthly agreements, within 10 km of Hoan Kiem Lake, at VND 50,000 per trip or VND 100,000 both ways. Ask the shop whether your address is in range.

### Is a monthly 50cc rental cheaper than a day rate?

Weekly and monthly packages are priced on their own, not as the day rate times the days. The 50cc monthly price is VND 2,000,000; see the prices page.

### Can I take the bike into my campus?

That depends on the university's rules. Ask the university or other students before you plan to ride in.

### What if I am under 18?

Sixteen is the minimum age for a moped, but the shop's conditions for younger renters differ, so ask before you book.
