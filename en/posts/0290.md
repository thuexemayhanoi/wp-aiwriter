Hai Ba Trung rewards a rider who treats it as a chain of short, planned stops: markets early in the day, parks and temples when the heat builds, museums on its northern edge and a guarded space at each one rather than a hunt for kerb. The streets are busy and many are narrow, and the parking rules in Law 36/2024/QH15 bite hardest in exactly the places visitors want to go, around market gates and along tight lanes. This guide explains how the 2025 ward changes affect names, how to handle Hom Market and Mo Market by scooter, which museums are realistic for a ride and how to avoid the parking mistakes that get a bike moved. It is general information as of October 2026, based on the sources listed at the end, and it is not live traffic advice.

## The district name has changed, the streets have not

Hai Ba Trung was one of Hanoi's four original urban districts. Under Resolution 1656/NQ-UBTVQH15, the district level ended on 1 July 2025, and the Hanoi culture department lists three new wards here: Hai Ba Trung, Vinh Tuy and Bach Mai. Vietnamnet's description of the plan says the new Hai Ba Trung ward includes the former Dong Nhan and Pho Hue wards and parts of Bach Dang, Le Dai Hanh, Nguyen Du, Thanh Nhan and Pham Dinh Ho.

You will still see old labels. One travel guide notes that Hom Market's old address, Ngo Thi Nham Ward, Hai Ba Trung District, is now "Hai Ba Trung Ward", and advises using the new name on maps and ride-hailing apps. If a pin looks odd, trust the street and number: 79 Hue Street for Hom Market, 459 Bach Mai for Mo Market.

## Hom Market: go early, park once

Hom Market on Hue Street is a traditional market with a wet and ready-goods floor and textile stalls upstairs. Two guides give its hours as roughly 06:00 to 18:00 daily, and one says the fabric floor is better visited late morning or early afternoon, when sellers bargain more calmly. A 2025 guide reports a parking lot at the market, with fees a little above the city average. Treat both hours and parking as things to confirm on arrival.

Market-gate streets are where parking rules matter most:

- **Do not stop in front of a gate with a vehicle entrance** or within 5 metres either side. Law 36, Article 18, lists this as a banned position, and it applies to organisation and office gates as well.
- **Do not park abreast of another stopped vehicle.** The same article bans parking parallel and in the same direction as another vehicle that is already stopped, which is the usual way a second row forms outside a market.
- **On a narrow street, stay clear of cars parked opposite.** Article 18 bans stopping within 20 metres of an oncoming parked car on a narrow city street.
- **Signal when you pull in and out,** and do not hinder pedestrians. Those are requirements in Article 18, not courtesies.

The simplest approach is to ride past the crowd, park in a lot or a signed bay a street or two away and walk in with a bag you can carry. ## Mo Market and Bach Mai Road

Mo Market, at 459 Bach Mai Street, is described by a Gtrip market guide as an unusual underground market beneath a modern commercial complex. That matters for riders in two ways. First, the entrance may be inside a larger building with its own vehicle access, so ask an attendant where to leave a bike. Second, Bach Mai is a long arterial road, so plan your stop on the side you will be riding on, to avoid a U-turn. Our [intersection guide](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/crossing-hanoi-intersections/) explains how locals handle turns across heavy traffic.

Mo Market is a better stop than Hom Market for a first-time rider who dislikes crowds, because you can park under cover and walk down rather than negotiating a gate. That is a reading of the description, not a promise, so look at the actual entrance before you commit.

## Parks, temples and small stops

Between markets, Hai Ba Trung has quieter places. One guide lists Thong Nhat Park, on Tran Nhan Tong in the Le Dai Hanh area, with opening hours of about 06:00 to 22:00, and notes Thien Quang Lake and the Thien Quang pagoda group nearby. Another guide describes Dong Nhan Temple, also called Hai Ba Trung Temple, as dedicated to the Trung sisters and dating to 1142. Vincom Center Ba Trieu, at 191 Ba Trieu, is the main mall on that avenue.

The practical pattern is to pick one cluster, park once and walk between its places. The park and the lake are close enough to treat as one stop, and the temple is best saved for another outing or reached by ride-hailing if you are tired.

## Museums: what is really within reach

Hai Ba Trung proper has fewer headline museums than its northern neighbour, and a ride-and-walk day usually crosses into Hoan Kiem ward. The clearest example is the Vietnam National Museum of History, at 216 Tran Quang Khai and 1 Trang Tien. Its official site gives opening times of 08:00 to 12:00 and 13:30 to 17:00. The site's "Opening time" page says it is open every day except the first Monday of each month, while the home page says "except the Monday", so confirm the day you plan to go.

Other museums such as the Vietnam Women's Museum and the Hanoi Police Museum are on Ly Thuong Kiet in Cua Nam ward. A 2026 visitor guidebook lists the police museum as open Tuesday to Friday, but it is a guide rather than the museum's own page. Because these sit near the old centre, use a single parking point and walk between them. ## Where parking rules turn into costs

Beyond the specific bans above, three other provisions of Law 36 are relevant in market areas.

**Pavements are for pedestrians.** Article 77 says roadways are used for traffic and pavements for people on foot, and temporary use for other purposes needs official permission. A bike on a pavement that is not a designated parking area is a problem even if the stalls beside it are using it.

**Illegal parking can be moved.** Article 69 lets traffic police move a vehicle parked in breach of the rules in a way that obstructs traffic or creates a risk, if the driver or owner is absent or refuses, and the owner or driver pays the cost. The article also says the police must keep the vehicle safe while moving it.

**Parking on a slope needs care.** Article 18 requires a vehicle parked on a slope to be turned towards the kerb and chocked. On a scooter, that means using the side or centre stand on level ground, and not leaving it on a ramp.

For fines, see [traffic fines that catch foreign riders out](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/traffic-fines-vietnam-foreign-riders/). This article quotes no amounts because they change with the decrees.

## The Ring Road 1 plan

Hai Ba Trung ward is one of the nine wards that a VnEconomy report on Hanoi's draft low-emission zone lists inside Ring Road 1. VOV World and Lao Dong reported that the pilot, from 1 July 2026, covers only parts of Hoan Kiem ward, with weekend evening limits on motor vehicles in its first zone, and that the zone is meant to widen to the whole of Ring Road 1 from 2028. As of October 2026, ordinary riding in Hai Ba Trung continues, but check recent notices before a long stay.

## A market-and-park morning

1. Collect the scooter and cross the river early, before the commuter peak.
2. Ride to Hom Market, park in a lot or marked bay a short walk away and browse.
3. Ride to Thong Nhat Park or Thien Quang Lake for a quiet hour.
4. Have lunch locally, then decide: museum on the Hoan Kiem side, or Mo Market on Bach Mai.
5. Return before the evening peak.

## FAQ: Hai Ba Trung by scooter

### Where should I park for Hom Market?

Use the market's own lot if it is open, or a signed bay a street away. Do not stop in front of a gate or abreast of another parked vehicle.

### Is Hai Ba Trung still a district?

No. The district level ended on 1 July 2025, and the area is now covered by Hai Ba Trung, Vinh Tuy and Bach Mai wards.

### Which museums can I reach easily?

The Vietnam National Museum of History at 216 Tran Quang Khai is the clearest option, on the Hoan Kiem side. Check its official opening times.

### Can police move my parked scooter?

Under Article 69 of Law 36, they can move a vehicle that is parked illegally and causes an obstruction, and the owner or driver pays.

### Is the Ring Road 1 plan in force in Hai Ba Trung?

As of October 2026, the pilot covers only parts of Hoan Kiem ward. The plan extends to the whole Ring Road 1 area later.
