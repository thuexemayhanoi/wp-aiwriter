Before you extend a monthly motorbike rental, settle the contract questions in writing while the current period is still running: what the price for the extra period will be, whether you keep the same bike and how it will be checked and serviced, what happens to the deposit, when you pay, whether your licence and stay cover the new dates and how the extension is recorded. Nguyen Ha's published terms say that any extension must be agreed before the existing period ends, including price and return arrangements, so the safe sequence is to ask early, get the answer in writing and treat the new period as agreed only once the shop has confirmed it.

A monthly rental is long enough for wear, small faults and changed circumstances to build up, and an extension is the natural moment to put them right. This guide lists the questions to ask, explains how a monthly extension differs from a short one, covers price, condition, deposit, documents and payment, shows what to put in writing and explains what happens if you do not agree an extension in time. It is general information, not legal advice.

## How a monthly extension differs from a short one

Extending a rental by a day or two is mostly about price and return time. Extending a monthly rental is larger, because you are renewing a longer commitment with more history behind it.

- **More has happened to the bike.** A month of use means a month of kilometres, wear and maybe repairs.

- **More has happened to you.** Your licence, visa, plans and budget may have changed.

- **More money is involved.** The extension may be another month, and the deposit has been held for a month already.

- **More can be negotiated.** A renewal is a chance to adjust the terms, though the shop decides.

Our guide to [how to ask for an extension on a motorbike rental](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-ask-for-an-extension-on-a-motorbike-rental/) covers the general request. This guide covers the contract questions that a monthly renewal adds. Our guide to [weekly and monthly motorbike rental in Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/weekly-monthly-motorbike-rental-hanoi/) explains what to ask when you first sign.

## What the shop publishes

As of October 2026, Nguyen Ha's published terms say these things that bear on an extension.

- **Any extension must be agreed before the existing period ends,** including price and return arrangements.

- **Reference rates for the manual group** are VND 150,000–200,000 per day, VND 700,000–800,000 per week and VND 1,000,000–1,200,000 per month. Packages are priced separately, and the exact price is confirmed on the actual bike, its condition and your dates before you sign.

- **A cash deposit** of VND 2,000,000–5,000,000, depending on the bike and agreement, is separate from the rent and is reconciled on return according to the recorded condition.

- **Late return** is VND 30,000 per hour for 1 to 5 hours, and one extra rental day at that bike's daily rate from 6 hours late. Telling the shop you are late does not automatically waive the charge.

- **A raincoat** may be provided on a monthly rental, but is not part of short ones.

- **Delivery and collection** are for eligible weekly or monthly rentals, within 10 km of Hoan Kiem Lake at VND 50,000 per trip or VND 100,000 for delivery and collection.

The published terms do not say how an extension of a monthly rental is priced, so ask.

## Question 1: what will the price be?

The extension price is the first thing to settle, and it is not automatic.

- **Is the price the same as the current month,** or will it change?

- **If I extend by less than a month,** is it priced weekly, daily or as a part-month? Packages are priced on their own, so a part-month may not be a simple fraction.

- **Is there any change in the daily rate** if I go over the month by a few days?

- **Does the new price apply to the same bike?**

- **Is anything else added or removed,** such as delivery, a raincoat or a second helmet?

Ask for the figure in writing, with the dates it covers. Our guide to [manual motorbike rental: daily versus monthly use cases](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/manual-motorbike-rental-daily-versus-monthly-use-cases/) and our guide to [choosing a rental period when your travel plans may change](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/choosing-a-rental-period-when-your-travel-plans-may-change/) explain how to weigh the options. Vietnam's Civil Code (Law No. 91/2015/QH13), in force since 1 January 2017, says rent is set by agreement (Article 473) and the term of a lease is set by agreement (Article 474). We read the lease articles in the text published by the Tuyen Quang Department of Justice. As of October 2026, that means the extension price and period are whatever you and the shop agree, so agree them clearly.

## Question 2: do I keep the same bike, and in what condition?

After a month of daily use, the bike has aged. An extension is a good moment to deal with that.

- **Will I keep the same bike,** or might the shop swap it?

- **When was the bike last serviced,** and is a service due? Honda's Wave RSX FI manual gives a chain check and lubrication interval of every 500 km, and a commuter covering about 20 km a day reaches that in roughly 25 days.

- **Are any small faults outstanding,** such as a noisy chain, a long brake pedal, a weak starter or a worn tyre? Our guides to [checking the rear brake on a Honda Wave](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/checking-the-rear-brake-on-a-honda-wave/), [how to notice a worn chain and sprocket set](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-notice-a-worn-chain-and-sprocket-set/) and [reading a Honda Wave tyre size and pressure label](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-read-a-honda-wave-tyre-size-and-pressure-label/) explain what to look at.

- **Will the shop inspect the bike** before the extension, and may it ask to do so?

- **Who pays for servicing and wear** during the extension?

Our guide to [what counts as normal wear on a rented Honda Wave](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/what-counts-as-normal-wear-on-a-rented-honda-wave/) explains how wear and damage are separated, and the Civil Code says the lessee is not responsible for natural wear (Article 479). Raise any faults now, so that they are recorded as wear and not mistaken for damage later.

## Question 3: what happens to the deposit?

Your cash deposit has been held for the first month. Ask what happens at renewal.

- **Does the existing deposit carry over** to the extension?

- **Will it change,** for example if the bike or the period changes?

- **Is the deposit returned and re-paid,** or kept as it is?

- **Will the condition record be updated,** and what will the deposit be reconciled against at the end?

The shop's pages say the deposit is reconciled on return according to the recorded condition and obligations, and returned once the bike has been checked against the handover record and any late or fuel charge has been settled. If you extend, ask which handover record applies: the original one, or a new one made at renewal. Our guide to [how to check deposit return conditions before signing](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-check-deposit-return-conditions-before-signing/) explains what to check.

## Question 4: a new handover record

A month is long enough for marks to appear. Consider asking for a fresh inspection and photographs at renewal, with the shop, so that the condition at the start of the extension is agreed. That draws a line under the first month and avoids arguments about what happened when.

- **Walk round the bike with staff,** and photograph every side, the tyres, the odometer and the fuel level. Our guides to [how to document existing scratches on a rental bike](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-document-existing-scratches-on-a-rental-bike/) and [how to record the fuel level at handover](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-record-the-fuel-level-at-handover/) explain how.

- **Note any new marks,** and who agrees they are wear or damage.

- **Ask for the record to be written on the contract** or an addendum.

A fresh record may mean the shop reviews the bike's condition against the original. That is fair, and it works both ways.

## Question 5: when do I pay?

- **When is the extension payment due:** now, at the end of the current period or on a schedule?

- **Which methods are accepted?** Our guide to [how to confirm payment methods before pickup](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-confirm-payment-methods-before-pickup/) explains how to ask, and the shop's pages do not list payment methods.

- **Will I get a receipt** for the extension? Our guide to [what should be written on a rental payment receipt](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/what-should-be-written-on-a-rental-payment-receipt/) explains what it should show.

- **What if I pay late?**

The Civil Code says the lessee must pay the rent in full and on time as agreed (Article 481), so a clear payment date matters.

## Question 6: do my documents cover the new dates?

A longer rental raises a question that a short one does not: will your papers still be valid at the end?

- **Your driving licence and IDP** must be valid and cover the class of bike for the whole period. Article 56 of the Law on Road Traffic Order and Safety (Law No. 36/2024/QH15) requires a valid licence that matches the vehicle. Check the expiry dates before you extend. Our guide to [licence rules for foreigners in Vietnam](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/motorbike-licence-rules-for-foreigners-in-vietnam/) explains what is needed.

- **Your visa or permitted stay** should cover the extension, because a rental that outlasts your stay is a problem.

- **The shop may ask to see updated documents** and to photograph them again. Our guide to [what identity documents a rental shop may ask to see](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/what-identity-documents-a-rental-shop-may-ask-to-see/) explains what to expect.

- **The bike's insurance certificate** should be valid for the extended dates, so ask the shop to confirm.

Under Article 32 of Decree 168/2024/ND-CP, which we read on the Government portal, an owner who lets a person who does not meet the licence conditions ride a motorbike can be fined, so a shop may refuse to extend if your licence has lapsed.

## Question 7: delivery, helmet and extras

- **If the bike was delivered,** will collection and any redelivery apply? The published delivery terms are for eligible weekly or monthly rentals.

- **Is a raincoat or extra item still provided?**

- **Does the helmet need replacing?** A month of use wears a helmet. Our guide to [what to do if a helmet does not fit at pickup](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/what-to-do-if-a-helmet-does-not-fit-at-pickup/) explains the checks.

## Question 8: what if I do not agree in time?

The published terms say an extension must be agreed before the existing period ends. If you do not, you risk the late-return charges, VND 30,000 per hour from the first hour and a full extra day from 6 hours, and the shop may not be able to keep the bike for you if it has been booked. The Civil Code says that if the lessee is late returning the property, the lessor may demand its return, rent for the period of delay and compensation, and that the lessee bears the risk during the delay (Article 482). So agree early, and confirm in writing. Our guide to [late rental returns: how to calculate the possible charge](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/late-rental-returns-how-to-calculate-the-possible-charge/) explains the arithmetic.

## What to put in writing

A short written confirmation, in a message or on the contract, should include:

- the bike and its plate number;

- the old end date and time, and the new end date and time;

- the extension price and what it includes;

- the payment date and method;

- what happens to the deposit;

- any service or inspection agreed;

- any change in delivery, extras or other terms.

For example: "Confirmed: the Honda Wave, plate [number], rental extended from [old date, time] to [new date, time]. Extension price [amount], payable [when and how]. Deposit unchanged. Chain service on [date]." Keep the reply. Prices and terms are on the [prices page](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/prices/), and Nguyen Ha can be reached on 0334 699 969 (+84 334 699 969), open 09:00 to 21:00 daily.

## A quick checklist

1. **Ask before the current period ends.**

2. **Agree the extension price and dates in writing.**

3. **Ask about the bike's service and condition.**

4. **Ask what happens to the deposit.**

5. **Consider a fresh handover record.**

6. **Agree payment timing and method, and get a receipt.**

7. **Check your licence, IDP, visa and the bike's insurance cover the new dates.**

8. **Keep the written confirmation.**

## FAQ: extending a monthly rental

### Do I have to agree an extension before the month ends?

Yes. Nguyen Ha's published terms say any extension must be agreed before the existing period ends, including price and return arrangements.

### Will the extension cost the same as the first month?

Not necessarily. The published terms do not say, so ask for the price in writing.

### What happens to my deposit?

Ask whether it carries over, and which handover record it will be reconciled against.

### Should the bike be inspected at renewal?

It is sensible. A fresh record, with photographs, draws a line under the first month.

### Does my licence need to cover the extension?

Yes. A valid licence matching the bike is required throughout, so check the expiry dates before you extend.

### What if I forget to ask?

Late-return charges apply from the first hour, and a full extra day from 6 hours late. Ask early.
