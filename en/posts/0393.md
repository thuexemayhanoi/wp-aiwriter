To book a motorbike rental in Hanoi on WhatsApp, message Nguyen Ha Motorbike Rental on +84 334 699 969 during opening hours, 09:00 to 21:00 Hanoi time, with your pickup and return dates, the kind of bike, how many bikes you need and where you are staying. The shop replies with the model actually available, the price for your dates and the deposit, and you collect the bike at the shop in Long Bien, where your documents are checked and the contract is signed. The chat is for agreeing the details, not for paying: the deposit is cash, paid at signing. As of October 2026 this is the published process; the contract you sign is what finally applies.

## What WhatsApp is good for, and what it is not

WhatsApp is a convenient way to settle the questions that decide a rental before you travel: whether a bike is free for your dates, what it will cost and what you need to bring. It works from abroad over Wi-Fi, which helps if you are still planning from another country, and you keep a written record of what was said.

It is not a substitute for the handover. The bike is checked in person, your documents are looked at and photographed for the contract at the shop, and the final price is confirmed on the actual bike before you sign. Think of the chat as the planning stage and the shop counter as the booking itself. If a message says a particular bike is yours, ask for that to be written down with the dates and the time until which it is held, because availability changes daily.

The shop's contact details are the same everywhere on the site: 0334 699 969 by phone or Zalo, and +84 334 699 969 on WhatsApp, which is the same number in international format. The [contact page](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/contact/) lists the address, Alley 5 Nguyen Van Cu Street, Bo De Ward, Long Bien District, near Gia Lam bus station.

## Check three things before you message

A short check saves a wasted conversation.

**1. Your licence class and permit.** Vietnam's Law on Road Traffic Order and Safety, Law 36/2024/QH15, has applied since 1 January 2025. Published summaries of Article 57 put class A1 at motorcycles up to 125 cc and class A above that. Foreign riders use their national licence together with an International Driving Permit from a country that belongs to the Vienna Convention; the Law says holders must carry both. Our [licence guide for foreign riders](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/motorbike-licence-rules-for-foreigners-in-vietnam/) covers the details, including which permit versions count.

**2. Your age.** The shop's own condition is that renters aged 20 and over bring a licence of the right class for any bike that requires one. Younger renters are handled case by case or not at all; see the [FAQ](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/faq/).

**3. Your documents.** Bring your passport or identity card and your driving licence with its permit. Our guide to [documents to carry when riding in Vietnam](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/documents-to-carry-riding-in-vietnam/) explains what to keep on you once you ride.

## A message you can copy

A complete first message gets a complete reply. Use this structure and change the details:

> Hello, I would like to rent a motorbike. Pickup: Saturday 7 November, about 10:00. Return: Tuesday 10 November, about 10:00 (3 days). Bike type: automatic scooter, one bike, one rider. I have a driving licence for 125 cc and an International Driving Permit. I am staying in Hoan Kiem. Please confirm the model, the price for these dates and the deposit.

Why each part matters:

- **Dates and times.** A rental day is 24 hours from the handover time, and shorter use still counts as a day, so the pickup time sets when the bike is due back.
- **Bike type.** Say manual, automatic, 50cc or electric. You may name a model, but it is confirmed when you book, not promised in advance. The [motorbikes page](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/motorbikes/) explains the groups.
- **Number of bikes and riders.** It affects availability and helmets, since one basic helmet per rider is included.
- **Licence statement.** Saying what you hold avoids a wasted trip.
- **Where you are staying.** It only matters if you want delivery, which is possible for weekly and monthly rentals only.

Write times in Hanoi time, which is seven hours ahead of UTC, and say so if your flight or plans cross time zones.

## Payment and the deposit: what to expect

The published process is simple. The deposit is cash, VND 2,000,000 to 5,000,000 depending on the bike and the length of the rental, and it is paid together with the rent when you sign the contract at the shop. Documents are checked and photographed for the contract and returned straight away; the shop does not keep a passport in place of a deposit and does not describe the service as deposit-free.

That means there is normally no reason to send money through a chat before you arrive. If you are asked to transfer money, pay a fee to hold a bike or send a deposit to a bank account or a different number, stop and ask the shop to confirm the arrangement in writing at the number on the contact page. Do not pay until you have heard it from a verified channel.

## Staying safe on WhatsApp

Booking by chat is safe when you know you are talking to the right person. WhatsApp's own help centre lists warning signs to pause on: an unknown number, someone rushing you, someone asking you to trust them, or a request to transfer money or share a password, PIN or personal information. If you cannot verify the contact, it advises not sharing personal or financial information, and blocking and reporting the user.

Practical steps for a rental booking:

1. **Get the number from the shop's own contact page**, not from a social media comment or a message that reached you first. The only phone number for this shop on this site is 0334 699 969 (+84 334 699 969).
2. **Look at the profile.** A business may show as a WhatsApp Business account, and some carry a verified badge, which WhatsApp says means Meta verified the business. A missing badge is not proof of a problem, and a badge does not replace the other checks.
3. **Never share a verification code.** A registration code sent to your phone is for you alone, whoever asks.
4. **Do not send passport photos or bank details until you need to.** The documents are checked at handover, so you do not need to send scans in advance. If the shop asks for something, ask why and how it will be used.
5. **Keep the chat.** It is your record of the price, the model and the times that were agreed.

WhatsApp says chats between people are end-to-end encrypted, and it treats chats with businesses that use the WhatsApp Business app as encrypted by default; once the business receives the message, its own privacy practices apply.

## If you need more than one bike, or delivery

For a group, say how many bikes and riders you have and whether you are all licensed for the class you want. Availability is the limiting factor, so ask early and say how flexible you are on model.

Delivery is offered only for weekly and monthly rentals. Within 10 km of Hoan Kiem Lake the fee is VND 50,000 per trip, or VND 100,000 for delivery and collection together; beyond 10 km it is agreed in advance. The shop does not deliver to or collect from Noi Bai airport. Send the exact address so the distance can be confirmed. Our guide to [weekly and monthly rentals](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/weekly-monthly-motorbike-rental-hanoi/) lists what longer renters should ask.

## Changes, delays and cancellations

The published terms cover late and early returns, not changes to a booking made by chat. A return one to five hours late costs VND 30,000 per hour, and from six hours late one more day is charged. Rent for unused time is not refunded if you return early. Because the written terms do not describe cancelling a chat arrangement, ask directly: "What happens if my flight is delayed or I need to change the date?" and keep the answer in the chat.

If you plan to ride beyond Hanoi, say so in your first message. Our guide to [taking a rental motorbike out of Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/take-rental-motorbike-out-of-hanoi/) lists the questions to settle, and the shop will suggest a suitable bike.

## On the day you collect the bike

Bring your passport or identity card, your driving licence and your International Driving Permit, plus the cash deposit and the rent. Expect to check the bike with the staff before signing; our [10-minute inspection checklist](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/rental-scooter-inspection-checklist/) shows what to test. Photograph any marks, note the fuel level and write down the handover time. Check your travel cover too; our guide to [what travel insurance usually does not cover](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/travel-insurance-motorbike-rental-vietnam/) explains why the licence class matters.

## FAQ: booking a Hanoi motorbike rental on WhatsApp

### Does a WhatsApp message reserve a bike?

Availability is confirmed when you book, and the model is not promised in advance. Ask the shop to state in the chat which bike is held for you, for which dates and until what time.

### Do I pay anything through WhatsApp?

The published process is a cash deposit and the rent paid at signing in the shop. If anyone asks for a transfer before you arrive, verify it with the shop through the number on the contact page first.

### Can I send my documents in advance?

The shop checks and photographs documents at handover. You do not need to send scans to book, and you should only send personal documents if you are sure who you are talking to and why they are needed.

### What if I write outside opening hours?

The shop answers between 09:00 and 21:00 Hanoi time. Send your message with full details and expect a reply when it reopens.

### Can I book by phone or Zalo instead?

Yes. The same number, 0334 699 969, takes phone calls and Zalo messages, and +84 334 699 969 is the WhatsApp format.
