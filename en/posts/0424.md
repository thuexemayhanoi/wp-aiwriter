A rental motorbike needs to be collected whenever it is unsafe or impossible to ride: a brake or steering fault, a damaged wheel, a fuel leak, an engine that will not start, a lost key, a bike that has been in an accident or one that has been in flood water. In those cases, stop, keep yourself and the bike safe, call the shop with the plate number and your exact location and ask what it will do. Do not ride on to "make it back", and do not pay a stranger to tow the bike without the shop's agreement.

Whether the shop collects the bike, sends a mechanic or tells you to use a nearby workshop depends on the fault, the distance and its own arrangements. This guide explains when collection is the right call, how to tell it apart from a fault you can fix on the spot, how to keep the bike safe while you wait, how to hand it over to the right person, what to ask about cost and time and what happens to your rental while it is out of service. It is general information, not a promise about any particular shop.

## When collection is the right call

Some faults mean the bike must not be ridden at all. Others can be dealt with on the spot. The table helps you decide.

| Situation | Ride on? | Usual next step |
|---|---|---|
| Brakes do not hold, or the pedal or lever goes to the end of its travel | No | Stop, call the shop, ask for collection |
| Steering is loose, bent or stiff, or a wheel wobbles | No | Stop, call the shop, ask for collection |
| Tyre cut, bulging or badly damaged, or a bent rim | No | Collection or a workshop, with the shop's agreement |
| Simple puncture near a repair stall | Not on the flat | Ask the shop, then repair at a stall |
| Chain off, broken or hanging | No | Collection |
| Fuel or oil leak | No | Collection |
| Engine will not start, or stalls repeatedly | No | Short checks, then collection if it persists |
| Flat battery, bike will not turn over | No | Advice by phone, then collection or a jump |
| Lost key or smart fob | Cannot | Collection or a spare key, with the shop |
| Accident with damage or injury | No | Safety first, then collection |
| Bike went through deep water and stalled | No | Do not keep cranking, ask for collection |
| Lights failed after dark | Not unlit | Stop in a lit place, then collection |

If you are unsure, treat it as a collection case and ask. ## Step 1: stop and make the scene safe

Pull the bike completely off the carriageway if you can do so safely, switch off the engine, take the key, put the bike on its stand on firm ground and move yourself away from the traffic. Under Article 18 of the Law on Road Traffic Order and Safety (Law No. 36/2024/QH15), in force since 1 January 2025, a driver whose vehicle stops because of a technical fault and is left partly on the carriageway, or where stopping is not allowed, must warn other road users with hazard lights or a warning sign behind the vehicle. Many small bikes have no hazard switch, so moving the bike off the road is the practical answer.

If the bike has been in an accident and someone is hurt, the first priority is people. Our guides to [what to do after a minor collision on a rental scooter](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/what-to-do-after-a-minor-collision-on-a-rental-scooter/) and [what to do after a serious accident on a rental motorbike](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/what-to-do-after-a-serious-accident-on-a-rental-motorbike/) cover the steps.

## Step 2: call the shop with the right information

Call Nguyen Ha on 0334 699 969 (+84 334 699 969), open 09:00 to 21:00 daily. Give clear facts:

- your name and the plate number;

- your exact location, with a map pin or a landmark and a street name;

- what the problem is, in one or two sentences;

- whether the bike can be moved or is in a dangerous place;

- whether you or anyone else is hurt;

- whether you have the key;

- whether you are in a guarded car park or on the roadside.

Send a photograph of the bike and the problem. If the shop is closed, send a message and call when it opens. For an emergency, call 112, the national emergency number set up by Decree 200/2025/ND-CP, which is free, answers 24 hours a day and is linked with the police, fire and rescue and medical emergency centres.

## Step 3: ask what the shop will do

The shop may do one of several things, and you should ask which.

- **Send someone with a vehicle** to collect the bike.

- **Send a mechanic** to look at it where it stands.

- **Bring a spare key** or a spare part.

- **Ask you to take it to a workshop** it names, and agree the cost.

- **Send a replacement bike** to you, and collect the first one later.

- **Ask you to leave it** in a guarded place until it can be collected.

Ask how long it will take, whether anyone will call you before arriving and what you should do in the meantime. Nguyen Ha's published terms do not describe a standard recovery procedure or a fixed fee for collecting a broken-down bike. They do describe delivery arrangements for weekly and monthly rentals, within 10 km of Hoan Kiem Lake at VND 50,000 per trip or VND 100,000 both ways, but that is a delivery service, not a breakdown recovery charge, so do not assume the same rate applies. Ask what applies to your case.

## Step 4: keep the bike safe while you wait

- **Leave it in a guarded place** if one is near. If it is in a car park, tell the attendant that the shop's staff will collect it.

- **Stay with it** if the bike is on a roadside or in an open area and the wait is short, or if it contains valuables you cannot take.

- **Lock the steering** if the key works, and take the key.

- **Take your own belongings** and documents with you.

- **Do not leave valuables on the bike,** such as a phone, passport or cash. Honda's manual advises against valuables in the storage compartment.

- **Photograph the bike where it stands,** with the plate and surroundings visible.

Our guide to [parking a motorbike in Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/parking-a-motorbike-in-hanoi/) explains guarded lots.

## Step 5: hand the bike over to the right person

Someone will arrive to collect the bike. Make sure it is the right person.

1. **Ask the shop who is coming,** and ask for a name, a description and the plate number or type of the vehicle.

2. **Ask the arriving person** to confirm your name and the bike's plate number.

3. **Call the shop to confirm** if you have any doubt. Use the number you already have, not one the arriving person gives you.

4. **Do not hand over the bike or the key to anyone** who cannot be identified by the shop.

5. **Take photographs** of the bike and the collector's vehicle as it leaves.

6. **Hand over the key and any equipment,** such as the helmet, and note it.

7. **Ask for the time and place of the handover** to be confirmed by message.

A call-back to the shop on a number you already know protects both you and the shop. Treat anyone who offers to "take the bike for repair" without the shop's introduction with suspicion.

## What happens to your rental time

This is a common worry, and the answer depends on the shop and the cause. Ask these questions.

- **Will the rental clock stop** while the bike is out of service?

- **Will you receive a replacement bike,** and on what terms?

- **If no bike is available,** will the rent be adjusted, or the rental ended?

- **What happens to the deposit?**

- **Does anything change if the fault is not your doing?**

The published terms say that a rental day is 24 hours and that early return does not refund unused rent. Those are the standard terms for a normal rental, and a breakdown that is not your fault may be a different case, so ask for the answer in writing. Vietnam's Civil Code (Law No. 91/2015/QH13), in force since 1 January 2017, says that if a leased property has a defect that is not the lessee's fault, the lessee may ask the lessor to repair it, reduce the rent or exchange it, and in some cases terminate the contract. We read the lease articles in the text published by the Tuyen Quang Department of Justice, and as of October 2026 the contract fills in the details. This is general information, not legal advice.

## Cost: who pays for collection

Cost depends on the cause.

- **If the fault is not your doing,** such as a defect or ordinary wear, collection is normally part of the shop's duty to keep the bike fit for use. Ask the shop to confirm.

- **If the problem arose from your use,** such as a drop, an accident or flood damage, the contract may make you responsible for repair and recovery costs.

- **If it is unclear,** ask the shop to explain its decision and to write it down.

Do not agree to pay a collection fee on the roadside without understanding what it covers. Keep every message and receipt. Our guide to [how to keep receipts for repairs on a rental motorbike](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-keep-receipts-for-repairs-on-a-rental-motorbike/) explains what to keep, and our explanation of [what the clauses in a Hanoi rental contract mean](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/hanoi-motorbike-rental-contract-explained/) covers the damage and breakdown wording.

## If you are far from Hanoi

On a trip outside the city, collection is harder and slower. Before you go, ask how a breakdown would be handled, as our guides to [whether you can take a Hanoi rental motorbike out of the city](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/take-rental-motorbike-out-of-hanoi/) and [whether to rent a manual bike for touring outside Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/should-you-rent-a-manual-bike-for-touring-outside-hanoi/) explain. If a breakdown happens, tell the shop where you are, find somewhere safe and shaded to wait and ask what the shop would like you to do. It may be easier to arrange a local workshop or to leave the bike with a trusted guard or owner.

2. **Call the shop with the plate, location and problem.**

3. **Ask what the shop will do, and how long it will take.**

## FAQ: when a rental bike needs collecting

### When should I ask the shop to collect the bike?

Whenever it is unsafe or impossible to ride: faulty brakes or steering, a damaged wheel, a leak, a bike that will not start, a lost key, an accident or flood water.

### Will the shop charge for collection?

Nguyen Ha's published terms do not set a breakdown recovery fee. Ask what applies to your case and get it in writing.

### Is the delivery fee the same as a collection fee?

No. The published delivery fee applies to delivery arrangements for weekly and monthly rentals, not to recovering a broken-down bike.

### Who should I hand the bike to?

Only someone the shop has identified to you. Call the shop on a number you already know if in doubt.

### Does my rental time stop?

Ask. A breakdown that is not your fault may be treated differently from an ordinary early return, and the answer should be in writing.

### What if I am outside Hanoi?

Tell the shop where you are, keep the bike safe and ask what it wants you to do. Collection may be slower.
