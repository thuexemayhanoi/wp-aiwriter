A rental payment receipt should show who was paid and by whom, the date and time, the exact bike and its plate number, the rental period with start and end times, each amount on its own line, with the rent and the deposit kept separate, the payment method and a signature or stamp from the shop. It should also say what happens to the deposit and when. That is a short list, but each item answers a question that tends to come up later: which bike was it, how long was it for, how much was rent and how much was security, who took the cash and when will I get the deposit back.

Nguyen Ha's published pages explain the deposit and the charges but do not describe a standard receipt format, so this is something to ask for at the counter and check before you leave. This guide sets out what a good receipt contains, gives a sample layout, explains how it fits with the rental contract, shows how to handle a shop that gives no receipt and explains how to keep your records. It is general information, not accounting or legal advice.

## Why a receipt matters

A receipt is evidence of a payment, and a rental involves several. You pay the rent. You pay a cash deposit, which is meant to come back. You may pay extra charges for a late return, an empty tank or an extension. Without a record of each, two things go wrong. You cannot show what you paid if there is a disagreement, and you cannot show what the shop owes you at the end.

Nguyen Ha's pages say that the deposit is cash, is paid separately from the rental charge and is reconciled on return according to the recorded condition and obligations. They also say the deposit comes back once the bike has been checked against the handover record and any late or fuel charge has been settled. For that to work, the amount of the deposit must be on record. A receipt, or a line on the contract, is where it is recorded. Our guide to [understanding the difference between rent and deposit](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/understanding-the-difference-between-rent-and-deposit/) explains why they should never be mixed.

## The contract and the receipt

A receipt does not replace the rental contract. The contract sets the terms. The receipt proves the money. Many shops combine them, with the payment noted on the contract itself, and that is fine as long as the payment details are clear. Nguyen Ha's pages say that published rates are not a replacement for the written agreement made for a particular bike and rental period, and that the confirmed vehicle and the written contract determine the actual amount.

If your shop uses a single sheet, check that it contains the receipt items below. If it uses two papers, check that they match. Our guide to [reading a motorbike rental agreement in English](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/reading-a-motorbike-rental-agreement-in-english/) explains how to read the contract, and our explanation of [what the clauses in a Hanoi rental contract mean](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/hanoi-motorbike-rental-contract-explained/) covers the money clauses.

## What the receipt should show

| Item | What to look for | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| The shop | Name, address and phone | Shows who received the money |
| Your name | As on your passport or ID | Shows who paid |
| Date and time | Of the payment | Fixes when it happened |
| The bike | Make, model, colour and plate number | Shows which bike the payment is for |
| The period | Start and end date and time | Shows the rental, and when it is due back |
| The rent | Rate, number of periods, total | Shows what the rent was |
| The deposit | Amount, on its own line, marked refundable | Shows the security, and that it is separate |
| Other charges | Delivery, extras, any fee, each on a line | Shows nothing is hidden |
| The total paid | The sum of the lines | Lets you check the arithmetic |
| Payment method | Cash, card or transfer | Shows how it was paid |
| Deposit return | When and how it will be returned | Avoids later argument |
| Signature or stamp | From the shop, ideally also yours | Shows the paper is genuine |

Not every receipt will have every item, and a small shop's receipt may be handwritten. But the more of these items are present, the safer you are.

## A sample layout

This is an invented layout to show how the lines can be arranged. The brackets stand for details you should expect to see. The figures are placeholders, and your own receipt should show the real ones.

**Rental payment receipt**

- Shop: [name], [address], [phone]

- Renter: [full name], passport or ID no. [number]

- Date and time of payment: [date], [time]

- Bike: [make and model], [colour], plate [number]

- Rental period: from [date, time] to [date, time]

- Rent: [rate] × [number of days or period] = [amount] VND

- Cash deposit (refundable): [amount] VND

- Other charges: [description] [amount] VND

- Total received: [amount] VND

- Paid by: cash

- The deposit is returned at the end of the rental once the bike has been checked against the handover record and any late or fuel charge has been settled.

- Signed: [shop staff name and signature]; [renter signature]

Photograph the receipt as soon as it is handed to you. If the layout is different, the important thing is that the same facts appear.

## Keep rent and deposit on separate lines

This is the most important single habit. If a receipt shows only "total paid", you cannot tell how much of it is the refundable deposit. When the time comes to return the bike, the shop and you may disagree about what was paid as security. Two separate lines remove the doubt.

Also check that the deposit line says it is a deposit, not "rent" or "payment". If the shop writes only a total, ask for it to be split, and ask for the word "deposit" and the amount. ## Record other payments as they happen

During the rental, you may pay more.

- **An extension.** The shop's pages say any extension must be agreed before the existing period ends, including price and return arrangements. Ask for the new end date and the extra amount to be written down. Our guide to [how to ask for an extension on a motorbike rental](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-ask-for-an-extension-on-a-motorbike-rental/) explains how.

- **A late-return charge,** at VND 30,000 per hour for 1 to 5 hours, or one extra rental day from 6 hours late. Ask for it to be itemised.

- **An empty-tank charge** of VND 20,000, if it applies.

- **A delivery fee,** of VND 50,000 per trip or VND 100,000 for delivery and collection within 10 km of Hoan Kiem Lake, for eligible weekly or monthly rentals.

- **A repair or a replacement,** if the shop asks you to pay.

Each should have its own record, with the date, the amount and the reason. At return, go through all the receipts together. Our guide to [returning your rental motorbike in Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/returning-your-rental-motorbike-hanoi/) explains the return routine.

## When the deposit comes back

The deposit return is a payment too, and it should be recorded. When you get it back, ask for a note or a line on the contract that says the deposit was returned in full, with the date and the amount, and that the rental is settled. Count the money before you leave. If a deduction is made, ask for it to be itemised and for the reason to be written down. Keep your copy of the contract showing that it was settled. ## If the shop does not give a receipt

Some small businesses do not issue formal receipts automatically. Do not leave without a record.

1. **Ask politely for a written receipt,** or for the payment to be written on the contract.

2. **If the shop uses a notebook,** ask for the entry to be written and signed, and photograph it.

3. **If nothing is offered,** write your own note of the date, the amount, what it was for and the bike, and ask the staff member to sign or initial it. Photograph it.

4. **Send a message** to the shop confirming what you paid, such as "Thank you, I paid the deposit of [amount] today for the Wave, plate [number]". Keep the reply.

5. **Photograph the handover** with the cash visible, if the staff agree, though a signed note is stronger.

A message exchange on the shop's own number is a record. It is not as strong as a signed receipt, but it is far better than nothing.

## What the law says about paying rent

Vietnam's Civil Code (Law No. 91/2015/QH13), in force since 1 January 2017, says the lessee must pay the full rent on time as agreed, and that the rent is set by agreement (Articles 473 and 481). We read the lease articles in the text published by the Tuyen Quang Department of Justice. As of October 2026 that is the general rule, and what the parties agreed is what counts, so a receipt that records the agreed amounts and dates is the best evidence of what you paid. This is general information, not legal or accounting advice.

## Keep your records

- **Photograph every receipt** and every signed paper.

- **Store the photographs** in one folder, and back them up.

- **Keep the paper copies** flat and dry.

- **Keep them until the deposit has been returned** and for a few weeks afterwards, as fines and queries can arrive late.

2. **Your name, and the date and time.**

4. **The rental period with start and end times.**

5. **Rent and deposit on separate lines.**

## FAQ: rental payment receipts

### Do I need a receipt for a cash deposit?

Yes. It is the proof of the amount, and the deposit is meant to be returned.

### Should rent and deposit be on separate lines?

Yes. A single total makes it hard to show how much was refundable security.

### What if the shop only writes on the contract?

That is fine if the payment, date, amount and bike are clear. Photograph the page, including the signature.

### What if the shop gives no receipt?

Ask for a signed note, write your own, photograph it and send a confirming message to the shop's number.

### Should extra charges be recorded?

Yes. Each late, fuel, delivery or extension charge should have its own line with the date and reason.

### How long should I keep it?

Until the deposit has been returned, and for a few weeks afterwards.
