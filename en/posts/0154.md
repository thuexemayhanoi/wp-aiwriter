A premium scooter adds a short list of real things and leaves several expected ones out. On Honda Vietnam's published figures for the SH160i and SH125i, the extras over a mid-range scooter are larger 16-inch wheels, a longer wheelbase, a 7-litre tank, ABS on both wheels on the higher versions, traction control on the ABS versions, a colour TFT display, a front compartment with a USB Type C socket and a smart key with an alarm. It does not add a bigger box than a Lead, an easier time in narrow lanes, lower risk of theft or any change to your responsibilities as a renter. This guide goes through what a premium scooter genuinely changes, what it does not, how to judge whether the difference is worth paying for, and what to ask the shop. It uses Honda's pages for the SH, Air Blade and Lead as the yardstick.

## What "premium" means in this guide

In Vietnam, "premium" in scooters usually means the Honda SH family, with a few rivals. It sits above the everyday models, such as the Vision, Lead and Air Blade, in price and finish. The shop's price page lists the automatic scooters it expects to offer, and the bike actually available is confirmed at booking, so a premium model may not always be on hand. This article is a guide to the trade, not a promise of any particular bike. If a premium scooter is on offer, you can use the list below to judge what you are paying for.

## The measurable extras

Honda Vietnam's SH160i/125i page and its Air Blade and Lead pages give comparable numbers, summarised here.

| Feature | SH160i/125i (2026) | Air Blade 160/125 | Lead |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kerb weight | About 133 to 134 kg | About 113 kg | About 114 kg |
| Seat height | 799 mm | 775 mm | 760 mm |
| Wheelbase | 1,353 mm | 1,286 mm | 1,273 mm |
| Wheels | 16 inch, front and rear | 14 inch | 12 inch front, 10 inch rear |
| Fuel tank | 7 litres | 4.4 litres | 6.0 litres |
| ABS | Both wheels on Sport, Special and Premium versions | Front wheel | Front wheel on the special version |
| Under-seat storage | Up to 28 litres | 23.2 litres | Over 37 litres on Special and Premium versions |

Honda's descriptions of versions change from year to year, so the figures are guides for the current models. An older bike that a shop hands you may differ.

### Larger wheels and a longer wheelbase

The SH's 16-inch wheels and longer wheelbase are the heart of the premium ride. In general, bigger wheels roll over bumps more smoothly, and a longer wheelbase makes a bike steadier at speed and with two people. That is a general engineering point and not a Honda measurement, but it explains why many riders find the SH calmer on rough surfaces and longer roads.

The cost is size. A kerb weight of about 133 kg is roughly 20 kg more than the Air Blade or Lead, and the seat at 799 mm is higher. Many riders will notice this when they walk the bike backwards out of a parking row.

### A bigger tank

A 7-litre tank is the largest in this comparison. Honda gives test consumption of about 2.33 to 2.34 litres per 100 km for the SH, a laboratory figure that real riding will exceed. In practice, a larger tank means fewer fuel stops on a day trip, and a bigger safety margin. For the other models, the 4.4-litre Air Blade tank needs more planning, as our guide on [Air Blade comfort on longer rides](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/honda-air-blade-for-longer-rides-comfort-trade-offs/) explains.

### Two-channel ABS on higher versions

Honda says ABS fitted to both wheels is on the Sport, Special and Premium versions of the SH, and that the system helps the rider stay in control when braking hard or on slippery surfaces. The Standard version is not listed with it. The Air Blade page lists front-wheel ABS. The word "premium" on a rental listing does not tell you whether this particular bike has ABS, so ask which version it is.

Honda also says its HSTC traction control, which reduces rear wheel slip when you accelerate, is fitted on the ABS versions. It is a safety aid, not a licence to ride faster on wet roads.

### A display and smart key

The 2026 SH is the first Honda Vietnam model with a TFT display, in colour at 4.2 inches, and Honda says the SH160 version connects to a smartphone by Bluetooth. The SH also has a front compartment with a USB Type C socket and a smart-key system with a locate function and an anti-theft alarm. These are convenience features. The Air Blade and Lead also have a smart key and USB Type C sockets on current versions, so they are less of a premium difference than they once were.

## What a premium scooter does not add

### A bigger box

The SH's under-seat storage is up to 28 litres, according to Honda. The Lead's is over 37 litres on its Special and Premium versions and the Air Blade's is 23.2 litres. For errands and luggage, the Lead can beat the SH. Our guide to [Honda Lead for daily errands](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/honda-lead-for-daily-errands-practical-rental-checklist/) covers the details.

### Easier city parking

A heavier, longer bike is harder to park in the narrow bays and tilted pavements around Hoan Kiem Lake and the Old Quarter. Our guide to [riding in the Old Quarter](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/riding-in-hanoi-old-quarter/) explains why a lighter scooter suits the centre, and the guide to [parking a motorbike in Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/parking-a-motorbike-in-hanoi/) explains lots and tickets.

### Lower risk of theft or damage

A premium scooter draws more attention, and a scratch or a stolen mirror costs more to fix. The smart-key alarm helps, but it does not replace parking in a guarded lot. The shop does not provide an extra lock, so lock the steering and choose guarded places. Our guide to [protecting items in an under-seat compartment](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/protecting-items-in-a-scooter-under-seat-compartment/) explains why valuables stay with you.

### A different set of rules or cover

A premium bike is still a rented scooter, and the contract still sets out who pays for what. Engine size can affect the licence class, and cover under a travel policy may depend on the bike, so check both before you book. Our guides to [licence rules for foreigners](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/motorbike-licence-rules-for-foreigners-in-vietnam/) and [travel cover for rentals](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/travel-insurance-motorbike-rental-vietnam/) explain where to look.

### A guarantee of better service

Nothing about a higher price list changes the basics at the shop. Honda's features do not substitute for a good handover. One basic helmet per rider is included free, but raincoats are not included for short rentals and no extra lock is provided, whatever the bike.

## When the extras are worth it

The premium features pay off when your trip uses them.

- **Two people or long days on the road.** The longer wheelbase, bigger wheels and larger tank matter more.
- **Rough or patched roads** outside the centre, where larger wheels help.
- **Wet weather riding,** when ABS on both wheels adds margin, if the bike has it.
- **A taller or larger rider** who finds small scooters cramped.
- **A comfort-first trip,** such as a relaxed week with a partner.

They do not pay off when your day is mostly parking and walking in the old city, with one rider, short hops and a day pack. In that case a Vision or Lead is lighter and easier. Our comparison of the [Honda SH as a premium rental](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/honda-sh-rental-hanoi-worth-it/) takes the SH in more detail, and the guide to [Honda Vision versus Air Blade](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/honda-vision-vs-air-blade-which-scooter-to-rent-in-hanoi/) compares the mid-range pair.

## A simple way to decide

Ask yourself five questions:

1. **How long will I ride each day?** Under two hours around the centre points to a lighter bike.
2. **Will I carry a passenger or luggage?** If yes, the bigger bike or the bigger box helps.
3. **Do I need ABS?** If so, ask which versions the shop has.
4. **Can I handle 130 kg or more on a stand?** If not, choose lighter.
5. **Is the difference in price worth these features for my trip?** The [prices page](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/prices/) shows current ranges.

If your answers lean toward lighter, cheaper and easier to park, a mid-range scooter is the better rental. If they lean toward comfort, stability and range, a premium model adds something real.

## FAQ: premium scooter rental

### Is a premium scooter safer?

It can be, if it has ABS on both wheels and you ride within your limits, but size and weight make it less forgiving in tight spaces. Safety depends on the version, your skill and the conditions.

### Does a premium scooter need a different licence?

Engine size can change the licence class, so check the exact bike. The SH comes in 125 cc and 160 cc forms, as Honda's page shows.

### Is the SH faster than an Air Blade?

Honda lists maximum power of 9.6 kW for the SH125i and 12.4 kW for the SH160i, but a city scooter's speed is limited by traffic and rules, so the difference is mostly felt in reserve.

### Will a premium scooter carry more luggage?

Not necessarily. Honda lists up to 28 litres under the SH seat, against over 37 litres on some Lead versions.

### Is a premium rental worth it for a short visit?

For a short city stay, usually not. For a long, comfortable ride with a passenger, it can be.

### Is a premium scooter available at Nguyen Ha?

The shop lists automatic models such as the Vision, Air Blade, Click and Lead. Ask whether any premium model is available for your dates; the bike actually available is confirmed at booking.
