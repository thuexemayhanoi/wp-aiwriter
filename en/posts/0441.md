If the bike you reserved is unavailable, do not pay or sign for a substitute until you understand what you are being offered: ask why it is unavailable, ask for the closest equivalent in writing with its exact model, condition and price for your dates, compare it with your original plan and decline if it does not suit you. Nguyen Ha's published pages warn that a model mentioned online may not be available for your dates and that the exact price is confirmed on the actual bike, its condition and your dates before you sign, so a change of bike at the counter is a possibility the shop itself describes.

What matters is how it is handled: openly, with a comparable bike, a clear price and a written record. This guide explains why a reserved bike can be unavailable, what to ask in the first minutes, how to judge a substitute, what to do about any advance payment, what the Civil Code says about delivery of the agreed property and what your options are if nothing suitable is offered. It is general information, not legal advice.

## Why a reserved bike can be unavailable

A rental fleet is shared among many renters, and bikes come and go.

- **A previous renter returned late,** or did not return, and the bike is not back.

- **The bike is being repaired** or serviced, or was found unfit after the last rental.

- **The model you saw online** was an example of the group, not a guarantee. Nguyen Ha's pages say that a model mentioned online may not be available for your preferred dates, and that the bike actually available is confirmed at booking.

- **A booking was mixed up,** or a message was missed.

- **A change of class** is needed because no bike in the class is free.

None of these is necessarily a sign of bad faith. The question is how the shop deals with it. Our guide to [how to confirm the exact model you will receive](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-confirm-the-exact-model-you-will-receive/) explains how to reduce the risk beforehand.

## What the shop publishes

As of October 2026, the published terms say:

- **The exact price is confirmed** on the actual bike, its condition and your dates before you sign.

- **Ask the shop to confirm** the exact vehicle, engine capacity, battery requirements, accessories and condition before paying.

- **A model mentioned online** may not be available for your preferred dates.

- **A cash deposit** of VND 2,000,000–5,000,000 is paid separately from the rental charge, normally when the contract is signed.

The pages do not describe what the shop does if a reserved bike is unavailable, or whether a booking payment is refundable, so ask. Do not assume either way.

## In the first minutes: ask and note

When you hear that the bike is unavailable, stay calm and ask:

1. **Why is it unavailable,** and for how long?

2. **Is there an equivalent bike,** of the same class or better?

3. **What exactly is it:** make, model, engine size, year, colour?

4. **What is its condition,** and when was it last serviced?

5. **What will it cost** for my dates, compared with the original quote?

6. **Is the deposit the same?**

7. **Will the rental period and pickup time stay the same?**

8. **Can I have this in writing?**

Write down the answers, or ask for them by message. Nguyen Ha can be reached on 0334 699 969 (+84 334 699 969), open 09:00 to 21:00 daily. If you are still travelling, message from where you are so that there is a record.

## How to judge a substitute

A substitute is fair if it serves the same purpose for the same price, and unfair if it is worse, dearer or both.

| Check | Questions | Fair signs | Warning signs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Class | Same group as I booked? | Same or better | A smaller or older class at the same price |
| Model | Exact model and engine size | Named and shown | "A Honda" and no detail |
| Condition | Age, mileage, last service | Clear answers | Vague or defensive |
| Price | For my dates | Same or lower | Higher with no reason |
| Deposit | Same amount? | Same | Raised without explanation |
| Suitability | Fits my plan, load and riders | Yes | Not suited to your trip |
| Handover | Will I get a record and photographs? | Yes | Rushed |

For a manual bike, the published group covers the Honda Wave, Yamaha Sirius and Honda Blade, with reference rates of VND 150,000–200,000 per day, VND 700,000–800,000 per week and VND 1,000,000–1,200,000 per month. Automatic scooters, 50cc bikes and electric bikes are separate groups with different ranges, so a switch between groups can change the price. ## Check the substitute properly

Do not let the change rush you. Treat the replacement as a new rental.

- **Inspect it.** Our [10-minute inspection checklist](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/rental-scooter-inspection-checklist/) explains the routine, and our guide to [questions to ask about a high-mileage manual rental bike](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/questions-to-ask-about-a-high-mileage-manual-rental-bike/) lists questions about condition.

- **Check the brakes first.** Our guide to [manual bike brakes: what to test before leaving the rental shop](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/manual-bike-brakes-what-to-test-before-leaving-the-rental-shop/) explains how.

- **Photograph existing marks,** and ask for them to be written down. Our guide to [how to document existing scratches on a rental bike](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-document-existing-scratches-on-a-rental-bike/) explains how.

- **Record the fuel and the odometer.**

- **Check the helmet fits.** Our guide to [what to do if a helmet does not fit at pickup](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/what-to-do-if-a-helmet-does-not-fit-at-pickup/) explains the checks.

- **Check the papers** that come with the bike, including the compulsory insurance certificate.

- **Make sure the contract names the new bike** and its plate number.

If anything is wrong, say so, and decline the bike if it is not satisfactory.

## If you have paid something in advance

Whether you can get an advance payment back depends on what it was and what you agreed.

- **If you paid nothing,** you can simply decline. At Nguyen Ha the deposit and rent are normally paid when the contract is signed.

- **If you paid a booking payment,** ask in writing what it covered, whether it is refundable if the bike you booked is unavailable and how it will be applied to a substitute.

- **If you paid by transfer,** keep the confirmation, and ask for the refund to go back to the same account.

- **If the shop refuses to explain,** keep your evidence, and consider disputing the payment through your bank or card provider if one was used.

Do not pay again for a substitute until the first payment is accounted for. ## What the Civil Code says

Vietnam's Civil Code (Law No. 91/2015/QH13), in force since 1 January 2017, says in Article 476 that the lessor must hand over the property in the agreed quantity, quality, type and condition, at the agreed time and place. If the lessor delivers late, the lessee may extend the delivery time or cancel the contract and claim damages, and if the property is not of the agreed quality, the lessee may ask for repair, a reduction in rent or cancellation with damages. We read the lease articles in the text published by the Tuyen Quang Department of Justice. As of October 2026, that means a bike that is not what was agreed is something you can raise. How it applies to a particular booking depends on what was agreed, so the clearer your written booking, the stronger your position. This is general information, not legal advice.

## If nothing suitable is offered

You have choices.

- **Wait,** if the original bike will be back soon, and if the delay is acceptable. Ask for a time.

- **Take a different class,** if you are happy with it, at a price you have confirmed.

- **Change your dates,** if the shop can offer the bike later.

- **Cancel,** and ask for any advance payment back. Ask in writing.

- **Look elsewhere.** There are other shops, and a delay at the counter is better than a bad rental. Our guide to [how to check the rental shop's opening hours and pickup location](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-check-the-rental-shops-opening-hours-and-pickup-location/) helps you check another shop.

If you have already travelled to the shop, make sure you can still get back, or to another place to stay, before you decide.

## How to avoid the problem

- **Confirm the exact model and dates in writing** before you travel.

- **Message again the day before,** to check that the bike is still free.

- **Ask for a backup:** what would be offered if the model is not available.

- **Avoid paying in advance** unless the terms are clear.

- **Arrive in good time,** so that a change leaves you room to decide.

## FAQ: a reserved bike that is unavailable

### Can the bike I booked really be unavailable?

Yes. Nguyen Ha's pages say a model mentioned online may not be available for your dates, and that the bike actually available is confirmed at booking.

### Do I have to accept a substitute?

No. You can decline a bike that does not suit you.

### Will the price change?

The exact price is confirmed on the actual bike, its condition and your dates before you sign. Ask for the substitute's price in writing.

### What if I paid in advance?

Ask in writing what the payment covered and whether it is refundable, and keep the confirmation.

### What does the Civil Code say?

The lessor must hand over the property in the agreed quality and condition. If it does not, the lessee may ask for repair, a lower rent or cancellation, in some cases with compensation.

### How can I avoid this?

Confirm the exact model and dates in writing, and message again before you travel.
