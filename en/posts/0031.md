Renting a motorbike in Hanoi for three weeks is not simply a longer version of renting one for a sightseeing afternoon. You will be living with the vehicle: commuting in rain, parking it at home, keeping track of maintenance, dealing with unexpected schedule changes and eventually returning it in the agreed condition. The cheapest number on a price board is therefore only one part of the decision. This guide is for residents, interns, remote workers and longer-stay visitors deciding between a weekly and monthly arrangement. It uses Nguyen Ha's published rental terms as of October 2026 and explains the questions worth answering **before** a contract starts. The exact model, availability and price are always confirmed with the shop.

## Start with your real travel pattern, not the headline rate

Write down where the bike will spend most of its time. A five-day commute across Long Bien calls for different priorities than occasional errands from an Old Quarter guesthouse. For a daily trip involving heavy traffic, comfort at a standstill and predictable brakes may matter more than luggage capacity. For a rider carrying shopping or a laptop, the storage on an automatic scooter can make a meaningful difference. For a person already experienced with gears, a semi-automatic Honda Wave or Yamaha Sirius can be a practical and economical choice.

Think about parking before you think about speed. Does your apartment offer a guarded place for a motorbike? Is the entrance narrow? Does the parking attendant issue a ticket that must be returned? If you rely on a hotel's public pavement or a street corner overnight, a cheap monthly rental can turn into an expensive inconvenience. Ask your accommodation about a legal, secure parking arrangement and when the gate is open.

A long hire also means choosing a bike suited to *your* ability. The engine size and vehicle classification determine which driving documents apply, not how long you hire it. A motorbike you cannot legally or confidently operate is not a bargain. Compare the practical characteristics on the [motorbikes page](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/motorbikes/) and read the [rental guide](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/rental-guide/) before committing.

## Weekly or monthly: compare the full package

Nguyen Ha quotes rental packages separately rather than multiplying a daily rate by seven or thirty. Published reference rates for manual and semi-automatic bikes are VND 700,000–800,000 per week and VND 1,000,000–1,200,000 per month. For automatic scooters, the published range is VND 600,000–1,000,000 weekly and VND 1,200,000–2,000,000 monthly. Electric models have their own weekly and monthly ranges, and 50cc vehicles are priced by vehicle group as well. These are reference ranges, not a promise that every model is available for the lowest figure.

When comparing a weekly and a monthly deal, calculate the *period you are certain you will use*. Someone who is leaving Hanoi soon after two weeks should not automatically sign for a whole month simply because the average day looks cheaper. Ask whether the weekly rate can be extended, whether a newly agreed period starts at the existing return deadline, and what price the extension would use. Have any change recorded in writing. The shop's published policy does not provide an automatic refund for unused rental time after an early return.

Consider a real example: your apartment is ready on Monday, but your onward travel date may change. A week followed by another week might cost more than a monthly price, yet preserve flexibility. Conversely, if you are living in Hanoi for six weeks with a dependable schedule, asking about a monthly package and a separately agreed extension may be simpler. The correct decision depends on the confirmed bike, the expected return time and the shop's written offer, not a generic online calculator.

The [English price guide](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/prices/) lists current editorial reference figures for vehicle groups, deposit and extras. Ask the shop for one total covering the period you actually want, including any delivery or collection service, before you pay.

## Clarify the cash deposit and identity documents

The rental charge and the cash deposit are different amounts. Nguyen Ha normally requires a cash deposit of VND 2–5 million, depending on the model and contract, paid alongside the agreed rental amount. It is not a deposit-free service. The deposit is reconciled when the vehicle is returned and the responsibilities recorded at handover are settled.

Ask which identity document and driving entitlement the shop needs to inspect. Staff may photograph relevant details for the contract, but the published arrangement says the customer's original passport or identity card is not held after the rental charge and cash deposit have been paid. Read the information-use explanation in the [English privacy notice](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/privacy/) rather than handing personal documents to an unknown intermediary.

Photograph the actual bike at pickup. Take images from both sides, the dashboard and the areas that already have marks or scrapes. Note the vehicle's registration details where relevant and the agreed fuel or battery level. A dated set of photographs protects both parties from arguments over damage that existed before the handover.

It is also worth agreeing *how* a returned deposit is reconciled. If the bike needs attention or there is an alleged deduction, ask for an itemised explanation supported by the contract and the handover record. The easiest disagreement to resolve is one in which both parties wrote down the condition and return time at the beginning.

## Delivery and pickup: do not mistake a service area for another shop

Nguyen Ha operates from one shop at Ngo 5 Nguyen Van Cu, Bo De, Long Bien, near Gia Lam bus station. A guide on this website about Ba Dinh, Hoan Kiem or Gia Lam does **not** indicate another rental branch. Daily and short rentals are normally collected and returned at the Long Bien shop.

Eligible weekly and monthly contracts may include arranged delivery or collection. For qualifying addresses within the shop's published reference area of ten kilometres from Hoan Kiem Lake, the reference service charge is VND 50,000 per journey, or VND 100,000 for both ways. Addresses beyond that range are quoted by agreement, and delivery to Noi Bai airport is not offered. The shop needs the precise address and preferred time to confirm whether a request is feasible.

There is a difference between a delivery *request* and an accepted meeting point. A pedestrian-only lane, gated compound or busy hotel entrance may require a nearby place where both parties can inspect the bike safely. Decide who will meet the staff, what contact number works at handover and whether your building allows the vehicle inside. The [Hanoi areas guide](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/areas/) helps with neighbourhood planning without promising delivery to every street.

## What maintenance questions matter over a month?

An ordinary city rental still needs periodic attention. Before collecting the vehicle, ask when it was last checked and what to do if it starts making an unfamiliar noise, a brake feels inconsistent, a tyre loses pressure or a warning light appears. You are not expected to diagnose an engine fault on the road. Stop somewhere safe and contact the shop before authorising major repairs or attempting a makeshift mechanical fix.

Check tyres visually before riding. Look for underinflation, obvious cuts or an object embedded in the tread. Test both brakes carefully in a safe area and confirm that the horn, indicators, lights and mirrors work. On a semi-automatic, also check for a chain that appears unusually loose or noisy; have the shop inspect it rather than adjusting it without the correct tools. The separate [pre-rental inspection guide](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-check-a-semi-automatic-motorbike-before-renting-in-hanoi/) covers the method for geared machines.

For electric vehicles, agree the supplied charger, suitable charging location and what happens if the battery or charger fails. A display reading alone is not a dependable promise of range for a full day's riding with a passenger. The [electric scooter guide](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/electric-scooters/) explains the trade-off between range, convenience and charging access. Ask whether any model swap is possible if a vehicle becomes unsuitable during a long rental; never assume the contract includes free exchanges.

Keep a direct way to contact the shop while you ride. Tell them about small problems before they become large ones. A timely message about a slowly deflating tyre is easier to handle than a damaged rim after attempting to ride home on it.

## Return deadlines, extensions and early departures

A rental day is twenty-four hours from the contract handover time. If a contract specifies a Tuesday morning return, returning later can trigger a late charge even if you did not ride the bike that day. The published reference for returns one to five hours late is VND 30,000 per hour. From six hours late, a full additional day is charged according to the bike's rate. Confirm these terms on the actual contract rather than expecting a grace period.

Long-stay renters are especially likely to change plans. If you need the vehicle for an extra weekend, contact the shop **before** the original return deadline. Ask for the new end time, additional rent and the location for the eventual return. Keep the agreed change in a message or revised agreement. If you leave Hanoi early, do not assume that unused days of a monthly package will be refunded automatically.

At return, allow time for the shop to check the vehicle, compare photographs and confirm fuel or charge level. For petrol bikes, the reference handover amount is approximately half a litre to two litres, with around half a litre to one litre expected back; returning the tank empty can lead to a VND 20,000 fee under the published terms. Do not rely on a fuel gauge alone if the contract recorded an exact level.

## A practical checklist before signing

- Which *exact* model, engine capacity and registration will I receive, and does my driving entitlement cover it?
- What are the written start and return timestamps, package rent and separate deposit?
- Does the quoted price include any agreed delivery and collection, or are those charged separately?
- If my schedule changes, what does a new week or a partial month cost and when must I request it?
- Who should I call about a puncture, unusual mechanical noise or breakdown?
- Where may I park securely overnight, and who supplies the helmet and vehicle key?
- What evidence is recorded at pickup and what steps determine deposit reconciliation on return?
- If I return early or late, which exact terms in the signed agreement apply?

Write the answers down or keep the booking message. A long-term rental is much more relaxing when the small details are resolved before you start using the bike every morning.

## FAQ

### Is a monthly rental always cheaper than several weekly rentals?

Not necessarily for your actual stay. Monthly package rates often have a lower average daily cost, but an early return does not automatically create a refund. Compare the period you expect to keep the bike with the price confirmed for that model.

### Can Nguyen Ha deliver a motorbike to my apartment?

Delivery and collection may be arranged for eligible weekly or monthly contracts. The shop must approve the actual address, time and applicable charge first. Ordinary short rentals use the Long Bien shop.

### Does the shop keep my original passport as the deposit?

The published terms use a cash deposit, usually VND 2–5 million depending on the bike and agreement. Staff may inspect and photograph the necessary documents, but do not retain the originals after payment and deposit are completed.

### What should I do if the bike develops a problem during a month?

Stop riding if the bike seems unsafe, contact the rental shop and explain the symptoms and your location. Agree who authorises and pays for any repair under the contract before spending money.

### Can I return my motorbike a few days before my monthly contract ends?

You can discuss an early handover with the shop, but the published policy does not provide an automatic refund for unused days. Confirm the return arrangements and deposit settlement in writing.
