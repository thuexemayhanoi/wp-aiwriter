For one passenger and two backpacks, the scooter that works is the one whose under-seat space can swallow at least one of the packs, so the Honda Lead (over 37 litres under the seat, per Honda) is the clear first pick, the Honda Air Blade (23.2 litres) is a workable second, and the Honda Vision and the Click family (roughly 16 to 18 litres) only suit two small daypacks that one person wears. The decision is mostly arithmetic: add up the litres of your bags, compare them to the compartment, and decide in advance who wears what. This guide shows how to do that sum, how to share the load between rider and passenger, and how to test the fit at the rental counter before the bike leaves with you on it.

## Start with the bags, not the bike

Most visitors choose the scooter first and the packing second. For this trip, reverse the order. Weigh and measure each backpack before you reserve, because backpack volume ranges widely:

- A small daypack is usually 15 to 25 litres, enough for a camera, water, a rain jacket and a snack.
- A larger day or overnight bag is 30 to 40 litres.
- A full travel backpack is 50 litres or more and is simply not a scooter load.

Write down the litres printed on the label or the manufacturer's page, and weigh each bag when it is packed for a normal day. A bag that weighs 8 to 10 kg behaves very differently on a 100 kg scooter from one that weighs 3 kg. If both bags are over about 5 kg, the rest of this guide matters even more.

Then decide what has to be in reach while riding (water, phone, a rain layer) and what could stay at the hotel. Cutting a bag from 30 litres to 20 is the cheapest upgrade you can make.

Two caveats apply. First, litres are not shape: a compartment designed around a helmet has curved edges, so a 20-litre rectangular pack may not fit in an 18-litre helmet space, and a pack with a rigid frame fits worse than a soft one. ## Four ways to carry two packs

There are only a few sensible arrangements, and it helps to pick one before you start loading.

**1. One pack under the seat, one worn by the passenger.** This is the most common and often the best. The heavier or bulkier pack goes in the compartment, low and central, and the passenger wears the smaller one, snugly, with the chest strap fastened. It works best on the Lead and the Air Blade.

**2. Both packs worn.** Each person wears a small daypack. This keeps the compartment free for helmets and rain gear and works on any scooter, but only for light packs. A heavy pack on the rider's back pulls the shoulders back and tires the neck, and a heavy pack on the passenger shifts the balance rearward.

**3. One pack on the floor.** On a flat-floored scooter such as the Lead, a small soft bag can sit between the rider's feet. It must not touch the pedals or levers, hide the rider's view of the footboard or prevent both feet from reaching the ground. If you have to angle your legs to fit it, it is the wrong place.

**4. Leave one at the hotel.** For city days, this is often the best answer. Only a daypack's worth of things needs to travel with you.

Two things to avoid whatever the arrangement: hanging a bag from the mirrors or the grips, and letting any strap hang near the rear wheel. The small hook under the handlebars suits a light shopping bag, not a loaded pack.

## Who should wear a pack?

The rider should usually wear the lighter one, or none at all. Anything heavy on the rider's back changes how the bike steers and brakes and tires the shoulders and neck during a long day. A passenger sits on the load-bearing part of the bike anyway, so a snug small pack on the passenger adds the least disruption.

If the passenger is much smaller than the rider, the passenger's pack should be small and light. A big pack behind a small passenger can push them forward into the rider and block the rider's mirror view. If the passenger is the one with the heavy bag, consider the Lead, where a longer seat and a flat floor give more space and a better chance of storing the bag.

## Do the weight sum

You do not need exact numbers, but add them up roughly:

1. Rider's weight.
2. Passenger's weight.
3. Weight of both packs.
4. Helmets, rain gear and water.

Compare the total with the figure your rental shop gives for the bike. Honda sets a maximum weight capacity for each model in its owner's manual, but the figure for these specific scooters could not be confirmed for this guide, so ask the shop for it rather than guessing. If you and your passenger are already heavy, the Lead's heavier frame and larger engine are a better base than the lightest scooters, and a 125 cc machine is a better base than a 110 cc one.

With a passenger, brake earlier, allow a larger gap and ride slower through speed bumps and ramps. Ask the shop to check the rear tyre pressure for two people. Our guide to [riding a manual motorbike with a passenger](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/riding-a-manual-motorbike-with-a-passenger-in-hanoi/) describes the passenger's role, and much of it applies to scooters too.

## The rules in one paragraph

A motorbike may carry the rider and one passenger, with limited exceptions listed in Article 33 of Law 36/2024/QH15, in force since 1 January 2025. The same article forbids the rider from carrying bulky objects and forbids a passenger from holding them, which is the reason a suitcase or a large box is not an option and why a pack should be sized to sit within the line of the bike. Every person aboard must wear a helmet with the strap fastened; Nguyen Ha includes one basic helmet per rider, free. As of October 2026 this applies to visitors in the same way as to residents. Our [helmet rules guide](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/helmet-rules-in-vietnam/) has the detail.

## Where the bags go when you stop

A scooter that carries your bags is also a scooter that you leave carrying them. Nguyen Ha does not provide an extra lock, so lock the steering, park in guarded places and never leave a passport, a laptop or a camera in the compartment. Take valuables with you, and let the compartment hold the bulky but unimportant things: a rain jacket, a spare layer and the helmets. Our [parking guide](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/parking-a-motorbike-in-hanoi/) explains guarded lots and tickets.

If you are moving between hotels with full-size luggage, a taxi is the right tool. A scooter is a day-trip machine, and it does not offer an airport service from Nguyen Ha either.

## A ten-minute fit test at the shop

Before you sign, ask to try your actual bags on the actual bike:

1. **Fill the compartment.** Put the pack in the under-seat space with the helmets you will use. Does the seat close and latch without force?
2. **Sit as a pair.** With both of you on the bike, is there room between the rider and the passenger's pack?
3. **Check the mirrors.** Can the rider see both mirrors with a pack on the passenger's back?
4. **Check the feet.** If a bag is on the floor, can the rider put both feet down at a stop?
5. **Push test.** With the load on, push the bike off its stand and wheel it a few metres. Weight that feels awkward now will feel worse in traffic.
6. **Short loop.** Ride a hundred metres together, with a slow turn and a stop.

If anything is wrong, swap the scooter or lighten the load; do not ride off hoping it will sort itself out. The [10-minute rental scooter inspection](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/rental-scooter-inspection-checklist/) covers the bike itself.

## When to pick two scooters instead

If your bags add up to more than the Lead's compartment, if one of you is a newer rider who would find a loaded scooter hard to control, or if you want to take a longer ride at your own pace, two scooters can be a better answer than one overloaded one. ## FAQ: passenger plus two backpacks

### Can two backpacks fit under a scooter seat?

On the Lead, a small daypack and two helmets can fit, and sometimes two small packs. On the Air Blade, expect one small pack. On the Vision or the Click family, a helmet plus small items is the realistic limit.

### Should the passenger wear a backpack?

A small, snug pack is usually fine. A large or heavy pack pulls the passenger backwards and can unsettle the bike, so keep the heavy one in the compartment.

### Can I put a backpack between my feet?

On a flat-floored scooter, only if it is small, sits flat and leaves both feet free to reach the ground.

### Can I hang a bag from the handlebar?

Only a light shopping bag from the hook. A heavy bag changes the steering and may catch on the grips.

### Is the Lead worth it for two backpacks?

If your bags are over about 20 litres combined, yes. If they are small, the Air Blade or Vision can do the job and may be easier to park.
