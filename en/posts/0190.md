A week on a 50cc scooter is a different deal from a single day, so ask about six things before you agree: what the weekly package includes and how it is priced, what deposit is held and when it comes back, how fuel and late return are handled, whether delivery is possible, who deals with a breakdown mid-week and what exactly the bike is and what condition it is in. Weekly rates are usually set as a package, not as a day rate multiplied by seven, so the questions are not the same as for a quick afternoon hire. This guide gives the questions in the order they come up at a counter, explains why each one matters on a small-engined bike and shows how to record the answers so that both sides remember them on day seven.

## Why a week raises different questions

For one day, the main risk is a bad choice of bike. For a week, the risks are cumulative. The bike will be parked overnight in places you choose, ridden in whatever weather arrives, refuelled several times and used by one person who gets steadily more confident. A small fault that is trivial on day one, such as a soft rear tyre or a mirror that drifts, becomes a daily annoyance by day four.

A 50cc scooter adds its own points. It is light, cheap to run and easy to handle in slow traffic, but it has a modest engine, so route planning matters more than on a larger bike. Our guide to [renting a 50cc scooter in Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/renting-a-50cc-scooter-in-hanoi/) explains who the category suits. This page assumes you have already decided, and covers what to settle before you pay.

## Questions about price and what it covers

The shop's published figure for a 50cc scooter by the week is VND 1,000,000, with VND 200,000 for a single day and VND 2,000,000 for a month. The page of [rental prices](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/prices/) is the place to confirm current figures, and the final price is agreed on the actual bike before you sign. Ask these questions:

- **Is the weekly rate a package?** The shop prices weekly and monthly rentals on their own terms, not as a day rate times the number of days, so ask what the seven days cover.
- **Is anything extra?** One basic helmet per rider is included at no charge. A raincoat is not included for short rentals, and the shop does not provide an extra lock.
- **What is the rental period exactly?** A rental day is 24 hours. If you collect at 15:00, ask what time the week ends.
- **What happens if I want it longer?** Ask how an extension is arranged, and at what rate, so you do not negotiate under pressure on the last evening.
- **What if I return early?** Unused days are not refunded when a bike comes back early, so be realistic about the length you book.

Write the agreed total on the contract or take a photograph of it. Our explainer on [what the clauses in a Hanoi rental contract mean](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/hanoi-motorbike-rental-contract-explained/) helps you read the other lines on the page.

## Questions about the deposit and documents

A weekly rental involves a cash deposit, which is separate from the rent. The shop's deposit range is VND 2,000,000 to VND 5,000,000 depending on the bike and the contract, and it is not a no-deposit service. Ask:

1. **How much is the deposit for this specific bike?**
2. **In what form is it taken, and will I get a receipt?**
3. **When is it returned?** At the shop, on return, after the bike has been checked, is the usual answer to confirm.
4. **What can be deducted?** Ask for the list, and compare it with the contract.
5. **What documents are checked?** Identity documents are checked and photographed for the contract, and originals are not kept. If a shop asks to hold your passport, that is worth questioning.

Our guide on [how a motorbike rental deposit works in Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/motorbike-rental-deposit-hanoi/) goes through the whole cycle. For age, the national rules say a person aged 16 or over may ride a moped of up to 50 cm3, but the shop may have its own requirements, so ask what it needs from you.

## Questions about the bike itself

On a weekly hire you cannot swap a bad bike halfway through without fuss, so the checks at handover carry more weight. The shop lists a Cub 50cc and 50cc scooters among its 50cc vehicles, and notes that the bike actually available is confirmed at booking. Ask:

- **Which exact model will I get?** A classic Cub with gears and a twist-and-go scooter feel very different.
- **How old is it, and how many kilometres has it done?** You will not always get an exact answer, but the way the question is answered is informative. The 50cc bikes page lists what the shop offers.
- **When was it last serviced?** Oil, brakes, tyres and chain are the items that matter most over a week.
- **Are the bike's registration papers with it?** Ask what the shop provides.
- **What are the tyre pressures meant to be?** Honda's tyre guidance says that every motorcycle has a specified air pressure and that both too little and too much are harmful, so ask for the number for your bike and keep it in your phone.

Then do the practical inspection. The [10-minute rental scooter inspection](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/rental-scooter-inspection-checklist/) is a good routine, and photographs of any scratches, taken before you leave, protect you when the bike comes back.

## Questions about fuel, range and late return

Fuel is simple on a 50cc scooter, but it is worth agreeing the rules. At handover, the shop's petrol bikes carry about 0.5 to 2 litres, and they are expected back with about 0.5 to 1 litre. An empty tank on return costs VND 20,000. For context, Honda's own specification pages list tank sizes of 4.3 litres for the Japanese-market Super Cub 50 and 5.0 litres for the Dio, so a 50cc tank is small, and you will fill up often. See our guide to [fuel in Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/fuel-in-hanoi-petrol-stations-rental-scooter/) for stations and habits.

Ask what happens on the last day:

- **What time must the bike be back?** Late return is charged at VND 30,000 per hour for 1 to 5 hours, and from 6 hours it is treated as one extra rental day.
- **Where will the return be done?** The shop is at Alley 5 Nguyen Van Cu Street, Bo De Ward, Long Bien District, Hanoi, open 09:00 to 21:00 daily. A late-night arrival after closing has to be arranged in advance.

## Questions about delivery

Delivery is available only for weekly and monthly agreements, within 10 km of Hoan Kiem Lake. The shop's figures are VND 50,000 per trip or VND 100,000 for delivery and collection, and anything beyond 10 km is agreed in advance. There is no airport service. If you want the bike brought to a hotel, ask:

1. **Is my hotel within the 10 km?**
2. **Will you bring it at a set time, and who will be there?**
3. **Is the handover inspection done at the door, with photos?**
4. **Who collects it at the end, and when?**

If you do not need delivery, collection at the shop is simple, and you can look at the bike in daylight before deciding.

## Questions about breakdowns and servicing during the week

Nobody expects trouble, but a week is long enough for a flat tyre or a flat battery. Ask before you ride off:

- **Who do I call, and on what number?** The shop's phone is 0334 699 969, or +84 334 699 969 from abroad. Save it now.
- **Who pays for a puncture or a worn part?** Normal wear and damage are treated differently, and the contract should say so.
- **Will you replace the bike if it cannot be repaired quickly?** Do not assume this, and do not take a verbal yes as a promise.
- **Can I bring it in for a quick check on day four?** A short visit costs little and often saves trouble.

## Questions about how you plan to use it

The honest answer to "is a 50cc enough?" depends on your week. A scooter is well suited to short urban trips, because it is light and agile. A moped as defined in the national rules has a design speed of no more than 50 km/h, so it is not built for fast roads. Plan routes on quieter streets and ask the shop which roads it would avoid. ## FAQ: weekly 50cc rental

### Is a weekly rate cheaper than seven single days?

Usually, yes, because weekly packages are priced on their own and not as seven times a day rate. The shop's published figures are on the prices page, and the final amount is confirmed on the bike you are given.

### Can I swap the bike halfway through the week?

Ask this at the start. Do not assume a swap is available, and do not expect one for a minor preference. A real fault is different, and the shop should say how it handles that.

### Do I need to pay in advance?

The rent and the deposit are separate amounts. Ask what is due at handover and what is due at the end, and keep the receipt for each.

### Does the helmet come with the rental?

One basic helmet per rider is included free. If you want more protection, you can bring your own. Our [FAQ page](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/faq/) covers other common questions.
