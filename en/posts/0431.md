A motorbike rental shop in Hanoi may reasonably ask to see your passport or identity card, your driving licence and, for a foreign licence, your International Driving Permit, and it may ask for your hotel or address and a phone number. At Nguyen Ha these documents are checked and photographed for the contract and the originals are handed straight back, with a cash deposit of VND 2,000,000–5,000,000 as the security. The reason for the checks is practical and legal: the shop has to know who is renting a valuable vehicle, and Vietnam's penalty decree fines an owner who lets a person who is not licensed to drive that class of bike ride it.

Asking to hold the original of your passport, however, is a different thing, and you should not agree to it. This guide explains which documents a shop may ask for and why, what a licence has to cover, what the shop may photograph and keep, what it should not ask for, what to bring and what to do if you do not have the right licence. It is general information, not legal advice.

## The usual documents

A shop that rents to visitors and residents will typically ask for some or all of the following.

| Document | Who | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Passport | Foreign visitors | Proves identity and the right to be in the country |
| Vietnamese identity card (CCCD) | Residents who are Vietnamese citizens | Proves identity |
| Driving licence | Everyone who will ride | Proves the right to ride this class of bike |
| International Driving Permit (IDP) | Visitors with a foreign licence | Needed alongside the national licence |
| Visa or entry stamp, or residence card | Foreign visitors and residents | Shows the stay is lawful, and how long you will be in Hanoi |
| Hotel or address in Hanoi | Visitors | Contact and return planning |
| Phone number or messaging app | Everyone | To reach you during the rental |

Not every shop asks for every item, and a shop may ask for fewer or for extra ones. Our guide to [what to send the rental shop before pickup](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/what-to-send-the-rental-shop-before-pickup/) explains how to pass details in advance.

## Why the shop checks your licence

The licence check is not a formality. It has a legal basis.

Article 56 of the Law on Road Traffic Order and Safety (Law No. 36/2024/QH15), in force since 1 January 2025, says a driver must have a licence that is valid, still has points and matches the vehicle being driven. Article 57 defines the classes: class A1 covers two-wheeled motorbikes up to 125 cc or electric motors up to 11 kW, and class A covers bikes above that. A Honda Wave, Sirius, Blade, Vision or Air Blade 125 sits inside A1.

Under Article 32 of Decree 168/2024/ND-CP, which we read in the text on the Government portal, an owner of a motorbike or moped who hands the vehicle to, or lets, a person who does not meet the conditions in Article 56(1) drive it is fined VND 8,000,000–10,000,000 if an individual, and VND 16,000,000–20,000,000 if an organisation.

A shop that rents a bike to someone with the wrong licence is exposed to that penalty, which is why a responsible shop checks and may decline. Our guides to [licence rules for foreigners in Vietnam](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/motorbike-licence-rules-for-foreigners-in-vietnam/) and [what documents to carry when riding in Vietnam](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/documents-to-carry-riding-in-vietnam/) explain what you need. Decree 238/2026/ND-CP amended Decree 168/2024/ND-CP from 15 August 2026. As of October 2026, the summaries we found did not show a change to the licence fines for riders, but check the current text if you need it for an actual case. This is general information, not legal advice.

## What a licence has to show

When staff look at your licence, they check:

- **The name and photograph** match you and the passport.

- **The class covers the bike.** For a bike up to 125 cc, a motorcycle category is needed, not a car-only licence.

- **The licence is valid** and not expired.

- **For a foreign licence, the IDP** matches the convention and covers motorcycles. Our guide to [can tourists ride a motorbike in Vietnam](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/motorbike-licence-rules-for-foreigners-in-vietnam/) explains the rules.

- **The documents are originals,** not copies.

If your licence is in a script that staff cannot read, bring the IDP, which is meant to translate the details.

## What the shop may photograph and keep

The shop may photograph your documents for the contract. Ask:

- **Which documents** will be photographed, and why.

- **How the photographs are stored,** and who can see them.

- **How long they will be kept.**

- **Whether they will be shared** with anyone, such as the authorities.

A good shop will answer plainly. You can reasonably ask that the photographs be used only for the rental and for any official request. You should expect the shop to keep a record of who rented the bike, because the owner may be asked to identify the driver when a violation is detected, as our guide to [who is responsible for parking and traffic fines](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/who-is-responsible-for-parking-and-traffic-fines/) explains.

## What a shop should not ask for

- **Your original passport as a deposit,** which we recommend you refuse.

- **Your original licence as security.**

- **Your bank card PIN,** your passwords or your online banking details.

- **A copy of your card for "later charges"** without a written explanation of what may be charged.

- **Any document that has nothing to do with renting a bike,** such as your travel itinerary beyond what is needed for return planning.

If you are asked for something that feels excessive, ask why, and decline if the answer does not satisfy you. ## Sending documents before you arrive

Many visitors send photographs of their documents in advance. This can speed up pickup, but be careful.

- **Send them only to the shop's verified number,** which is +84 334 699 969 on WhatsApp and 0334 699 969 by phone and Zalo. Check it against the shop's own website before you send.

- **Send only what is needed:** the photo page of the passport, the licence and the IDP.

- **Do not send them to a number that contacted you first** unless you have confirmed it.

- **Delete your sent copies** if you wish, once the rental is over, and ask the shop what it does with its own.

## What to bring on the day

- **Your passport** (original, to show, and to take back).

- **Your driving licence and IDP,** matching the class of bike.

- **Your phone,** with the shop's number saved.

- **Cash for the deposit and the rent,** as the contract specifies.

## If you do not have the right licence

Do not rent. A licence that does not match the bike can cost you in several ways: a fine for riding without a valid licence, a refusal of cover by your insurer and a dispute with the shop. As of October 2026, riding a motorbike of up to 125 cc without a valid licence is fined VND 2,000,000–4,000,000, and above 125 cc VND 6,000,000–8,000,000, under Article 18 of Decree 168/2024/ND-CP, as our guide to [traffic fines in Vietnam that catch foreign riders out](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/traffic-fines-vietnam-foreign-riders/) explains. Consider a taxi, a ride-hailing service or a bus, or a vehicle that does not need a licence in the same way. Do not accept a bike from a shop that does not check, because that tells you something about the shop. Our guide to [whether you need a licence for a 50cc scooter](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/renting-a-50cc-scooter-in-hanoi/) explains the small-engine rules.

## FAQ: documents a rental shop may ask for

### What documents do I need to rent a motorbike in Hanoi?

Typically your passport or identity card, your driving licence and, for a foreign licence, your International Driving Permit. Ask the shop which it requires.

### Will the shop keep my passport?

Not at Nguyen Ha. Documents are checked and photographed for the contract, and the originals are handed back. The security is a cash deposit.

### Why does the shop check my licence?

The law requires a licence that matches the bike, and an owner who lets an unlicensed person ride can be fined under Article 32 of Decree 168/2024/ND-CP.

### Can I rent a bike with only a car licence?

A car licence does not cover a motorbike. You need a licence class that covers the bike, and for a foreign licence an IDP.

### Is it safe to send my documents in advance?

Only to the shop's verified number, and only what is needed. Do not send them to a number that contacted you first.

### What if I do not have an IDP?

Ask the shop what it requires, but be aware that riding without a valid licence is fined and may void your insurance.
