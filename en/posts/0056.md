A Honda Wave is a light bike with a small frame, and what it can carry is set by three limits that all apply at once: the maximum load Honda lists for the model, the small allowance Honda gives for luggage, and the load rules in Vietnam's 2024 traffic law. Most visitors only think about the third one. This guide turns those limits into a weight budget you can check at the shop, so that your bags help your trip instead of making the bike unstable. For packing a single backpack as a solo traveller, see our separate article on [renting a manual motorbike with a backpack](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/manual-motorbike-rental-for-solo-travellers-with-a-backpack/). This one is about the numbers.

## The three limits at a glance

- **Honda's maximum load.** The owner's manuals for Wave models list a maximum load that includes the rider, a passenger, all luggage and accessories. In the manuals we checked, the figure is 115 kg for some model versions and 155 kg for others.
- **Honda's luggage allowance.** The same manuals give a maximum luggage weight of 5 kg, a figure that surprises most people.
- **The legal limits.** Article 33 of the Law on Road Traffic Order and Safety (Law No. 36/2024/QH15), in force since 1 January 2025, limits how far goods may extend beyond the luggage carrier and how high they may be stacked, and bans carrying bulky loads.

Which Honda figure applies to your bike depends on the exact model and version. Ask the shop to read the figure from the manual or the label on the bike, or to say plainly that they do not know. Do not guess.

## What Honda says in the manuals

Honda Vietnam's Wave Alpha manual gives a kerb weight of 96 kg, lists the maximum load capacity as either 115 kg or 155 kg depending on the model version, and gives the maximum luggage weight as 5 kg. It also states that the bike carries the rider and one passenger. The Wave RSX FI manual is similar: a kerb weight of 97 or 98 kg depending on version, a maximum load of 115 kg or 155 kg depending on version, and a maximum of 5 kg for its storage compartment, with a 4.0-litre fuel tank.

The manuals also give practical rules for packing. Tie all luggage down firmly, keep it balanced and as close to the centre of the bike as possible, and keep it away from the lights and the exhaust. Honda warns that overloading or loading incorrectly can lead to accidents, serious injury or death, and tells owners to follow the load limits in the manual. It also says not to put flammable items, valuables or fragile things in the storage compartment and not to attach a trailer or sidecar, because the bike is not designed to tow.

## Doing the weight sum

The maximum load is a budget that rider, passenger, bags and accessories share. A simple sum shows how fast it fills:

| | Example A: solo | Example B: two riders |
|---|---|---|
| Rider | 75 kg | 75 kg |
| Passenger | none | 60 kg |
| Bags and gear | 8 kg | 8 kg |
| Total | 83 kg | 143 kg |

Example A fits comfortably within either Honda figure. Example B fits within a 155 kg limit but exceeds a 115 kg limit by a wide margin. These are only examples. Your own total is what matters, and a heavier couple with bags can pass 155 kg too.

Honda's figures are limits, not targets. The closer you get to the maximum, the longer the bike takes to accelerate and stop, the more the rear suspension sags and the less steady the steering feels. Treat the limit as the edge of the safe zone, not the middle.

## Why 5 kg is so small

The 5 kg luggage figure is a manufacturer limit for what you carry in or on the bike's own compartments. It is enough for a rain jacket, a water bottle, a small camera and a power bank. It is not enough for a laptop bag, a full backpack or a wet-weather kit.

In practice many riders in Hanoi carry far more than 5 kg on a Wave. That does not make it safe. If you plan to carry more, spread it so that the load stays low and central, keep the total within the bike's maximum load and make sure nothing hangs where it can touch a wheel, the chain or the exhaust. If your bag weighs more than a few kilograms, wearing it on your back raises your centre of gravity and shifts the balance backwards, so test the bike parked and then at walking pace before you head into traffic.

## The legal limits on loads

Article 33 of the law sets out several rules that apply to goods on a motorbike:

- Goods must not extend more than 0.3 metres beyond the width of the luggage carrier on each side.
- Goods must not extend more than 0.5 metres behind the carrier, measured against the manufacturer's design.
- The height of the load, measured from the road surface, must not exceed 2 metres.
- A rider must not carry, haul or push bulky loads, and a passenger must not hold bulky objects.

The penalty decree, Decree 168/2024/ND-CP, fines a rider who carries bulky objects at VND 600,000–800,000 in Article 7. We read the decree on the Government portal, and Decree 238/2026/ND-CP, which amends it from 15 August 2026, does not change that article. As of October 2026 these rules apply to everyone, including visitors. ## How to load a Wave sensibly

The aim is a low, central, tied-down load that does not change how the bike steers.

- **Heavy items low and forward.** Put the heaviest item closest to the middle of the bike, not at the end of the rear rack.
- **One soft bag, not many hanging bags.** Loose bags swing and catch on the foot controls. A single bag secured with straps, or a bungee net, is better.
- **Keep the footpegs and the chain clear.** A strap or a loose bag corner near the rear wheel or the chain is dangerous. Ask the shop how the chain guard looks on your bike.
- **Keep clear of the exhaust.** Plastic bags and rain jackets melt on a hot pipe.
- **Check before every ride.** Straps loosen over bumps. A thirty-second check after the first kilometre saves a lot of trouble.

If you cannot meet these conditions, leave something at your hotel. Our guide to [parking a motorbike in Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/parking-a-motorbike-in-hanoi/) explains why you should not leave valuables on a parked bike either.

## How luggage changes the ride

Extra weight changes more than the sum. Acceleration is slower, so give yourself longer gaps at junctions. Braking distances grow, so ease off earlier and use both brakes together. The rear sags, which can lift the headlight beam and reduce front-wheel grip in corners. At low speed a high load makes the bike feel top-heavy and harder to hold upright at a stop.

None of this is dramatic at normal city speeds, but it adds up with a passenger, a hill or a wet road. If you load the bike near its limit, ride more slowly, brake earlier and avoid steep descents. ## FAQ: luggage on a Honda Wave

### How much weight can a Honda Wave carry?

Honda's manuals list a maximum load of 115 kg or 155 kg depending on the model version, covering the rider, one passenger and all luggage. The exact figure depends on your bike, so ask the shop to confirm it.

### How much luggage does Honda allow on a Wave?

The manuals we checked give a maximum luggage weight of 5 kg. A heavier load is not covered by that figure and reduces stability.

### Is there a legal limit on the size of a load on a motorbike in Vietnam?

Yes. Under Article 33 of the 2024 law, goods may not extend more than 0.3 metres beyond each side of the carrier or 0.5 metres behind it, and the height from the road may not exceed 2 metres. Bulky loads are banned.

### What is the fine for carrying a bulky load?

As of October 2026, Decree 168/2024/ND-CP fines a rider who carries bulky objects VND 600,000–800,000.

### Can I wear a heavy backpack instead of strapping it on?

It is allowed within the legal limits but changes your balance. Keep the weight low, test the bike at walking pace and avoid a bag that is heavier than a few kilograms.

### Should I change the tyre pressure for a heavy load?

Ask the shop for the correct pressure for your model and load, and check the tyres cold before you go.
