When you bring an automatic scooter back, five things decide how smoothly the return goes: the petrol level, the bodywork against your handover photos, the keys and helmet, anything left under the seat, and the time. For fuel, the shop's rule is to return a petrol scooter with about 0.5 to 1 litre in the tank, not full, and an empty tank at return costs VND 20,000. For bodywork, walk round the scooter with a staff member and compare each panel with the photos you took at pick-up. Hand back every key, the helmet and your belongings, and arrive before the shop closes at 21:00. This checklist is written for step-through automatic scooters, such as the Honda Vision, Lead and Air Blade, and it covers the details that are different from a manual bike: the under-seat fuel cap, plastic panels, a smart key if your scooter has one, and the storage box.

## The five-minute return in outline

If you only have a few minutes, work through this order. Each step is explained in the sections that follow.

| Step | What to do | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Arrive well before 21:00 | Avoid late-return charges |
| 2 | Check the fuel gauge, and refuel only if needed | About 0.5 to 1 litre is expected |
| 3 | Park the scooter, engine off, and empty the storage box | Nothing left behind |
| 4 | Walk round the scooter with staff, panel by panel | Compare with your handover photos |
| 5 | Hand back every key, the helmet and any documents given | Closes the contract items |
| 6 | Ask when and how the deposit is returned | Know what happens next |

## Fuel: how much to bring back

The shop's terms for petrol bikes are simple. The bike is handed over with about 0.5 to 2 litres, and is expected back with about 0.5 to 1 litre. You are not asked to fill the tank. If the tank is empty at return, there is a VND 20,000 charge. For electric scooters, battery level and charging are confirmed at handover instead.

That means a good return is neither a full tank nor an empty one. If the gauge shows a little fuel, leave it. If the gauge is at or near empty, put in a small amount at a petrol station on the way. A scooter tank is small: the Honda Vision manual lists 4.8 to 4.9 litres by version, so a single litre is a noticeable part of it.

Honda's manuals help you read the gauge. On the Vision, the first bar flashes when about 0.9 litre is left, and on the Lead and Air Blade the first bar corresponds to about 1.2 litres. The Vision manual advises refuelling when the gauge reaches the E mark, and Honda's global guide warns that running out of petrol stops the engine and may damage the catalytic converter. Our guide to [fuel in Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/fuel-in-hanoi-petrol-stations-rental-scooter/) explains the stations and how to pay. If you are unsure how much the shop wants, ask when you collect the scooter and write it down.

## Refuelling an automatic scooter correctly

On a typical automatic scooter, the fuel cap is under the seat. The Honda Vision manual describes the steps, and the same pattern applies to many models:

1. **Switch the engine off** and keep the scooter away from heat, sparks and flames. The manual adds that petrol should be handled outdoors.
2. **Open the seat.** The cap sits beneath it.
3. **Turn the cap anticlockwise to its stop and lift it off.**
4. **Fill gently.** The manual says not to fill above the lower edge of the filler neck, which keeps room for the fuel to expand. For the small amounts needed here, you will not come near that mark.
5. **Wipe up any spill at once.**
6. **Refit the cap and tighten it clockwise** until the arrow on the cap lines up with the arrow on the tank, then close the seat.

The Vision manual calls for unleaded petrol with an octane rating (RON) of 91 or higher, or 92 or higher, depending on the version, and it says the ethanol content should not exceed 10 per cent. Station staff will usually know which pump is correct, but ask the shop which grade it prefers if you are unsure. Do not put diesel in a petrol scooter, and do not top off after the pump clicks.

## The bodywork walk-round

Most disagreements at return come from damage that was already there. The fix is the same one every time: photograph the scooter at pick-up, and compare it with the scooter at return, with staff beside you.

Work round in a fixed order so nothing is missed. Start at the front and move clockwise:

1. **Front panel and headlight lens.** Look for cracks or fresh scratches.
2. **Handlebar cover and dashboard glass.** These are easy to scuff in a fall.
3. **Left mirror and left grip.** A bent mirror stem or a scuffed grip end suggests a tip-over. Honda's training document asks riders to adjust mirrors before riding, so make sure they still move.
4. **Left side panels and floorboard.** Check the side cover, the floor mat and the edge of the floor, where a side stand or kerb can scrape.
5. **Rear: tail light, rear panel and grab handle.** A cracked tail lens is a common find.
6. **Right side panels and exhaust cover.** Pay attention to the lower edge, which meets kerbs and walls.
7. **Right mirror and grip.** Same as the left.
8. **Seat.** Look for rips, cuts or burns.
9. **Tyres and wheels.** Not a cosmetic check, but it is the moment to say if a tyre was damaged.

Plastic panels scratch easily, and a thin layer of dust is not the same as a scratch. Honda's manual advises cleaning plastic panels with a soft sponge and plenty of water, because wiping dry grit across the surface scuffs it, so do not rub dust off yourself. Our guide to [what counts as normal wear on a rented Honda Wave](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/what-counts-as-normal-wear-on-a-rented-honda-wave/) separates ordinary wear from damage, and the same ideas apply to a scooter.

## Under the seat, keys and extras

Scooters have storage that manual bikes lack, and it is where belongings go missing. Before you hand the scooter back:

- **Empty the under-seat box.** The Vision manual gives a 10 kg limit for it. Check the corners for charging cables, receipts and rain gear.
- **Check the front compartment and hooks.** The manual lists a small limit for the front compartment and hook.
- **Hand back every key.** If the scooter has a smart key, the Vision manual notes that anyone holding the fob within range can start the engine, unlock the ignition, open the seat and unlock the steering, so give it directly to a staff member and do not leave it in the box or on the seat.
- **Return the helmet.** One basic helmet per rider is provided free with the rental, and it should come back with the scooter.
- **Ask for any documents you handed over** and check you receive them.
- **Park with the steering locked.** Honda's training document ends its procedure by switching off and turning the handlebar fully to the left.

## Return checklist

- Arrive before 21:00 and tell the shop if you will be late.
- Fuel: about 0.5 to 1 litre, not full, not empty.
- Engine off, scooter parked, steering locked.
- Under-seat box, front compartment and hooks emptied.
- Walk-round with staff, compared with handover photos.
- Every key and fob handed over, including spares.
- Helmet returned.
- Any damage reported before the check starts.
- Photographs of the scooter at return.
- Deposit timing confirmed.

## FAQ: returning an automatic scooter

### Do I have to fill the tank before I return the scooter?

No. The shop expects about 0.5 to 1 litre, not a full tank. An empty tank costs VND 20,000.

### Where is the fuel cap on a scooter?

On many Honda scooters, including the Vision, under the seat. Switch the engine off and lift the seat.

### What petrol should I use?

The Vision manual calls for unleaded petrol of RON 91 or 92 or higher, depending on the version, with no more than 10 per cent ethanol. Ask the shop which grade it prefers.

### What if I already refuelled to full?

Ask the shop how it treats extra petrol. The guideline is to return with a small amount, so there is no need to fill the tank.

### Can I return the scooter after 21:00?

Plan to return within opening hours, 09:00 to 21:00 daily. If you will be late, call the shop and ask what applies.

### What if I lost the smart key?

Tell the shop immediately. A lost key is handled under the rental contract, so ask what applies before you sign.
