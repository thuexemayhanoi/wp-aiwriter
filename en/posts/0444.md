At the final return inspection of a rental motorbike, staff walk round the bike with you and compare it with the record made at handover, check the fuel or charge level and the odometer, count the items you are handing back, classify any difference as ordinary wear or new damage, settle any late or fuel charge and then release the deposit once everything is reconciled. At Nguyen Ha the published terms describe it in those terms: the deposit is reconciled on return according to the recorded condition and obligations, and it is returned once the bike has been checked against the handover record and any late or fuel charge has been settled.

For you, the practical points are to arrive with time to spare, to stand with the staff during the check, to point out anything you know about and to ask for any deduction to be written down with the reason. This guide explains the inspection step by step, what is checked and why, how differences are classified, how charges are worked out, what to do if you disagree and what happens after you leave. It is general information, not legal advice.

## The purpose of the inspection

The inspection answers one question: is the bike coming back in the condition in which it left, allowing for ordinary wear? Vietnam's Civil Code (Law No. 91/2015/QH13), in force since 1 January 2017, says that a lessee must return the leased property in the condition in which it was received, apart from natural wear or as otherwise agreed, and that the lessor may claim compensation if its value has dropped, apart from natural wear (Article 482). It also says the lessee is not responsible for natural wear from use (Article 479). We read the lease articles in the text published by the Tuyen Quang Department of Justice. As of October 2026 that is the general rule, and the inspection is how it is applied in practice.

The inspection is also a settling of accounts. Late-return and fuel charges are worked out. The deposit is reconciled. At the end, the rental should be closed, with a record. Our guide to [returning your rental motorbike in Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/returning-your-rental-motorbike-hanoi/) explains the whole return, and this guide focuses on the inspection itself.

## Before the inspection starts

The inspection begins before anyone touches the bike.

1. **You arrive,** ideally well before the agreed return time. A late arrival can mean a charge, as we explain below.
2. **Staff note the time of return,** which matters if there is any question of lateness.
3. **You park the bike** where staff ask, and switch off the engine.
4. **You take your own belongings** from the bike and the compartment.
5. **You photograph the bike,** as our guide to [what to photograph when returning a rental scooter](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/what-to-photograph-when-returning-a-rental-scooter/) describes, before handing over the key.

Staff will usually pull out the contract and the handover record, with any photographs, to compare against. Have your own pickup photographs ready.

## The walk-round

The walk-round is the heart of the inspection. A typical sequence is:

| Step | What staff look at | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Overall look from a few steps back | Stand with them, and follow |
| 2 | Front: headlight, mudguard, front panel, plate | Mention anything you know about |
| 3 | Each side: panels, mirrors, levers, footrests, exhaust | Point to existing marks, with your photographs |
| 4 | Rear: tail light, panel, rack or grab rail, plate | Point out the same |
| 5 | Seat and top: seat cover, instrument panel | Show the pickup photograph if queried |
| 6 | Tyres and wheels | Say if you had a puncture or repair |
| 7 | Lights, horn and indicators | Test them together if asked |
| 8 | Brakes and controls: a short check | Mention any change you noticed |
| 9 | Under the seat and any compartment | Confirm it is empty |

The shop's own pages say that both sides should inspect brakes, tyres, lights, mirrors, bodywork, accessories and the fuel or charge level and record the handover time. The same list applies at the end. Staff may also start the engine and listen to it, and they may look at the chain and the underside.

## Fuel, charge and the odometer

Two numbers are recorded.

- **The fuel or charge level.** For a petrol bike, the published terms say it comes back with about 0.5 to 1 litre, and that an empty tank at return costs VND 20,000. For an electric bike, the battery level is compared with the level at handover. Our guides to [how to record the fuel level at handover](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-record-the-fuel-level-at-handover/) and [how pickup battery level is recorded on an electric scooter rental](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/electric-scooter-rental-how-pickup-battery-level-is-recorded/) explain the pickup side.
- **The odometer.** The reading is compared with the pickup reading to show the distance covered. It also shows whether the odometer works.

## Counting what you hand back

Staff check the items on the contract.

- **The key or smart fob,** and any second key.
- **The helmet or helmets,** since one basic helmet per rider is included.
- **Any other accessory** that was lent and written on the contract. The published terms say a raincoat is not part of short rentals but may be provided on a monthly rental.
- **The bike's papers,** if you were given copies.
- **A charger and cable,** for an electric bike.

If an item is missing, say so at once, and look for it together. Check the list on your contract, not your memory.

## How differences are classified

When staff find a difference between the bike and the record, they decide what it is. Typically there are four categories.

1. **No change.** Nothing to discuss.
2. **Ordinary wear.** Tread worn gradually, a slightly longer brake pedal, light fading. This is not charged. Our guide to [what counts as normal wear on a rented Honda Wave](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/what-counts-as-normal-wear-on-a-rented-honda-wave/) explains the difference.
3. **New damage.** A fresh scratch, a crack, a bent lever, a broken indicator. This is assessed by its cause, the actual condition and the contract. The shop's pages say that damage from a collision, an accident or misuse is priced by its cause, the actual condition of the bike and the contract, and that the renter may bear a cost in proportion to the real damage.
4. **A mark that is unclear.** Staff and renter disagree about whether it was there at the start. This is where your pickup photographs matter.

Ask staff to tell you in which category each difference falls, and why.

## Charges worked out at the end

At the end of the inspection, any charges are added up. The published terms set these out.

- **Late return:** VND 30,000 per hour for 1 to 5 hours late, and one extra rental day from 6 hours late. Telling the shop you are late does not automatically waive the charge. Our guide to [late rental returns: how to calculate the possible charge](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/late-rental-returns-how-to-calculate-the-possible-charge/) explains the arithmetic.
- **Empty tank:** VND 20,000.
- **Damage:** as assessed above.
- **Any other agreed charge,** such as an extension not yet paid.

Ask for each charge to be itemised, with the reason and the amount, and written on the contract. If you have receipts for earlier payments, show them. Our guide to [what should be written on a rental payment receipt](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/what-should-be-written-on-a-rental-payment-receipt/) explains what they should show.

## Reconciling the deposit

The last step is the deposit. The published terms say the cash deposit is reconciled on return according to the recorded condition and obligations, and returned once the bike has been checked against the handover record and any late or fuel charge has been settled. In practice:

1. **Charges are totalled.**
2. **The deposit is compared** with any amount owed.
3. **The remainder is returned,** in the agreed form.
4. **A record is made** that the deposit was returned.

The published terms do not give a timeline for returning the deposit, so you should have asked before you signed. Our guide to [how to check deposit return conditions before signing](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-check-deposit-return-conditions-before-signing/) explains what to ask. Count what you are handed back, and keep your copy of the contract showing that it was settled.

## If you disagree with a finding

Stay calm, and keep it documented.

- **Show your pickup photographs** next to the shop's finding.
- **Show your return photographs.**
- **Ask for the shop's reasons in writing,** and for any amount to be itemised.
- **Ask to see the part,** a photograph or a repair receipt, if a repair is charged.
- **Agree a reasonable figure** rather than a standoff, and get it written on the contract.
- **If you cannot agree,** keep every record and ask for a documented resolution, as the shop's own pages suggest. You may need advice from your embassy or a local lawyer.

Do not hand over the key, or leave, until you have what you need: your photographs, the written record and any agreed amount noted. Staff can be reached on 0334 699 969 (+84 334 699 969), open 09:00 to 21:00 daily.

## Typical situations

**A clean return.** The walk-round matches the record, the fuel is in range and the deposit is returned. This is the normal result.

**A small scuff you did not notice.** Staff point it out. Compare with the pickup photographs. If it was not there before, ask how it will be assessed, and for the figure in writing.

**A mark that was there at the start.** Show the pickup photograph, and ask for the note on the contract. If it is written on the contract, it is part of the starting condition that the return is compared with.

**A puncture repaired during the rental.** Show the receipt. Our guide to [punctured tyre during a rental: who to contact](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/punctured-tyre-during-a-rental-who-to-contact/) explains how to record it.

**A small drop you did not report earlier.** Say so now. Honesty at this stage usually costs less than a surprise. Our guide to [what to do after dropping a rented semi-automatic bike](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/what-to-do-after-dropping-a-rented-semi-automatic-bike/) explains why.

**Damage that appeared during the rental and was reported.** Show the messages and photographs. Our guide to [how to report damage that appears during your rental](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-report-damage-that-appears-during-your-rental/) explains how to build the record.

## After you leave

- **Keep your photographs and paperwork** until the deposit has been returned, and for a few weeks afterwards.
- **Watch for messages** from the shop for a day or two.
- **Be aware that a fine can arrive later.** For violations found by technical devices, the Government's Decree 168/2024/ND-CP provides for the authority to send a notice asking the vehicle owner and related persons to come and settle it. Our guide to [who is responsible for parking and traffic fines](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/who-is-responsible-for-parking-and-traffic-fines/) explains how.

Prices and terms are on the [prices page](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/prices/).

## A quick checklist

1. **Arrive early, and photograph the bike first.**
2. **Stand with staff during the walk-round.**
3. **Point out anything you know about.**
4. **Check fuel, odometer and the items you hand back.**
5. **Ask how each difference is classified.**
6. **Ask for every charge to be itemised and written.**
7. **Get the deposit back, and a record of it.**
8. **Keep your records.**

## FAQ: the final return inspection

### What do staff check at return?

The bike against the handover record, the fuel or charge level, the odometer, the items you hand back and any marks, then they settle charges and the deposit.

### How long does it take?

A clean return can be quick. Allow extra time if there are differences to discuss.

### What is the fuel rule?

The published terms say about 0.5 to 1 litre at return, and VND 20,000 for an empty tank.

### What if staff find a mark I did not cause?

Show your pickup photographs and ask for the shop's reasons in writing.

### When do I get the deposit back?

At the end of the rental, once the bike has been checked against the handover record and any late or fuel charge has been settled. The published terms do not give a timeline, so ask in advance.

### Can I leave before the paperwork is done?

It is better not to. Wait until the charges, the deposit and the record are settled.
