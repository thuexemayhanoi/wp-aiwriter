For a manual or automatic bike in Hanoi, a weekly package usually starts to beat separate days somewhere around the fifth day of use, and for a 50cc scooter it breaks even at five days. At Nguyen Ha Motorbike Rental the published day range for manual and automatic bikes is VND 150,000 to 200,000, while the weekly range is VND 700,000 to 800,000 for manual bikes. Those ranges are the shop's reference prices and the final price is confirmed on the actual bike, so use this comparison to decide which package to ask about, not to calculate a quote.

The choice also depends on things a price table hides: whether you will ride every day, whether you want delivery, and how much flexibility you need if your plans change.

## Reading the published ranges side by side

The shop publishes day, week and month ranges by bike type. The ones that matter for a daily-versus-weekly decision are these:

| Bike type | Day range (VND) | Week range (VND) |
|---|---|---|
| Manual | 150,000 to 200,000 | 700,000 to 800,000 |
| Automatic | 150,000 to 200,000 | 600,000 to 1,000,000 |
| 50cc | 200,000 | 1,000,000 |
| Electric | No day price listed | 600,000 to 800,000 |

These are editorial snapshots of the Vietnamese price page as read in October 2026 and can change. The current ranges are on the [prices page](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/prices/), and the bike you are offered sets the exact figure. Two details deserve attention. The automatic week range is wide because it covers several models, so a Honda Vision and a Honda Air Blade may not sit at the same end. And electric bikes have a weekly range but no day price in the published list, so for an electric scooter the question is not daily versus weekly but whether you want one at all.

## Where the weekly package starts to win

The shop prices weekly packages on their own, not as seven times the day rate, and that is where the saving comes from. You can still make a rough test with the published ranges.

- **Manual bike:** four days at the top of the daily range already equal the top of the weekly range. Around five days at the bottom of the daily range comes close to the bottom of the weekly range. From the sixth day, separate days cost more than the weekly package.
- **50cc scooter:** five days at VND 200,000 equal the weekly VND 1,000,000, so a week is cheaper from the sixth day.
- **Automatic bike:** the answer depends on the model, because the weekly range runs from VND 600,000 to VND 1,000,000. Ask for the weekly price of the specific model before you decide.

The rule of thumb is therefore a simple one. If you expect to ride on five or fewer days in a row, price the days. If you expect to keep the bike for six or seven days, ask for the weekly package. In between, ask the shop which is cheaper for your dates, since it knows the exact figure.

## What a week costs you even when you do not ride

A package is a block of time, not a block of riding. If your week includes two days when you stay in the city centre on foot, or two days when it rains hard, the bike is still yours and still paid for. Compare two travellers:

- A visitor who rides every day to different areas gets the best value from a week.
- A visitor who uses the bike on three days and walks on the other four is paying for four idle days.

Be realistic about the rest days. If you can imagine yourself leaving the bike parked for half the week, the daily option may suit you better. Note also that the shop does not refund unused rent for an early return, so a week cut short is not credited back. The key point is that you are buying time, whether or not you use all of it, and the [rental guide](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/rental-guide/) sets out the basic terms.

## Delivery is the quiet advantage of the week

The only way to have the bike brought to you is a weekly or monthly agreement. Delivery is available within 10 km of Hoan Kiem Lake at VND 50,000 per trip or VND 100,000 for both ways, with distances beyond 10 km agreed in advance, and there is no airport service. A daily rental has no delivery option, so you collect and return the bike at the shop.

For some travellers this is worth more than the nominal saving. If you are staying across the Red River from the Old Quarter, or carrying luggage, or arriving with a child, avoiding a taxi to the shop and back may justify choosing the week. The shop is in Bo De Ward, Long Bien District, near Gia Lam bus station, and our page on [Long Bien](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/long-bien/) shows the surrounding area.

## What stays the same whichever package you choose

Some terms do not change with the length of the rental, according to the shop's published rules:

- **The deposit** is a cash deposit of VND 2,000,000 to VND 5,000,000, depending on the bike and the contract. It is separate from the rent. Ask whether the figure differs between a day and a week; do not assume it does or does not.
- **Fuel:** a petrol bike is handed over with about 0.5 to 2 litres and comes back with about 0.5 to 1 litre. An empty tank at return costs VND 20,000.
- **Helmet:** one basic helmet per rider is free. A raincoat is not included for short rentals, so bring one in wet weather, and a monthly agreement may allow one to be considered.
- **Late return:** from 1 to 5 hours late it is VND 30,000 per hour, and from 6 hours it is one extra day.
- **Documents:** identity documents and the licence are checked and photographed for the contract, and the originals are not kept. A licence valid for the bike's class is required for any rental length; Law 36/2024/QH15 sets the classes and has been in force since 1 January 2025.

Because these do not vary, they should not drive the decision. They are worth knowing so that you do not mistake them for package differences. Our guide to [how a rental deposit works](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/motorbike-rental-deposit-hanoi/) covers the money in more detail.

## A seven-day test for your own plans

Take a sheet of paper and list seven days. Mark each one: riding all day, riding for a few hours, or not riding. Then apply the following:

| Pattern | Likely better choice |
|---|---|
| Riding on 6 or 7 of 7 days | Weekly package |
| Riding on 4 or 5 days, in one block | Ask both prices; the weekly may still be cheaper for a manual bike |
| Riding on 2 or 3 days spread across the week | Separate daily rentals, returning the bike between |
| One long trip out of the city plus short city days | Weekly, but check that leaving Hanoi is allowed |
| Arriving late at night or leaving early | Count the exact pickup and return times against the shop's 09:00 to 21:00 hours |

Returning the bike between rides is only a saving if the next rental is available when you want it. In busy periods, a bike returned on Tuesday may not be there on Thursday. Ask the shop how it handles repeat rentals, and whether you can keep your preferred model.

## Questions to ask before choosing

1. What is the exact daily and weekly price for the model I will get?
2. Is the deposit the same for both?
3. If I take the weekly package and want to return it early, is anything credited?
4. If I take daily rentals and decide to extend, can I switch to the weekly package, and from which day?
5. Does the week run in 24-hour blocks from handover?
6. Can delivery be arranged, and for which address?
7. What happens if the bike has a fault in the middle of the week? Our [breakdown guide](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/rental-bike-breakdown-hanoi-what-to-do/) explains the usual steps.

## FAQ about daily and weekly packages

### Is a weekly package always cheaper than seven single days?

At the published ranges it is cheaper for a manual or 50cc bike over a full week, but the prices are indicative and the final price is confirmed on the bike. For an automatic, it depends on the model.

### Can I start with daily rental and switch to a weekly package?

Ask the shop. Whether earlier days are credited, and what the new package costs from the switch date, are terms to agree before you start.

### Is the weekly package better for a trip outside Hanoi?

Often a longer rental suits a longer trip, but you must first check that the shop allows the bike to leave the city. Our article on [taking a rental motorbike out of Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/take-rental-motorbike-out-of-hanoi/) shows what to ask.

### Does a weekly package include delivery?

Delivery is only available with weekly or monthly agreements, within 10 km of Hoan Kiem Lake, at the published per-trip fee. It is not automatic, so ask for it when you book.
