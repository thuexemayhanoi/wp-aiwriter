Yes, in principle you can reach Cat Ba from Hanoi on a motorbike: ride to Hai Phong on National Highway 5, cross the Dinh Vu to Cat Hai sea bridge, then put the bike on a ferry from the Got or Dong Bai terminal to Cai Vieng on Cat Ba island. Travel sources give about 156 km and four to five hours of travelling time.

Whether you can do it on a bike rented in Hanoi depends first on the rental shop agreeing, and the sensible question is whether you should: most visitors who want a motorbike on the island rent one there, which avoids a long highway ride and the extra ferry step. This guide explains the route, the ferry stage, the rental question, the cost logic and a decision table, so you can choose between riding your Hanoi bike, taking a bus and renting on Cat Ba, or skipping the motorbike altogether.

## The short answer in four lines

- **Physically possible:** yes, by Highway 5, the sea bridge and a ferry that carries motorbikes.
- **Permitted on your rental:** only if the shop agrees; ask before you go and get the answer clearly.
- **Practical for most visitors:** usually not, because a long ride, a ferry queue and a return trip use up a good deal of time.
- **The common alternative:** go by bus to the island and rent a bike there.

## What Cat Ba is, and why people go

Cat Ba is the largest island of an archipelago that is part of the Ha Long Bay - Cat Ba Archipelago World Heritage property. UNESCO's description says the property covers 65,650 ha and 1,133 islands and islets across Quang Ninh province and Hai Phong city, and that the Cat Ba Archipelago was added through a boundary modification in 2023. Hai Phong's official English portal notes that Cat Ba National Park lies next to Ha Long Bay and advises visitors to hire a motorbike to get around. That last point matters for this question: the island is set up for rented bikes, so you do not need to bring one.

## The route a rider would take

Reading the travel sources together, the usual riding route runs like this:

1. **Hanoi to Hai Phong on National Highway 5.** Motorbikes cannot use the expressway, because Law 36/2024/QH15 bars motorcycles and mopeds from expressways. A travel operator puts Hanoi to Hai Phong at about 105 km and about three hours on Highway 5. The usual cautions about truck traffic and fatigue on that leg apply fully, and our guide to [which Hanoi highways allow motorbikes](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/hanoi-ring-roads-highways-motorbikes-allowed/) explains the access rules.
2. **Across the Dinh Vu to Cat Hai sea bridge,** also known as the Tan Vu - Lach Huyen bridge.
3. **On Cat Hai island to the ferry terminal.** One site says the terminal is about 7 km after the bridge.
4. **Ferry from the Got or Dong Bai terminal to Cai Vieng on Cat Ba.**
5. **Cai Vieng to Cat Ba Town.** Travel sources describe this as roughly a 30-minute onward journey.

The bridge deserves a note. Hai Phong's land-transport planning table gives its length as 5,440 m. A Vietnamese transport site describes four lanes, two for motor vehicles and two for non-motorised vehicles, with a design speed of 80 km/h. A Hai Phong police notice from August 2026 about a collision between a motorcycle and a trailer truck on the bridge shows that motorcycles use it and that it carries heavy traffic. Ride it with care.

Because the expressway also meets the bridge approach, car-oriented navigation will try to send you onto it. Our guide to [how to check whether a navigation app suggests an expressway](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/how-to-check-if-a-navigation-app-suggests-an-expressway/) shows how to catch that.

## The ferry stage

The ferry is what makes Cat Ba feel like a separate trip. Travel guides say the crossing takes about 15 to 30 minutes, with the terminal's operating hours roughly from early morning to early evening. One operator gives low-season hours of 5:00 to 18:00 and says peak-season hours start earlier. Treat the times and any fares as indicative; ferry operators change them, so check on the day. A hotel guide warns of long waits at peak times and holidays, and a transport site notes that bad weather can affect crossings.

On whether the ferry takes motorbikes, two travel guides say it does, with an extra fee beyond the passenger fare, and one says a parking lot at the Dong Bai terminal lets you leave a bike behind. This guide does not quote fees because they could not be confirmed against the operator.

### The northern alternative via Ha Long

Another route reaches the island from Ha Long, using the Tuan Chau to Gia Luan ferry. Travel sources describe it as scenic and ferry-based, with a separate fare for a motorbike. It is longer from Hanoi, so it only makes sense if you are already combining the trip with Ha Long; see our guide on [riding from Hanoi to Ha Long Bay](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/hanoi-to-ha-long-bay-by-motorbike/).

## The real question: will the shop allow it?

A rental bike is not an unrestricted vehicle. Whether you may take it beyond the city, onto a ferry and onto an island is a decision for the owner, and it affects insurance, breakdown help and the return time. Before planning anything, ask Nguyen Ha directly, name Cat Ba, say how many days you want, and ask whether the bike will be accepted for a ferry crossing. Get the answer in writing if you can. Do not assume that a travel article implies permission. ## The cost logic: paying for days you may not ride

Even if the shop agrees, remember how the shop charges. One rental day is 24 hours, a shorter use is still charged as a full day, and early return does not refund unused rent. If you take a Hanoi bike to the island for two nights and leave it parked while you take boat tours, you still pay for those days. Late return is VND 30,000 per hour for one to five hours late, and an extra rental day from six hours. ## Renting on the island instead

Several sources say most travellers rent a motorbike on Cat Ba, not in Hanoi, and give day rental figures that vary by source, which suggests prices are negotiable and change over time; this guide does not quote them. The advantages:

- **No highway ride.** You skip the long, truck-heavy ride covered in the route safety guide.
- **No ferry with a bike.** You travel as a foot passenger or on a coach that includes the ferry.
- **You only pay for days on the island.**
- **You are not responsible for a Hanoi bike far from the shop.**

The disadvantage is that you rely on an island rental business with different terms. Check the same things as you would in Hanoi: helmet fit, brakes, tyres, written terms and the return process. Our [ten-minute inspection](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/rental-scooter-inspection-checklist/) applies anywhere. Island roads include climbs and hairpins, so only rent a bike you are comfortable controlling.

## Which option suits you?

| Your situation | Suggested choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Want to ride only on the island | Bus or coach to Cat Ba, rent there | Avoids highway and ferry-with-bike steps |
| Experienced rider who wants a Hanoi to Cat Ba ride | Ride your own bike, with the shop's agreement | Flexible, but a long day and a firm turnaround time |
| Only 1 to 2 days, no strong need for a bike | Bus plus walking and taxis | Least effort |
| Combining Ha Long and Cat Ba | Ferry via Tuan Chau | Ties both places together |
| Nervous rider | Do not ride the highway | Truck traffic and distance are the main risks |

## If you decide to ride

Treat it as a serious journey, not a casual outing:

- **Start early.** The ride, the ferry queue and the island roads fill a day.
- **Fill up in Hai Phong,** before the sea bridge; check [fuel guidance](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/fuel-in-hanoi-petrol-stations-rental-scooter/) for what to look for.
- **Carry the right documents** per the [documents guide](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/documents-to-carry-riding-in-vietnam/), and wear a properly fitted helmet.
- **Leave the island with time to spare** for the ferry queue and the long ride back.
- **Plan an overnight rather than a same-day return,** and avoid riding in the dark; see [riding at night in Hanoi](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/riding-at-night-in-hanoi/).
- **Know what to do if the bike fails,** using our guide to [breakdowns with a rental bike](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/rental-bike-breakdown-hanoi-what-to-do/).

## FAQ: Cat Ba with a rental motorbike

### Can I take a motorbike on the ferry to Cat Ba?

Travel guides say the Got or Dong Bai ferry carries motorbikes for an extra fee. Check the current arrangements at the terminal.

### How far is Cat Ba from Hanoi?

One travel operator gives about 156 km and four to five hours for a motorbike trip, including the ferry.

### Can I ride on the expressway to Hai Phong?

No. Motorcycles and mopeds are not allowed on expressways under Law 36/2024/QH15. Use National Highway 5.

### Will Nguyen Ha let me take the bike to Cat Ba?

Ask the shop, naming the destination and dates. This guide cannot promise permission.

### Is it easier to rent on Cat Ba?

For most visitors, yes. Hai Phong's official portal suggests hiring a motorbike on the island, and travel sources say many travellers do.

### Is Cat Ba part of the World Heritage property?

Yes, UNESCO's Ha Long Bay - Cat Ba Archipelago property includes the Cat Ba Archipelago, added through a 2023 extension.
