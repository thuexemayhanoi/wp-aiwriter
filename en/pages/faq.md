These answers describe how Nguyen Ha works and the rules that apply to every rental, so that you can plan before you contact us. They are the shop's terms as published in October 2026; the contract you sign for a specific bike is what finally applies, so confirm anything that matters to you before you pay.

## Booking and prices

### How much does it cost to rent a motorbike in Hanoi?

At Nguyen Ha, manual bikes and automatic scooters are VND 150,000–200,000 per day, 50cc bikes VND 200,000 per day, and electric models are quoted by model. Weekly and monthly packages are cheaper per day and are listed on the [price guide](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/prices/). The exact price of the bike you receive is confirmed before you sign.

### Can I rent for just a few hours?

Yes, but it is charged as one day. A rental day is 24 hours from the handover time, and a shorter rental, whether one hour or twelve, still counts as a day. We do not price ordinary rentals by the hour.

### Is the weekly or monthly price just the daily price multiplied?

No. Weekly and monthly packages are priced on their own for each kind of bike and are the better value for longer stays. Compare the package price directly rather than counting days.

### Can I choose a specific model?

You can ask for one, and we will tell you whether it is available for your dates. Availability changes daily, so the model is confirmed when you book rather than promised in advance. The [motorbikes page](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/motorbikes/) explains the groups we rent.

### How do I book?

Send your pickup and return dates, the kind of bike, how many bikes you need and where you are staying to 0334 699 969 by phone or Zalo, or to +84 334 699 969 on WhatsApp, between 09:00 and 21:00. We confirm the model, the price and the deposit, and you collect the bike at the shop in Long Bien.

## Licence and documents

### Do I need a licence to rent a motorbike in Vietnam?

Yes for anything above 50 cc. Under the road traffic law in force since 1 January 2025 (Law 36/2024/QH15), class A1 covers motorcycles up to 125 cc, which includes the Honda Vision, Wave and most scooters we rent, and class A covers larger engines. Foreign riders use their national licence together with an International Driving Permit issued under the 1968 Vienna Convention; a permit issued only under the 1949 convention is not recognised in Vietnam. Bikes under 50 cc may be ridden from the age of 16 without a motorcycle licence.

### Which documents do I bring?

Your passport or identity card and your driving licence with its International Driving Permit. We check them and photograph them for the contract.

### Do you keep my passport?

No. Once the cash deposit and the rent are paid, every original document goes straight back to you. We never hold documents in place of a deposit, and we never describe the service as deposit-free.

## Deposit

### How much is the deposit?

VND 2–5 million in cash, set by the bike and the length of the rental. It is paid together with the rent when you sign the contract.

### When do I get the deposit back?

At the end of the rental, under the terms of the contract, once the bike has been checked against the handover record and any late or fuel charge has been settled.

## Collection and delivery

### Where do I pick the bike up?

At the shop at Ngo 5 Nguyen Van Cu, Bo De, Long Bien, near Gia Lam bus station, open 09:00 to 21:00 every day. Directions are on the [contact page](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/contact/).

### Will you deliver the bike to my hotel?

Only for weekly and monthly rentals. Within 10 km of Hoan Kiem Lake the fee is VND 50,000 per trip, or VND 100,000 for delivery and collection together; beyond 10 km we agree a price in advance. Daily rentals are collected at the shop.

### Can you bring the bike to Noi Bai airport?

No. We do not deliver to or collect from Noi Bai airport.

## During the rental

### How much fuel is in the bike, and how much should I return it with?

Petrol bikes leave the shop with about 0.5 to 2 litres and should come back with about 0.5 to 1 litre. An empty tank at return is charged VND 20,000. For electric models, the battery level and the charging arrangement are agreed at handover.

### Is a helmet included?

Yes, one basic helmet per rider, free of charge. It is an everyday helmet rather than a premium one. Helmets are compulsory in Vietnam for the rider and the passenger on every road.

### Do you provide a raincoat or a lock?

A raincoat is not included in short rentals, although we may lend one on a monthly rental. We do not supply a separate anti-theft lock: lock the steering and park in guarded lots or with an attendant, who gives you a numbered ticket for collecting the bike.

### Can I take the bike outside Hanoi?

Tell us your plan when you book so that we can suggest a suitable bike and confirm the return time. Motorbikes are not allowed on Vietnam's expressways, so trips to places such as Ninh Binh use the national roads.

### What if the bike breaks down or gets a puncture?

Call the shop first and describe where you are and what happened. Small repairs such as a puncture are available at roadside mechanics all over Hanoi and along the main roads; ask us how the cost is handled before you agree to any repair, and keep the receipt. Settle the exact procedure for breakdowns and damage when you sign, so that both sides know it in advance.

## Returning the bike

### What happens if I return the bike late?

A return 1 to 5 hours late costs VND 30,000 per hour. From 6 hours late, one more rental day is charged at the daily rate of that bike. Telling us in advance is appreciated and helps us plan, but it does not waive the charge.

### Do I get money back if I return early?

No. Rent paid for the unused part of the period is not refunded, so choose a period that matches your real plans; a weekly package that can be extended is often better than a month paid up front.

### What is checked at the return?

The bike is compared with the handover record: condition, fuel and time. The deposit is then returned under the contract. The handover check described in the [rental guide](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/rental-guide/) is what makes the return quick.
