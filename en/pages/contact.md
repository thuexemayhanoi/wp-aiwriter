Get in touch before you travel to the shop so that we can confirm the bike you want, the price for your dates and the deposit. A short message with your plans is enough for us to settle the details before you come.

## Address and opening hours

- **Nguyen Ha Motorbike Rental**, Ngo 5 Nguyen Van Cu, Bo De ward, Long Bien district, Hanoi, Vietnam, near Gia Lam bus station.
- **Open 09:00 to 21:00, every day of the week.**
- **Phone and Zalo:** 0334 699 969
- **WhatsApp:** +84 334 699 969
- **Website:** rentbikehanoi.com

From the Old Quarter, the shop is across the Red River by the Chuong Duong bridge, about ten to twenty minutes by taxi or motorbike depending on the traffic. Please contact us within opening hours so that we can confirm the bike that is actually available and the conditions for your dates.

## What to tell us

The more of this you send, the faster we can confirm:

1. The date and time you want to pick the bike up, and when you plan to return it.
2. Whether you are renting for a day, a week or a month.
3. The kind of bike: manual, automatic scooter, 50cc or electric, and a model if you have a preference.
4. How many bikes you need.
5. Where you are staying, if you want delivery on a weekly or monthly rental, so that we can check the distance and the fee.

We reply with the model actually available for your dates, the documents to bring, the deposit and the total, so that nothing is decided at the counter.

## Picking up and delivery

Daily and short rentals are collected from and returned to the shop in Long Bien. Delivery and collection are possible for weekly and monthly rentals within about 10 km of Hoan Kiem Lake for a fee listed on the [price guide](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/prices/); further away we quote in advance. There is no delivery to or collection from Noi Bai airport.

## Questions to settle before you sign

Ask how the rental day is counted, what happens if you are late or return early, how a breakdown or a puncture is handled, what extra charges can arise and how the deposit is returned. The [FAQ](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/faq/) explains each of these, and the [rental guide](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/rental-guide/) describes the handover step by step. Whatever you read here, confirm the terms of your own contract before you pay.
