Nguyen Ha rents motorbikes in Hanoi by the day, the week and the month from a single shop at Ngo 5 Nguyen Van Cu in Bo De ward, Long Bien district, close to Gia Lam bus station. The shop runs on one principle: every condition of the rental, from the price and the deposit to the handover and the return, is explained before you decide, not discovered afterwards. This page sets out who we are, what we rent and the terms we work by, so that an English-speaking rider can read them in advance and arrive knowing exactly what to expect.

## Where we are and who we serve

Long Bien lies on the east bank of the Red River. From the shop, the Chuong Duong and Long Bien bridges lead straight into the Old Quarter and Hoan Kiem, which makes the location practical for visitors staying in the centre as well as for people living or working in Long Bien and Gia Lam. We serve three kinds of rider: travellers who want a scooter for a few days of sightseeing, residents and long-stay visitors who rent for weeks or months, and riders who need a particular kind of bike, such as a 50cc model or an electric scooter, for a particular reason. We have one location. Guides on this site about other districts of Hanoi describe those areas for riders; they do not mean we have branches there.

## What we rent

The fleet falls into four groups, and the right choice depends on how long you are renting, how far you plan to ride and what you are used to, not only on price.

- **Manual and semi-automatic bikes** such as the Honda Wave, Yamaha Sirius and Honda Blade: light, economical and easy to repair anywhere, for riders who already know how to handle a geared bike.
- **Automatic scooters** such as the Honda Vision, Air Blade, Click and Lead and the Yamaha Mio: twist-and-go, with storage under the seat, the easiest choice for city riding.
- **50cc bikes**, both the classic Cub style and small scooters, which are a separate class with their own licence conditions.
- **Electric bikes and scooters**, from electric bicycles to 50cc-equivalent electric scooters and some VinFast electric motorbikes, each with its own range, charging method and licence situation.

Which exact model you get is confirmed when you book, because availability changes from day to day. Our [motorbikes page](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/motorbikes/) compares the groups in more detail.

## How a rental is priced

Reference prices are published on the [price guide](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/prices/) so that you can budget before you contact us. Manual bikes and automatic scooters start at VND 150,000 per day, 50cc bikes are VND 200,000 per day, and electric models are quoted by model. Weekly and monthly packages are set separately and are the better deal for longer stays. The final figure for the actual bike is confirmed before the contract is signed, which keeps the advertised range and the amount you pay from drifting apart.

A rental day is 24 hours from the handover time in the contract. If you take a bike at 10:00 and bring it back at 10:00 the next morning you have used one day; if you bring it back the same afternoon you have still used one day. We do not price ordinary rentals by the hour, so a short errand is best budgeted as a full day from the start.

## Deposit and documents

Every rental carries a cash deposit of VND 2–5 million, fixed by the bike and the contract and paid together with the rent when you sign. We check your passport or identity card and your driving licence, photograph them for the contract, and hand them straight back: no original document is kept once the deposit and the rent are paid. The cash deposit is what secures the bike, which is why we never describe the service as deposit-free.

## Delivery, returns and extras

Delivery and collection exist for weekly and monthly rentals only. Within 10 km of Hoan Kiem Lake the charge is VND 50,000 per trip, or VND 100,000 for both delivery and collection; further out we agree a price in advance, and we do not deliver to or collect from Noi Bai airport. Daily and other short rentals are collected and returned at the shop.

Returns are compared with the handover time. A return 1 to 5 hours late costs VND 30,000 per hour; from 6 hours late we charge one more rental day at the daily rate of that bike. Telling us in advance helps us plan, but it does not waive the charge. Bringing a bike back before the end of the agreed period does not earn a refund of the unused rent, so renters on weekly or monthly packages should choose the period that matches their real plans.

Petrol bikes leave the shop with roughly half a litre to two litres in the tank and should come back with about half a litre to a litre; an empty tank at return costs VND 20,000. One basic helmet per rider is included free of charge. A raincoat is not part of a short rental, although we may lend one on a monthly rental, and we do not supply a separate anti-theft lock, so lock the steering and park in guarded places.

## How we work with you

Renting from Nguyen Ha follows five short steps. You tell us what you need: the kind of bike, the pickup and return times, and whether you want a day, a week or a month. We confirm the model actually available, the price, the deposit and, for weekly or monthly rentals, the delivery terms. You inspect the bike with us. You sign the contract and pay the rent and the deposit. You take the bike, noting the handover time so that the return is on time. The [rental guide](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/rental-guide/) expands each step, and the [FAQ](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/faq/) collects the questions we are asked most.
