No single bike suits every rider or every trip in Hanoi. A first-time visitor sightseeing for three days, a resident commuting for a month and a rider heading out to Ninh Binh for the weekend need different machines. Nguyen Ha rents four groups of bike, and this page describes each one: the models you can ask for, who it suits, the licence it needs and its reference price. The exact bike is confirmed when you book, because availability changes from day to day.

## Manual and semi-automatic bikes

**Models to ask for:** Honda Wave, Yamaha Sirius, Honda Blade.

These are the workhorses of Vietnam: light, frugal, simple to repair at any roadside mechanic and comfortable on rougher roads outside the city. The semi-automatic gearbox has no clutch lever, but you still change gears with your foot, so the group suits riders who already ride geared bikes at home. For an experienced rider spending weeks in Hanoi or planning day trips on the national roads, a Wave or Sirius is the economical choice.

Reference price: VND 150,000–200,000 per day, VND 700,000–800,000 per week, VND 1,000,000–1,200,000 per month. Licence: class A1 or an equivalent foreign licence with an International Driving Permit, since these bikes are around 110 cc.

## Automatic scooters

**Models to ask for:** Honda Vision, Honda Air Blade, Honda Click, Honda Lead, Yamaha Mio and Mio Ultimo.

Twist-and-go scooters are the easiest bikes to ride in Hanoi traffic: no gears, one brake lever on each side, a flat floor and storage under the seat for a helmet or a bag. The Vision is the lightest and lowest, which shorter and first-time riders appreciate; the Air Blade and Lead are larger, with more storage and a steadier feel at speed, and suit two people or a rider carrying luggage. Most visitors who have ridden a scooter anywhere before can ride any of these within minutes.

Reference price: VND 150,000–200,000 per day, VND 600,000–1,000,000 per week, VND 1,200,000–2,000,000 per month. The range is wide because the models differ in size and the bikes in age and condition. Licence: class A1 or equivalent for the 110–125 cc models; an Air Blade with the larger 160 cc engine needs class A or a foreign licence that covers motorcycles above 125 cc.

## 50cc bikes

**Models to ask for:** Cub 50cc and 50cc scooters.

Fifty-cubic-centimetre bikes are a class of their own. Under Vietnam's road traffic law in force since 1 January 2025, a bike under 50 cc may be ridden from the age of 16 without a motorcycle licence, which is why students and some visitors without a licence choose them. They are slower and less powerful than a 110 cc bike, better suited to short trips inside the city than to long rides, and the rental conditions differ somewhat from ordinary motorbikes, especially for younger renters. Ask us about them when you enquire.

Reference price: VND 200,000 per day, VND 1,000,000 per week, VND 2,000,000 per month.

## Electric bikes and scooters

**Models to ask for:** electric bicycles, 50cc-equivalent electric scooters and some VinFast electric motorbikes.

Electric models vary more than any other group. An electric bicycle, a small electric scooter and a VinFast electric motorbike differ in speed, range, weight, how and where they are charged, and whether a licence is required, so each one is explained individually when you book. They are quiet, cheap to run and pleasant for short rides in the city; for a day trip or a long commute, check the range and the charging arrangement first.

Reference price: the daily rate is quoted by model; weekly rentals are VND 600,000–800,000 and monthly rentals VND 1,500,000–1,800,000.

## Which group should you choose?

- **First time on two wheels in Asia, city sightseeing:** an automatic scooter, and the Honda Vision if you are not tall.
- **Two people, or luggage, or trips out of town:** a larger automatic such as the Air Blade or Lead, or a manual bike if you ride one at home.
- **A month or more of commuting:** a Wave or Sirius for the lowest running cost, or a Vision for easy parking.
- **No motorcycle licence:** a 50cc bike or an electric model that does not need one, within the limits of your age and the law.
- **Quiet and cheap for short hops:** an electric scooter.
