These are the reference prices Nguyen Ha uses for motorbike rental in Hanoi, split by the kind of bike and by day, week and month so that you can compare packages before you book. The price of the specific bike you receive depends on the model, its condition and the length of the rental, and it is confirmed before the contract is signed. The table was taken from the Vietnamese price page in October 2026 and is kept in step with it.

Three things to know before you compare: a rental day is 24 hours and a shorter rental still counts as one day; the deposit is a separate cash amount on top of the rent; and short rentals are collected at the shop, while delivery for weekly and monthly rentals is a paid extra described below.

## What the ranges mean

Manual bikes sit in a narrow band because the models are similar in size and cost. Automatic scooters cover a wider band because a Honda Vision and a Honda Lead or Air Blade are different bikes, and because the condition and age of each one differs. 50cc bikes have a single rate. Electric models are quoted individually, since an electric bicycle, a 50cc-equivalent electric scooter and a VinFast electric motorbike are not the same product.

Weekly and monthly rates are their own packages, not the daily rate multiplied by seven or thirty. If you are staying a week or longer, compare the package price directly rather than counting days.

## How a rental day is counted

A day runs for 24 hours from the handover time in the contract. Take a scooter at 10:00 and it is due back at 10:00 the next day unless the contract says otherwise. A rental of one hour, six hours or twelve hours is still one day; we do not price ordinary rentals by the hour.

## Deposit

The cash deposit is VND 2–5 million, depending on the bike and the length of the rental, and it is paid with the rent when the contract is signed. We look at and photograph your identity document and driving licence for the contract and return the originals immediately; keeping documents is never used as a substitute for the deposit. The deposit is returned at the end of the rental according to the contract.

## Delivery and collection

Delivery is offered for weekly and monthly rentals only. Within 10 km of Hoan Kiem Lake the charge is VND 50,000 per trip, so VND 100,000 if we both deliver the bike and collect it. Beyond 10 km the price is agreed before the trip. We do not deliver to or collect from Noi Bai airport. Send the exact address when you book so that we can confirm the distance.

## Late and early returns

A return 1 to 5 hours after the agreed time costs VND 30,000 per hour. From 6 hours late, one more rental day is charged at the daily rate of that bike. Letting us know you are running late helps with planning but does not remove the charge. If you return the bike before the end of the period you paid for, the unused rent is not refunded; this is written into the contract so that both sides know the bike's schedule from the start.

## Fuel, helmet and accessories

Petrol bikes are handed over with about 0.5 to 2 litres in the tank, depending on the bike, and should come back with about 0.5 to 1 litre. A bike returned with an empty tank is charged VND 20,000. For electric models, the battery level and the charging arrangement are confirmed at handover.

A basic helmet is included free for each rider; it is an everyday helmet, not a premium one. Raincoats are not part of short rentals, though we may provide one on a monthly rental. We do not supply a separate anti-theft lock, so lock the steering and use guarded parking.

## When the final price is fixed

The website gives you the range so that you can plan. The exact price is confirmed on the actual bike, its condition and your dates before you sign, so there is no gap between what was advertised and what you pay.
