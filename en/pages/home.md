Nguyen Ha is a family-run motorbike rental shop at Ngo 5 Nguyen Van Cu in Bo De, Long Bien, a few minutes from Gia Lam bus station and one bridge away from the Old Quarter. We rent manual motorbikes, automatic scooters, 50cc models and electric bikes by the day, the week and the month, to visitors passing through Hanoi and to residents who need a bike for a season. The English side of this site explains how renting works here, what it costs, and what to expect on Hanoi's roads, written for international riders rather than translated from the Vietnamese pages.

## Renting from Nguyen Ha in brief

- **Four kinds of bike.** Semi-automatic Hondas and Yamahas for experienced riders, automatic scooters such as the Honda Vision and Air Blade for everyday city use, 50cc bikes, and electric models. The [motorbikes page](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/motorbikes/) describes each group and who it suits.
- **Clear reference prices.** Automatic scooters and manual bikes start at VND 150,000 per day; weekly and monthly packages are priced on their own and work out cheaper than multiplying the daily rate. The [price guide](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/prices/) has the full table, the deposit and every extra charge.
- **A rental day is 24 hours.** It starts at the handover time written in the contract. A shorter rental still counts as one day; there is no hourly rate.
- **A cash deposit instead of your passport.** The deposit is VND 2–5 million depending on the bike. We check and photograph your identity document and licence for the contract, and keep no originals once the deposit and the rent are paid.
- **One shop, open every day.** Pick up and return at the shop between 09:00 and 21:00. Delivery is available for weekly and monthly rentals within 10 km of Hoan Kiem Lake, never at Noi Bai airport.

## Before you ride

Hanoi traffic is dense but slow, and most visitors settle into it within a day. What matters more than confidence is paperwork and preparation: a licence that covers the engine size you rent, an International Driving Permit that Vietnam recognises, a helmet on every ride, and a handover check of the brakes, lights, horn and tyres before you leave the shop. The [rental guide](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/rental-guide/) walks through the whole process from the first message to the return, and the [FAQ](https://blog.rentbikehanoi.com/en/faq/) answers the questions riders ask us most often, from deposits and late returns to fuel and parking.
